2 / 7 · SCENARIO: RUBIN QSA DECODE & ROCM MLA HEAD-PADDING GATE Deep Dive

Qwen 3.8 QSA Cuts 32K Decode Traffic 32 MiB → 4 MiB (8.0x) While ROCm MLA Avoids 2x Head Padding

Input Scenario & Hardware Contract

Case A (FlashInfer #7136 + #7027): Rubin SM107 Qwen 3.8 Flash-Next decode at seq_len=32,768, d_head=256, 4-token compressed indexer key (128-dim BF16) + top_k_blocks=512; plus Rubin 2-CTA MoE with tile_size=256, cluster_shape_mn=(2,2).
Case B (vLLM #61863): ROCm AITER MLA prefill for Kimi-K2.x at TP8 (8 heads/rank) vs Kimi-K3 at TP8 (12 heads/rank) with 16-head assembly kernel alignment requirement.

Decision 1 · Qwen 3.8 QSA 4-Token Compressed Indexer + Top-512 Block Read Act automatically · 8.0x BW Speedup

Scans 8,192 compressed keys (2,097,152 B) + 2,048 sparse K/V tokens (2,097,152 B) = 4,194,304 B vs 33,554,432 B dense K/V.

Decision 2 · ROCm AITER MLA FP8 Prefill Gate (is_valid_fp8_prefill_num_heads) choice · Reject 1..8 Heads (1.40x Faster)

At 8 heads/rank, replicate-padding to 16 heads doubles work (cost 11.2 vs BF16 8.0); gate keeps BF16 prefill (1.40x gain) while allowing 12 heads/rank (cost 11.2 < 12.0).

Decision 3 · Rubin SM107 2-CTA Tile-256 Cluster (2, 2) MoE Tactic score · 2.0x A/SFA Multicast · 0 Pad Tiles

With mma_tiler_m=256 and atom_cta_m=2, both CTAs along M map to the same routing tile (0 extra metadata padding) while Cluster-N multicasts A/SFA across 2 CTAs.

Key Takeaway: Hardware alignment padding (`8 → 16` heads) silently erases FP8 compute gains when the pad factor reaches `2.0x`; explicit head-count gating restores a `1.40x` latency advantage on TP8 Kimi-K2.x prefill.

7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS Architect Verdict

Production Readiness & Architectural Boundaries for Phase 107

✅ Good Fit (Adopt Now)

  • Rubin SM107 2-CTA Tile-256 MoE: Use cluster_shape_mn=(2,2) with tile_size=256 and mma_inst_m=256 for 2.0x A/SFA multicast with zero routing-table padding.
  • ROCm MLA TP8 Prefill Routing: Keep BF16 prefill when per-rank heads ≤ 8 (e.g., Kimi-K2.x TP8) while enabling FP8 prefill at ≥ 9 heads (Kimi-K3 TP8 = 12 heads).
  • Silent-Stream SSE Keepalives: Enable 15.0s response.in_progress keepalives for long tool-call and reasoning streams behind 60s L7 proxies.
  • FP8 Checkpoint Path: Drop synchronous gc.collect() after checkpoint saves and use immortal heap singletons for CUDA/cuDNN handle caches.

⛔ Not For (Avoid / Guard)

  • Multi-Tile Rubin MoE Clusters: Do not pair cluster_shape_m=2 with 1-CTA mma_inst_m=128 tiles unless routing metadata is rounded and zero-padded past num_non_exiting_tiles.
  • Unconditional FP8 MLA Prefill on High TP: Do not force 16-head padded FP8 prefill when num_heads ≤ 8; replicate-padding overhead outweighs FP8 tensor core speedup.
  • Heavy Imports in Process-Stop Helpers: Never import full tensorrt_llm or initialize MPI inside a short-lived shutdown script.
