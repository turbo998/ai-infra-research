Why `MIG` Protects Multi-Tenant SLAs While `MPS` Accelerates Same-Tenant Pipelines

`Time-Slicing` offers neither memory limits nor bandwidth isolation. `CUDA MPS` fuses kernels efficiently but shares a single fault domain. `NVIDIA MIG` slices the GPU in silicon (separate HBM controllers, L2 cache, and SMs)!
