4 / 7 · UNDER THE HOOD

Symmetric `2P` ZigZag Slicing for Input, Pos & Labels

def zigzag_shard_sequence(tensor: torch.Tensor, cp_rank: int, cp_size: int): # Split sequence dimension into 2 * P contiguous blocks: chunks = torch.chunk(tensor, 2 * cp_size, dim=1) # Pair lightest head chunk (r) with heaviest tail chunk (2P - 1 - r): return torch.cat([chunks[cp_rank], chunks[2 * cp_size - 1 - cp_rank]], dim=1) ↓ # Apply identically to input_ids, position_ids (RoPE), and labels! local_ids = zigzag_shard_sequence(input_ids, cp_rank, cp_size) local_pos = zigzag_shard_sequence(position_ids, cp_rank, cp_size)
Every rank computes (r + 2P - 1 - r) = (2P - 1) units of causal attention work at every step.
