7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECTURAL GUIDANCE & READINESS

When to Enable Device-Planned Split-KV MLA, Half-Wave Tails & Draft-Free Capture

Practical deployment boundaries for solution architects designing Blackwell (`sm_100a`/`sm_103a`/`sm_120a`) inference and training clusters.

✅ Good Fit
  • Agentic Kimi-K3 Serving on SM100/SM103/SM107: Opt into `--attention-backend KIMI_K3_KV_BALANCED_MLA` when context lengths vary widely (`1K–128K`) and CUDA Graph capture is required (`3.91x` cycle reduction).
  • Sequence-Parallel MiniMax-H3 Diffusion (`P=4, 8`): Enable the N-split half-width tail schedule on GB200/GB300 when partial last waves fall at or below 38 tile pairs (`1.032x–1.057x` speedup).
  • Offline Distillation / Feature Collection in SGLang: Pass `--enable-return-aux-hidden-states` with `--speculative-algorithm EAGLE3|DFLASH|DSPARK` (`pp_size == 1`) to save `3.9–5.5 GiB` of dormant draft VRAM.
  • Disaggregated vLLM Sidecars in Dynamo: Keep `output_text = Some(false)` and decode in the Rust frontend (`-71.43%` wire bytes).
⛔ Not For
  • Small MIG Slices Running Kimi-K3 Arm M (`129..2048` tokens): Devices with `< 43 SMs` on CC 10.0 or `< 32 SMs` on CC 10.3 cannot hold 128 co-resident owner CTAs; route via strided `Arm GW` instead.
  • Bitwise-Strict Audits Using Fused Atomic MoE Epilogues: On SM120, fused BF16 MoE combine uses atomic additions whose arrival order varies; select preplanned non-fused combine (`#7405`) for bitwise repeatability.
  • Stochastic Rounding (`cvt.rs`) to `E8M0` Scales in TileLang: `f32 -> E8M0` rounds toward $+\infty$ and takes no `rbits`; do not attach `round="rs"`.
  • Multi-Batch (`PP > 1`) Draft-Free Aux Capture: `--enable-return-aux-hidden-states` currently requires `pp_size == 1`.
