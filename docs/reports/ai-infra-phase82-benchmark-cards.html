7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS ARCHITECT DECISION GUIDE

Architect's Verdict: Deploy Unthrottled `K256 FC2` & Dual Epilogue Staging Within Exact Token/SMEM Envelopes

✅ Good Fit

  • High-Expert NVFP4 MoE Decode (`E=512, K=10`) at Medium Batch (`T=7..32`): 1-D linear-grid `K256 FC2` (`179,456 B` SMEM) yields up to `1.05x` cold-L2 speedup on GB200/GB300 with bit-identical BF16 outputs.
  • Rubin (`sm_107a`) MLA NVFP4 Paged Decode & Mamba2 Decode: Double-buffered `4 KB` correction-warp staging tiles (`332,800 B` SMEM) cut small-batch latency up to `1.047x`, while `cake_selective_state_update` speeds up all `122` Mamba2 shapes (`1.015x–1.253x`).
  • Multimodal & Disaggregated KV Serving: SGLang per-RPC `zmq.IDENTITY` SHM reclaim (`#46154`), decoder-only `channels_last_3d` (`#46173`), vLLM preempted chunk cursor reset (`#63010`), and Mooncake $O(1)$ `getBatchTransferStatus` (`#4910`).

⛔ Not For

  • Ultra-Tiny MoE Decode Batch (`T=1..6` on `sm_103a`, `T=1..8` on `sm_100a`): 8-token route tiles in `K256 FC2` are under-filled at `T=1` (`1.015–1.021x` slower), so the manifest keeps existing kernels for those rows.
  • Applying `channels_last_3d` to Video VAE Encoders in I2V Pipelines: Alters cuDNN 3D conv rounding and breaks deterministic I2V conditioning seeds; keep `vae.encoder` contiguous.
  • Expanding FA4 SM100 `hdim=256` 2CTA Backward `tile_n` Beyond `64` in TMEM: `dV(128)+dK(128)+S/P(64)+dP/dS(64)+dQ(128)` already occupies all `512` TMEM columns per CTA.
