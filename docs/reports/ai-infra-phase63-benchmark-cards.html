✅ Good Fit (Immediate Production Adoption)

  • GB200 (`sm_100a`) Long-Context DSA Training & Prefill: `#7125` far-sample first threshold delivers 1.29x–1.78x speedups on 64k–262k attention-sink sequences while trimming `12,160 B` SMEM.
  • GB300 (`sm_103a`) Dense Projection & FP32 MoE Routers: `#7108` adds `80` native `sm_103a` kernels and `nn_t=6` router backward, beating cuBLAS BF16/BF16x9 by up to 3.55x (`dgrad`) and 2.17x (`router fwd`).
  • DeepSeek-V3 / Kimi K2.5 `torch.compile` Training & Prefill: TorchTitan `#5529` in-row `int32` pair rotation and `#5460` unmasked SwiGLU reach 95.9%–99.6% of the GB300 HBM bandwidth floor, allowing removal of custom Helion/Triton overrides.
  • Multi-Tenant MoE LoRA, SpecDec & Multimodal Serving: SGLang `#45681/#45916`, TRT-LLM `#20119/#19989`, and vLLM `#62757/#63120` eliminate silent cache/slot corruption and save 2.44 GiB of redundant FP32 RoPE tables.

⛔ Not For / Watch Outs (Boundary Conditions)

  • GB300 (`sm_103a`) Ascending DSA Kernels Expecting Speedup from `#7125`: `sm_103a` already scans tiles in ascending order (seeing row-head sinks first), so `#7125` leaves `sm_103a` performance unchanged (`1.00x–1.01x`).
  • Single-Document 262k KDA Sequences Without Document Boundaries: `kda_train_arena(batch_tokens=B)` (`#7102`) partitions only at `cu_seqlens` document boundaries (`ValueError` if any single document exceeds `batch_tokens`); use `lean=True, tail_passes=4` (`1.59x` arena cut) for monolithic single-doc rows.
  • Non-Contiguous or FP32 $q$ Tensors in TorchTitan `mla_qk`: `_RotateTail` (`#5529`) activates only for contiguous BF16 $q$ under `type(self.rope) is ComplexRoPE`; non-contiguous or FP32 tensors stay on the split $\to$ rotate $\to$ cat path.
  • Blind RDMA Auto-Recovery on Fatal HCA Errors: Never auto-reset endpoints on `IBV_EVENT_DEVICE_FATAL` or `IBV_EVENT_CQ_ERR` (`#4715`); restrict 1 Hz recovery strictly to `IBV_EVENT_PORT_ERR`.
Kimi K3 (`1T` MoE) Deployment Sizing (`dd63b36fcf`) On 2x 8-GPU B200 (`180 GB/GPU`), avoid `DEP16` (`~210 GB/rank` due to `114 GB` replicated BF16 attention); use **`TEP16`** (`~115 GB/rank`) to retain **~75 GB/rank** for KV cache and CUDA graphs.
