⛔ Not For (Known Boundaries & Exclusions)

  • TE FusedAttention with DelayedScaling under fullgraph=True: Python-side historical amax buffer mutation in DelayedScaling remains outside the graph-break-free custom op contract.
  • Air-Gapped SM107 TRT-LLM Without Cubin Pre-Warming: 128-token FMHA cubins are fetched on demand unless pre-staged via FLASHINFER_CUBIN_DIR + FLASHINFER_NO_DOWNLOAD=1.
