7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECTURAL GUIDANCE & READINESS

Deploy Long-Context Cake FP8, 8-Thread KVCMv2 Host Fill & GPU-Staged DistMuon Now; Gate Short-Context FP8 & Lossy MXFP8

✅ Good Fit (Deploy in Production / Staging)

• Long-Context SM90 Non-Causal MHA ($S \ge 32\text{K}, D=128$): Cake INT8 QK + FP8 E4M3 PV delivers `1.08x–1.16x` end-to-end speedup including on-GPU K-mean smoothing and quantization.

• Multi-Tens-of-GiB Host KV Cache Pools: TensorRT-LLM KVCMv2's `1 + 7` thread parallel `4 MiB` population cuts 64 GiB cold-start time from `15.24 s` to `2.38 s` (`6.40x`).

• FSDP2 CPU-Offloaded Muon Training: Setting `DistMuon(ns_device="accelerator")` stages only transient 2D compute views to GPU (`15s → 1s` step) while keeping parameters and momentum in host DRAM.

• Speculative Verify on AMD `gfx95`: `SGLANG_ROCM_OPT_DSPARK_FP8_LM_HEAD=1` halves `lm_head` weight bandwidth (`1.85x` at `M=64`).

⛔ Not For (Avoid or Gate Explicitly)

• Short-Sequence SM90 Prefill ($S \le 4,800$): The 4-kernel Cake INT8/FP8 chain regresses to `0.86x` vs BF16 FlashAttention-3 (`0.7530 ms` vs `0.6484 ms`).

• Unconditional FP32 Block-FP8 → MXFP8 on Ascend `arch35`: Converting arbitrary FP32 `128x128` block scales to `1x32` UE8M0 power-of-two scales is lossy; keep gated off by default.

• Multi-Node Prefill with Atlas A5 MXFP8 Normal Dispatch: A5 MXFP8 normal dispatch is intranode-only; keep normal dispatch in `bf16` and use `mxfp8` only on low-latency decode dispatch.

• Graph-Mode Provider Switching after FA2 Capture: Keep FA2 pinned per wrapper once planned so its CPU merge schedule stays refreshed.
