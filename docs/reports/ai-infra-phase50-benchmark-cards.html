7 / 7 · MY TAKEREADINESS & ARCHITECTURAL FIT

Production Adoption Guidance for Phase 50 Upstream Mechanisms

✅ Good fit

  • Blackwell Per-Token Sparse/Sliding Attention: Use TRTLLM-Gen `VariableWindow` FMHA (`+3.69%–4.17%` prefill cost, `0%` non-binding decode penalty).
  • Hybrid Mamba / KDA Serving: Adopt SGLang's host-tracked `mamba_ping_pong_empty`, `searchsorted` multimodal mask, and `FlashInferKDAPrefillGraph`.
  • Kimi K2.x Training & RL: Ensure decoder-layer `rms_norm_eps=1e-5` while keeping MLA q/kv norms at `1e-6`.

⛔ Not for

  • Non-Causal Sliding Window on Paged Prefill: Rejected in `BatchPrefillWithPagedKVCacheWrapper.plan()`; use `window_right` on TRTLLM-Gen instead.
  • Generic `head_dim_v` THD Learnable Softmax on cuDNN < 9.26: Only specialized `head_dim_v in {64, 128, 256}` are safe before cuDNN 9.26.0.
  • Hopper/Ada KDA FROST Kernels: `KimiDeltaAttention` cuDNN FROST requires SM100/SM103/SM107.
