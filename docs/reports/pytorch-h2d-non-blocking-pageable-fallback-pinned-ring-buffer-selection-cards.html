7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Architect Verdict: Never Call non_blocking=True on Pageable Tensors or .pin_memory() in Hot Loops

✅ Good fit
• vLLM / SGLang model runner step-input preparation (`input_ids`, `positions`, `block_tables`)
• CPU-to-GPU activation and KV-cache offloading engines
• High-throughput DataLoader H2D prefetchers
⛔ Not for
• Calling .to("cuda", non_blocking=True) on unpinned tensors
• Calling tensor.pin_memory() dynamically inside each step
• Overwriting a single pinned buffer without a `cudaEvent` guard
