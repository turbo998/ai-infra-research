3 Production Invariants: PDL Stream Serialization (`<=1.13×`), Single-Cycle `0x64006400` FP16x2 Unpack, and Subnormal/NaN E8M0 Reciprocals

Inspecting exact kernel dispatch and bit-level invariants across FlashInfer (`#7131`, `#7133`), vLLM (`#59717`, `#60644`), SGLang (`#45335`), and TransformerEngine (`#3459`).

Input Workload: (1) GB300 (`152` SMs) `cake_rmsnorm_train` backward with 1-thread side-stream kernel; (2) A100 (`108` SMs) `cake_qsa_nvfp4` `rows=128, selected_tokens=2051`; (3) TE MXFP8 `exp2f_rcp_2x(254/255)` & SGLang Inkling BF16 LM-head `[1+1/1024, 1+1/512]`.
Decision 1 · `cake_rmsnorm_train` PDL Pair & `cake_qsa_nvfp4` `select_wide` choice Act automatically · 92.3% (182.91 / 198.50 us)
Replaces `cudaLaunchAttributeCooperative` with chunk-claiming main kernel + `cudaLaunchAttributeProgrammaticStreamSerialization` (`griddepcontrol.wait`) reduce kernel (GB300 `4096×512`: `16.0 -> 13.7 us`, side-stream stall `280× -> 1.13×`). On `sm_80`, `select_wide` (`512 > 216` CTAs, `17 >= 16` tiles/CTA) selects `decode_whole_tile` (`198.50 -> 182.91 us`, `1.085×`).
Decision 2 · vLLM `moe_permute` Sentinel & SGLang Inkling FP32-Out score Act automatically · 100.0% Exact
Unconditionally remaps invalid `topk_ids` (`-1`) to `n_expert` even when `expert_map=None`, zeroes `mm1_out` when `not per_act_token` (preventing `51.2×` scale inflation), and uses `torch.mm(..., out_dtype=torch.float32)` in Inkling so `[1+1/1024, 1+1/512]` keeps `argmax == 1` instead of rounding both to `1.0` (`argmax == 0`).
Decision 3 · TE Register-Resident MXFP8 & TorchTitan `repin_load_ratio=1.5` noul Confirm · 86.4% Geomean vs TMA
TE `cast_bidim.cuh` reads each 32-row tile once into registers (`0.864×` geomean time vs TMA) and fixes `exp2f_rcp_2x` codes `254` (`0x00400040`, $2^{-127}$) and `255` (`0x7FFF7FFF`, NaN). TorchTitan re-pins sticky sessions when load $> 1.5 \times \text{mean}$ (`[6,2,2,2] -> idx 1`, `-8.0%` wall time).
Key Architectural Finding: Whole-device cooperative launches (`cudaLaunchAttributeCooperative`) are incompatible with multi-stream communication/telemetry overlap because a single resident side-stream warp blocks full-grid scheduling. Atomic chunk-claiming paired with PDL (`griddepcontrol.wait`) achieves lower single-stream latency while bounding concurrent slowdown to `<= 1.13×`.

7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & PRODUCTION READINESS Architect Guidance

When to Adopt PDL Reductions, Register-Resident MXFP8, `out_dtype=float32` LM-Heads & Sticky Re-Pinning

Actionable decision guide for Solutions Architects designing GPU training/inference clusters across Rubin (`sm_107a`), Blackwell (`sm_100a`/`sm_103a`), Ampere (`sm_80`), and ROCm (`gfx942`/`gfx1151`).

✅ Good Fit (Adopt Immediately)

  • Multi-Stream Training Overlapping Comm & Norm Backward: Replace `cudaLaunchAttributeCooperative` with atomic chunk-claiming + PDL (`cudaLaunchAttributeProgrammaticStreamSerialization`) to avoid `10–280×` side-stream stalls.
  • Blackwell (`sm_100+`) 32B-Aligned BF16->MXFP8 Cast-Only: Use TE register-resident `cast_rowwise.cuh` (packed or 512B GEMM-swizzled) and `cast_bidim.cuh` (packed) for `0.864×` geomean time vs TMA.
  • Large-Vocab BF16 Serving with Greedy/Top-1 Sensitivity: Enable `out_dtype=torch.float32` on the LM-head GEMM (`enable_lm_head_output_fp32=True`) to eliminate BF16 tie-rounding flips with zero extra weight memory.
  • Multi-Turn RL Rollouts with Straggler Risk: Set `repin_load_ratio=1.5` on `StickySessionRoutingStrategy` and check `is_timeout` before `ok` on Verifiers 0.4.0.
  • High-Frequency Kubernetes Readiness Probes on Dynamo: Point probes to `/health` (status-only) and reserve `/health?instances=true` for low-frequency topology checks.

⛔ Not For (Avoid / Fallback Required)

  • GEMM-Swizzled Bidimensional MXFP8 Casts: TE `cast_bidim.cuh` enforces a compile-time `static_assert(!WITH_GEMM_SWIZZLED_SCALES)` because colwise swizzling is transposed; route swizzled bidim tensors to the staged TMA kernel.
  • Pre-Blackwell (`sm < 100`) or Unaligned (`< 32B`) MXFP8 Pointers: 256-bit `ld.global.nc.v8.b32` requires `sm_100+` and 32B alignment; Hopper (`sm_90`) falls through to staged kernels.
  • Asymmetric EP Entry into `autotune_v2()`: Calling `rubin_megamoe(..., knobs="auto")` with `autotune_v2()` on only a subset of EP ranks fails closed with `RuntimeError`.
  • Narrow `sm_80` QSA Decode (`ceil(tiles/splits) < 16`): Do not force `decode_whole_tile` on `rows=64` (`9` tiles/CTA), where K-first `decode` remains faster.
  • Treating `io_uring` `cqe->res >= 0` as Full Transfer: Short reads/writes (`cqe->res < request.length`) must be rejected as `FAILED` to prevent silent tail corruption.
