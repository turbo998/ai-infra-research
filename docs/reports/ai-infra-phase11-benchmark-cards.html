Why all_reduce(diff, norm) Is Mandatory

Under Ulysses/Ring Sequence Parallelism, each GPU holds $1/P$ of the spatial tokens. Without reducing `diff` and `norm` across `get_sp_group()`, ranks disagree on `use_cache` and hang on the first `All-to-All` inside Attention.

Why 80 Layers Collapse to 2 Artifacts

Splitting dynamic Attention via `PiecewiseBackend` leaves 79 middle blocks with identical FX graphs. Content-addressed SHA256 hashing in `StandaloneCompiledArtifacts` compiles only the first layer and final norm block.

7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECT DECISION GUIDE & READINESS

Architectural Verdict: Where Dynamic Chunking, xDiT + TeaCache, and TorchInductor Excel — and Where They Break

Actionable production deployment rules for cloud and AI infrastructure solution architects.

✅ Good Fit (Deploy Now)

  • Dynamic Token-Budget Chunked Prefill (`C* = 192..768`) on colocated P+D replicas when `D ≤ 64` and `TPOT_SLA ≥ 25 ms`: harvests idle Tensor Core cycles during memory-bound decode to boost SLA-compliant Goodput by 4.49x.
  • xDiT (CFG=2 × Ulysses × PipeFusion) + TeaCache (t=0.08..0.12) for 720p/1080p video DiT generation (`Wan2.1`, `HunyuanVideo`, `Flux`): cuts exposed communication from `11.18%` to 0.98% and achieves 3.79x–4.65x speedup with ≤ 0.31 dB PSNR loss.
  • CI/CD Pre-Warmed FxGraphCache / MEGA_AOT + TorchCompileWithNoGuardsWrapper: shrinks 8-GPU LLM pod cold start from `29.44 min` to 11.8–18.0 s while fusing `RMSNorm + RoPE + FP8 Quant + SwiGLU` for +35.73% throughput.

⛔ Not For (Architectural Anti-Patterns)

  • Static `chunk_size=4096/8192` on Latency-Critical Online Serving: A single `8192` prefill chunk inflates step time to 255.68 ms, violating a `25 ms` TPOT SLA by `10.23x`.
  • Colocated Chunked Prefill when Pure Decode Already Exhausts SLA (`D ≥ 96, 16K+ KV` with `≤ 15 ms` SLA): Headroom drops to zero (`C* = 0`); you must use physical P/D Disaggregation (Mooncake/NIXL) instead.
  • Pure `TP=8` on Narrow-Hidden Long-Sequence Video DiTs (`H ≤ 5120, N ≥ 128K`) or `TeaCache` with `t ≥ 0.20` / without USP `all_reduce`: causes `784.6 ms/step` AllReduce stalls, `-1.18 dB` motion artifacts, or NCCL deadlocks.
  • Uncached Per-Rank JIT Compilation in Autoscaling K8s Pods: Causes `29+ min` startup timeouts and runtime recompilation stalls.
