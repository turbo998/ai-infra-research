Root-Cause Failure Analysis on ✕ Misses

2) Ring-N1024-4MiB suffers 1,023 sequential hop barriers and 256B NIC packets.

Key Robustness Takeaway

1) PDL (`enable_pdl=True`) provides the largest relative latency reduction at smaller batch sizes (-34.18% at B=16 vs -17.83% at B=128) where kernel launch serialization is a higher fraction of step time.

7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECTURAL GUIDANCE & PRODUCTION READINESS

When to Deploy Multi-Level Cascade Attention, NCCL PAT, and Aux-Loss-Free MoE Routing — and Where Their Boundaries Lie

✅ Good fit
  • Multi-Turn Agent & RAG Serving (Shared Prefix ≥ 1K tokens, Group ≥ 4): Use FlashInfer MultiLevelCascadeAttentionWrapper with causal=False on upper tree levels and enable_pdl=True on SM90/SM100 to cut HBM KV traffic by 83.9%–95.2%.
  • Large-Scale FSDP2 / ZeRO-3 (`N ≥ 256` GPUs, Shards `1–64 MiB`): Enable NCCL_ALGO_PAT (Multi-RPN with intra-node NVLS) to replace $O(N)$ Ring latency with $O(\log_2(N/8))$ binomial hops and `aggFactor` packet coalescing (`6.25x–19.21x` faster at 1,024 GPUs).
  • Fine-Grained MoE Training (`32–256` Experts): Combine FP32 expert_bias sign updates (or qb_dual_update) with CF = 1.25–1.50 and drop_policy="probs" to bound memory without auxiliary-loss gradient interference.
⛔ Not for
  • Zero-Overlap Unique Prompts or Tiny Prefixes (`L_shared < 256`): Splitting into multi-level Cascade kernels when requests share no prefix adds extra HBM LSE/output state read-writes and kernel launch overhead.
  • Small-Cluster Huge Bucket Collectives (`N ≤ 64` GPUs, `M ≥ 256 MiB`): Standard Ring or direct NVLS Tree already saturates NIC channels at `63` steps; Multi-RPN PAT's extra NVLS staging slot yields `0.99x` vs Ring at `N=64, 256 MiB`.
  • Batch-Invariant Deterministic MoE Inference: Never mutate expert_bias online or enable drop_and_pad=True during deterministic compliance/RL evaluation (moe_utils.py:424 requires frozen_expert_bias=True and dropless routing).
