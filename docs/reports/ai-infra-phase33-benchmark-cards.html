7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS PRODUCTION GUIDANCE

Architect's Decision Guide: Where Phase 33 Optimizations Deliver Immediate ROI vs Where They Must Be Gated Off

✅ Good Fit (适用场景)

  • Hybrid GDN/SSM Serving with Prefix Caching: Use gdn_prefix_materialize to snapshot exact 128-token boundary states from u-cache checkpoints + partial ring folds at $2.5\,\mu\text{s}$ idle grid cost.
  • Blackwell SM100a/SM103a Large-EP MoE & Uniform Decode: Attach 0x12F0000000000000ULL (`evict_first`) to one-use streaming FC2 expert weights and uniform split-KV tiles so routed activations stay L2-resident.
  • Hybrid Full/Shared DSA Models on NPU (GLM-5.2): Enable compact indexer layout across all NPU generations to reclaim $75\%$ of indexer memory ($+15.8\%$ KV tokens) and dispatch dynamic INT8 `MLAProlog` via kernel-class checks.
  • High-QPS Disaggregated KV Routers: Use `hash_complete_blocks` in-place slice hashing and `ActivationSnapshot::Pending` fail-closed HTTP 503 responses during prefill rollouts.

⛔ Not For (不适用或需门控场景)

  • Heavy Uniform ($d=128$, $\ge 64$ pairs/CTA) or Heterogeneous Decode Batches: Do NOT force L2 V-prefetch or `evict_first` stream hints (`plan_flags` must remain `0`) as aggressive hints degrade HBM/L2 efficiency under high CTA occupancy.
  • Static W8A8 INT8 Projections on NPU: Do NOT enable dynamic `MLAProlog` (`weight_quant_mode=2`) based solely on `weight.dtype == torch.int8`, and never free `w_kc` on disaggregated decode when `mode == 2`.
  • Checkpoint-Loaded Meta-Device Tied Embeddings Without Initializers: Never materialize with `torch.empty_like` before `embd_group` `all_reduce`; always use `torch.zeros_like` to avoid summing uninitialized `NaN`s.
