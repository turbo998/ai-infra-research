7 / 7 · MY TAKE GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & PRODUCTION READINESS

Architectural Guidance for Hybrid FP4/FP8 Diffusion, MoE Serving & Multi-Node KV Fabrics

面向混合 FP4/FP8 扩散生成、MoE 推理服务与多机 KV 传输织网的架构选型指南

✅ Good Fit

  • SM120 Video Diffusion (`MiniMax-H3`): Opt into `pv_mode="fp8"` when pure NVFP4 (`0.19` rel-L2) degrades visual fidelity; recovers `26.4%` error reduction while retaining `1.38x` (RTX 5090) and `1.19x` (RTX PRO 6000) speedup over FP8.
  • Jetson AGX Thor (`sm_110a`) Speculative Decode: Use `cake_sm110_xqa` `kernel="auto"` (`pair` on even-128-row E4M3 tiles, `tmem` otherwise) and `D128` decode (`1.386x–1.539x`).
  • JAX MoE + mHC Training (`TE #3680/#3639`): Use fused Triton `mHC` (`tf32x3`, 20-iter log-Sinkhorn) and per-step `ncclEpImportHandle` v2 across `jax.lax.scan` layers.
  • Ascend A5 Host RoCE KV Store (`Mooncake #4756`): Use 2 MiB THP `memfd` (`ASCEND_USE_SHMEM`) + `SharedHostMapper` so local peers copy via `aclrtMemcpyBatch` while NICs drive `roce:host`.
  • SM120 视频扩散 (`MiniMax-H3`)：当纯 NVFP4 (`0.19` rel-L2) 影响画质时启用 `pv_mode="fp8"`，误差降低 `26.4%` 且较纯 FP8 保持 `1.38x` (RTX 5090) 与 `1.19x` (PRO 6000) 提速。
  • Jetson AGX Thor (`sm_110a`) 投机解码：使用 `cake_sm110_xqa` `kernel="auto"`（偶数 128 行 E4M3 Tile 选 `pair`，其余选 `tmem`）与 `D128` 解码 (`1.386x–1.539x`)。
  • JAX MoE + mHC 训练 (`TE #3680/#3639`)：在 `jax.lax.scan` 层间采用融合 Triton `mHC` (`tf32x3` + 20 轮对数 Sinkhorn) 与每步 `ncclEpImportHandle` v2 重建。
  • 昇腾 A5 Host RoCE KV 存储 (`Mooncake #4756`)：启用 2 MiB THP `memfd` (`ASCEND_USE_SHMEM`) 与 `SharedHostMapper`，兼顾 `roce:host` 直驱与同机 `aclrtMemcpyBatch`。

⛔ Not For

  • Non-SM120 Architectures on `minimax_h3_sm120_*`: Cubins target `sm_120a` specifically (`H=24, D=128`); Hopper/Blackwell datacenter (`sm_90a/sm_100a`) require their respective WGMMA/tcgen05 attention kernels.
  • Retired SM110 Tree Routes: Passing `"tcgen05"`, `"register_mma"`, `"register_mma_split"`, or `"register_mma_auto"` to `sm110_xqa` now raises `ValueError`.
  • Pointer-Keyed EP Caches in JAX Scan Loops: Never cache `ncclEpHandle_t` by raw XLA buffer pointer across `jax.lax.scan` iterations due to XLA buffer relocation aliasing.
  • `cpp_extension.load_inline` in Multi-Rank Init: Never call `load_inline` with a shared `build_directory` across concurrent ranks without external synchronization; use packaged C++ sources with `cpp_extension.load`.
  • 非 SM120 架构调用 `minimax_h3_sm120_*`：该 Cubin 专为 `sm_120a` (`H=24, D=128`) 编译，Hopper/Blackwell 数据中心卡需使用各自的 WGMMA/tcgen05 内核。
  • 已退役的 SM110 Tree 路由参数：向 `sm110_xqa` 传入 `"tcgen05"` 或 `"register_mma*"` 会直接抛出 `ValueError`。
  • JAX Scan 循环中的指针 Key 句柄缓存：严禁在 `jax.lax.scan` 中按 XLA 缓冲区裸指针缓存 `ncclEpHandle_t`，否则会因缓冲区重定位发生跨层错用。
  • 多 Rank 并发初始化调用 `load_inline`：严禁多进程共享 `build_directory` 调用无锁覆写 `main.cpp` 的 `load_inline`，应使用静态源码配合 `cpp_extension.load`。
