7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Architectural Fit, Guardrails & Production Readiness

✅ Good fit
  • Multi-NIC EFA clusters (`p6-b300.48xlarge`) using `MC_EFA_NIC_SELECTION=local` for 30.4x faster KV registration.
  • TP>1 speculative decoding (`DFlash`/`DSpark`) requiring deterministic Top-P/Top-K renormalization.
  • Blackwell/Rubin (`sm_100a`/`sm_103a`/`sm_107a`/`sm_120a`) MoE, MXFP8 PDL, and varlen attention pipelines.
⛔ Not for
  • Using `fast_tanhf` where IEEE/OCML exact `libdevice.tanh` ULP bounds are strictly required on CDNA5.
  • Unconditionally emitting custom SSE event-only frames (`x-bypass-remote-prefill`) to standard OpenAI SDK clients.
  • Configuring `cuda_streams > 4` in NIXL DOCA GPUNetIO (`DOCA_POST_STREAM_NUM` hardware-bounded to 4).
