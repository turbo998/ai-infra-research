SCENARIO: FP8_1X128_AND_NVFP4_RHT_SR_PRETRAINING_NUMERICS 2 / 7

16x16 RHT Lifts NVFP4 Wgrad SQNR by +16.87 dB (18.19 → 35.06 dB) While Stochastic Rounding Cuts Gradient Bias 31.1x

INPUT: outlier_ratio=100x | fp8_tile=1x128 & 128x128 | nvfp4_block=1x16 (E2M1 + E4M3 scale + FP32 global) | K=4096 | seed=42
score 100x Outlier Non-Outlier Channel SQNR (`nvfp4.rst` & `sm90_fp8_gemm_1d2d.cuh`)
Act automatically

Under a `100x` activation outlier spike, Tensor-wise FP8 Delayed Scaling crushes normal channels to 21.49 dB SQNR (`37.5%` underflow). DeepSeek `1x128` tile scaling isolates the spike and restores 31.94 dB (`+10.45 dB`). On Wgrad (`16`-dim reduction), `16x16` Random Hadamard Transform (`RHT`) drops crest factor from `3.048` to `1.410`, boosting NVFP4 SQNR from 18.19 dB to 35.06 dB.

choice NVFP4 Sub-Grid Gradient Bias: Round-to-Nearest vs Stochastic Rounding (`core_nvfp4.cuh`)
Act automatically

Because `E2M1` has only 8 positive levels (`0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0, 4.0, 6.0`), deterministic Round-to-Nearest (`RTN`) systematically truncates small gradients (`38.89%` relative bias), stalling weight updates. Hardware `cvt.rs.satfinite.e2m1x4` Stochastic Rounding (`SR`) reduces mean bias to 1.25% (31.1x reduction).

noul Hopper WGMMA 13-Bit Mantissa Swamping vs DeepGEMM 128-Step FP32 Promotion
Act automatically

Accumulating `K=4096` products in a narrow 13-bit WGMMA accumulator yields 0.0346% relative error (`4.12x` above FP32). Promoting partial sums to CUDA Core FP32 every `BLOCK_K=128` steps (`sm90_fp8_gemm_1d2d.cuh`) cuts error to 0.0056% (6.18x lower error).

MY TAKE · ARCHITECTURAL FIT, ANTI-PATTERNS & READINESS 7 / 7

Deploy NVFP4 RHT+SR, NVRx In-Process Hot Standby & ORv3 Supercaps; Ban RTN Grad Casts & Dummy Burn Kernels

✅ Good Fit (Production Patterns)
  • DeepSeek `1x128` / `128x128` FP8 + 128-Step FP32 Promotion: Preserves `31.94 dB` SQNR on non-outlier channels (`+10.45 dB` over Delayed Scaling) and cuts Hopper WGMMA accumulation error `6.18x`.
  • Blackwell NVFP4 with `16x16` RHT, 2D Weight Tiles & SR: Lifts Wgrad SQNR to `35.06 dB` (`+16.87 dB`) and reduces small-gradient truncation bias from `38.89%` to `1.25%` (`cvt.rs.satfinite.e2m1x4`).
  • NVRx `inprocess.Wrapper` + `CliqueReplicationStrategy`: Recovers 64K-GPU faults in `19.8 s` without killing OS processes, sustaining `90.30%` ETTR (`+71.72 pts` over Slurm+PFS) at a `40.0 s` checkpoint interval.
  • Orthogonal ABFT + AllReduce Checksums (`0.073%` FLOP tax): Catches `100%` (`16/16`) of finite BF16 single-bit flips within `1` step.
  • OCP ORv3 `87.1 F` (`43.9 kJ`) Supercap CBU + `15 ms` DCGM Power Smoothing: Compresses 16K-GPU `GB200 NVL72` `dP/dt` `264.91x` (`3,014.7 → 11.38 MW/s`) with only `0.85%` step overhead.
⛔ Not For (Anti-Patterns to Avoid)
  • Tensor-Wise Delayed Scaling on Outlier-Heavy LLMs: A `100x` activation outlier forces `37.5%` of normal activations to underflow to zero (`21.49 dB` SQNR).
  • NVFP4 Backward Gradients Without RHT or Stochastic Rounding: Unrotated Wgrad collapses to `18.19 dB` SQNR, and RTN rounding zeroes out sub-grid updates (`38.89%` bias).
  • Slurm Whole-Job Restart + PFS Checkpoints at ≥16K GPUs: At `65,536` GPUs (`10.99 min` MTBF), `960 s` restarts collapse ETTR to `18.58%`.
  • Relying Solely on `NaN/Inf` Watchdogs for SDC Defense: `16 / 16` single-bit flips of finite BF16 weights (`exp < 255`) remain finite numbers (`0.0%` immediate detection), corrupting ~`250` steps.
  • Dummy Burn Kernels During Collectives: Wastes `+5.88%` to `+7.40%` facility power and still violates grid `dP/dt` limits (`1,191 MW/s`).
