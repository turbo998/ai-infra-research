7 / 7 · MY TAKE GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Production Adoption Guide for Phase 61 Kernel, Serving & Training Upgrades

✅ Good Fit

  • Blackwell/Rubin MXFP8 & NVFP4 Decode: Fusing MXFP8 block-scale quantization (`SWIZZLED_128x4`) directly into TRT-LLM and MNNVL AllReduce+RMSNorm when `hidden_dim % 32 == 0`.
  • 256K+ Packed KDA & GDN Training/Prefill: Using Cake KDA chunked grad on GB300/B200 (`1.435x–1.892x`) and native `sm_107a` GDN CP precompute on Rubin.
  • Multi-Node Kimi-K3 DWDP & ROCm MI355X: Using 14-stage TMA bulk-copy fabric gather (`212,992 B/SM` in flight) and BF16 KDA state (`-50%` state HBM).
  • Disaggregated PD & Dynamic Collectives: Poisoning deferred abort ACKs on NIXL/MoRI write failure and quarter-step bucketing `symmetric_all_reduce` workspaces.

⛔ Not For

  • Unaligned Hidden Dimensions (`hidden_dim % 32 != 0`): MXFP8 1D-32 UE8M0 block scaling requires `hidden_dim` divisible by 32.
  • Whole-GPU Cooperative Routing with Persistent DWDP: Leaving `TLLM_ROUTING_DISABLE_COOP_LAUNCH` unset serializes `smCount - 8` cooperative routing grids behind persistent DWDP prefetch blocks.
  • Expandable Segments for Cross-Process CUDA IPC: Allocating IPC barrier flags inside `expandable_segments:True` pools triggers `pidfd_getfd` `EPERM` in unprivileged containers and crashes `empty_cache()` on GB200/GB300.
  • MoRIIO Producer-Side Specialized Decode: vLLM `MoRIIOConnector` specialized decode is only valid on `kv_consumer` instances.
