7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS ARCHITECTURAL VERDICT

Production Adoption Guide: When to Enable Phase 64 Optimizations vs Boundary Constraints

✅ Good fit

  • Long-Context KDA CP Training (`P > 1`): Keep `SAVED_CARRY_DEFAULT=1` on GB300 (`sm_103a`) to skip backward segment re-walks (+2.4% to +3.9% B3 speedup for 12–80 MiB saved plane).
  • H100 NVFP4 Paged Decode (`rows = 8..64`): Enable Cake QSA NVFP4 (`backend="cake"`) for 1.074x–1.127x faster sparse GQA decode.
  • SM100/SM103 High-Batch BF16 MLP: Opt into `fuse_bf16_gate_up_swiglu=True` (e.g., MiniMax-H3) to eliminate 80% of GatedMLP activation HBM traffic.
  • MFSDPv2 MXFP8 Training: Gather only `rowwise` in Forward and `columnwise` in Backward to cut All-Gather bandwidth by 50%.
  • High-IOPS RDMA & Disaggregated KV: Use per-chain clock reads in Mooncake, `alignas(256)` ring buffers in NIXL, and callback-driven `LookupState` in LMCache.

⛔ Not for

  • Small-M Decode on QuACK Fused SwiGLU: Keep `fuse_bf16_gate_up_swiglu=False` (default) at tiny token counts where cuBLAS + standalone SwiGLU has lower launch overhead, or on SM107 where QuACK is unvalidated.
  • EP A2A Full Recompute + CUDA Graphs / FSDP: Megatron's per-segment EP A2A full recompute currently requires `--cuda-graph-impl none`, `distribute_saved_activations=False`, and is mutually exclusive with Megatron FSDP.
  • Shared `launch_epochs` Across Dynamic Grids: Do not share a single `launch_epochs` tensor across MegaMHC plans that launch different CTA counts (`64` vs `148`), or pass sliced tensor views.
  • Non-Unit Strided TIR Loops in `VectorizeLoop`: TileLang explicitly rejects `step != 1` in `VectorizeLoop`; tile/compact strided axes first.
