UNDER THE HOOD

Two FP16 values plus four bits occupy 56.25% of aligned dense bytes.

SYNTHETIC · independently_reproduced=false · no GPU, quality or physical-packing measurement

for each adjacent quartet:
    retain two positions i0 < i1
    values = [weight[i0], weight[i1]]
    logical_code = i0 | (i1 << 2)

FP16 values: 2 * 16 bits
metadata:    4 bits
dense:       4 * 16 bits
ratio:       36 / 64 = 56.25%

↓ physical backend format is a separate contract

CUTLASS FP16/BF16 sparse shape: M multiple 32, K multiple 64
cuSPARSELt FP16/BF16:          M multiple 16, K multiple 16
Caller padding and cropping are explicit here.
Backend swizzles, masks and actual packed allocation: unknown.
Dtype boundary: pinned CUTLASS FP32 takes a different 1:2 conversion branch. FP8 requires separate scaled operations and platform gates; neither is modeled.
