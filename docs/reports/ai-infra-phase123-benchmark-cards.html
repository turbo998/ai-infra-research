7 / 7 · MY TAKE · ARCHITECTURAL FIT & PRODUCTION READINESS Decision Guide

Adopt 4-Slot cuDNN Caching, Prefix KV Sharding & `add_request` Gates Now; Respect Hardware & NCCL Prerequisites

✅ Good Fit (Immediate Production Value)

  • Rubin `SM107` Deployments: Enable AOT `SM100f` CUTLASS FP8/MoE wheels (`#6795`) and `PACKED_M64_N8` (`#7735`) for narrow 8-row unbiased GEMMs.
  • Multi-Geometry Prefill & Prefix Sharing: Use FlashInfer's 4-slot `_cudnn_prepared_cache` (`#7720`) on B200 and SGLang `--enable-kv-cache-sharding` (`#46789`) on NVLink systems with NCCL `>= 2.31.2`.
  • Async RLHF & Disaggregated KV: Deploy veRL `_generate_admitted` (`#8397`), vLLM `_DeferredRelease` (`#63779`), LMCache atomic rollback (`#6077`), Mooncake EFA `kLocalOnlyKey` (`#4976`), and NIXL UCCL `optional<nixl_blob_t>` (`#2429`).

⛔ Not For (Known Boundaries & Caveats)

  • Biased or Non-Rubin 8-Row GEMMs: `PACKED_M64_N8` is strictly gated to `sm_107a` with `has_bias=False` and `K % 64 == 0`; Blackwell `sm_100a` retains 2-CTA tiles.
  • Compensated or Short/Ultra-Long SM120 Attention: `ATTENTION_WS_NODELTA_LEAD/TAIL` (`#7730`) is disabled when delta compensation is active or $\sum L_i^2 \notin [2^{23}, 24576^2]$.
  • Legacy NCCL `< 2.31.2` for Zero-SM KV Sharding: `--enable-kv-cache-sharding` zero-SM Copy Engine all-gather requires `torch.cuda.nccl.version() >= (2, 31, 2)`.
