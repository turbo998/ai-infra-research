7 / 7   MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS ARCHITECTURAL GUIDANCE · 架构师选型建议

Production-Ready for Long-Context Compiled Training & Tiered KV Offload; Avoid Blind Cat-0 on CosSinRoPE

综合源码级机制与定量实验结果，给出面向云与 AI 基础设施解决方案架构师的适用边界与反模式清单。

✅ Good fit / 推荐适用场景

  • vLLM Long-Context DCP Prefill (7b569ec895): Unquantized KV cache deployments without sliding window/soft-cap where stats_bytes + 1 MiB <= workspace.nbytes, with mandatory zero-init + mask_dcp_empty_shards_ for ragged/short prompts.
  • Compiled ComplexRoPE & SwiGLU Regions (b95271ed07, eb48bab350): Contiguous bf16 Q/K rotary embeddings (5.02x-6.07x prefill speedup) and packed [..., 2, F] SwiGLU backward (-27.24% latency) with autotune_pointwise=False for deterministic FSDP+CP+PP+EP training.
  • Mixed-Precision fp32 Residual Norms (b78c4aed96): Casting bf16 norm weights to input.dtype (fp32) before F.rms_norm to collapse ~20 fallback kernels into 1 fused kernel at zero precision loss.
  • Tiered KV Offload & Shared L1 Pools (41913522c7, 258746ce77, 5cf142e344): Filtering ephemeral KV pages via cold_tier_offload_min_priority=30, fencing crashed MP writers via incarnation retirement, and distinguishing InvalidArgument vs InternalError in Mooncake TENT notifications.

⛔ Not for / 不适用或需规避场景

  • Applying max_pointwise_cat_inputs=0 to CosSinRoPE: While unmasked cat=0 speeds up ComplexRoPE (fp32 stack) by 1.4x and SwiGLU backward by 27%, it makes split-half CosSinRoPE 1.3x–2.6x slower on Qwen3 16k–64k shapes; keep separate decorators per RoPE class.
  • Strided Split-View int32 RoPE Loads: Non-contiguous split views (such as DeepSeek-V3/Kimi q_pe/k_pe) cannot be viewed as int32 without copying first; they must load two halves in fp32 and pack only the output as int32.
  • Per-Request GDS/POSIX Transfer Modes in TRT-LLM KVCM V2: KVCacheManagerV2 rejects non-DRAM transfer modes in per-request KvCacheRetentionConfig; configure offload tiers at the manager level instead.
  • Long-Running Shared L1 Churn Without Extent Reclamation: LMCache RegionState in [4/N] uses a monotonic bump allocator where CONSUMED extents are not yet recycled until follow-up eviction lands.
