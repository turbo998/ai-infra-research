2 / 7 · SCENARIO: FLASHMLA SM100 V4.1 FUSED KERNEL & DEQUANT KERNEL & NUMERICS

`KVBlockDequantizer` +32B Padding Eliminates 4-Way Bank Conflicts and Fits Fused `H_Q=64/128` in 512/480 TMEM Columns

Input Case (`csrc/kernels/sm100/dequant_utils.cuh` & `core_attn/config.h`): Dequantize paged `V41_FP4` (`512 e2m1 + 32 e4m3 scales = 288 B/token`, `TMA_K_STRIDE = 256 B`) into `SW128` K-major BF16 tiles while fusing `Q Norm + Q RoPE + Sparse Top-K Attention + O RoPE + O FP8 Cast` on SM100.
Decision 1 · `+32 B` TMA Box Padding (`288 B` 1-CTA / `160 B` 2-CTA) for `LDS.32`
choice Act automatically

Unpadded `256 B` / `128 B` rows satisfy `stride/4 % 16 == 0`, causing 4-way bank conflicts across `gather4` rows. Padding to `288 B` / `160 B` (`stride/4 % 16 == 8`) places the 4 rows in banks 0, 8, 16, 24 (1-way conflict-free, `100.0%` efficiency).

Decision 2 · Bit-Exact `e2m1 × e4m3` in BF16 (`2 + 4 = 6 <= 8` Significand Bits)
score Act automatically

Verified across all `2,032` finite non-NaN `(e2m1, e4m3)` pairs: IEEE-754 FP32 product low 16 bits are `0x0000` in `2,032 / 2,032` cases (`0` rounding error).

Decision 3 · Fused SM100 TMEM Allocation (`512` cols at `H_Q=64`, `480` cols at `H_Q=128`)
noul Act automatically

`O=[0,256), Q=[256,384), P_0=[384,448), P_1=[448,512)` (`512/512 = 100%` TMEM utilization, `NEED_TP_EMPTY_BAR=0`) for 1-CTA; `O=[0,256), Q=[256,384), P_0=[384,480)` (`480/512 = 93.75%`) for 2-CTA.

Verification: `permute_q_b_proj` (`65,536` rows) and `permute_wv_proj` (`128` chunks/group) verified bijective; `st_async` `longlong2` (`16 B`) prevents MSVC LLP64 `long2` (`8 B`) upper-half truncation.

7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS ARCHITECTURAL GUIDANCE

Production Adoption Guide: When to Enable SM100 Fused MLA, `KVShard`, Uncached Routing & CXL Offsets

✅ Good Fit (适用场景)

  • Blackwell SM100 DeepSeek-V4/V4.1 Serving: Use `fused_norm_rope_attn_rope_cast_fwd` with pre-permuted `q_b_proj` / `wv_proj` weights and `V41_FP4` (`288 B/tok`) secondary KV cache for 2.28x KV capacity and conflict-free warpgroup dequantization.
  • Long-Context MLA Prefill Disaggregation: Enable `--enable-kv-cache-sharding` (`KVShard`) when prefill and decode share equal `attn_tp_size` and `attn_cp_size == 1`, widening logical prefill KV capacity by $N\times$.
  • Prefix-Heavy Bucketed Prefill Pools: Enable `--route-by-uncached-tokens` in `sgl-router` so cache-warm long prompts (`E <= 8,192`) utilize `short` prefill workers and spill to idle DP ranks (`idle_spill`).
  • Multi-Tenant CXL / Device-DAX Hosts: Use `--l1-devdax-offset-gb` with `2 MiB`-aligned disjoint ranges so multiple LMCache servers safely share one `/dev/dax*` CXL device.

⛔ Not For (不适用边界)

  • Split-KV Fused Epilogue: `fused_norm_rope_attn_rope_cast_fwd` does not support Split-KV because its fused conjugate `O RoPE` + `FP8 E4M3/UE8M0` cast epilogue is non-associative across splits.
  • Asymmetric TP or Decode DCP with `KVShard`: `KVShard` explicitly rejects decode DCP (`RuntimeError`) and requires equal prefill/decode `attn_tp_size` (`assert self.attn_tp_size == info.attn_tp_size`).
  • Lazy Allocator on Device-DAX: `L1MemoryManagerConfig` forbids combining `devdax_path` with `use_lazy=True`, and the primary arena in pure Device-DAX mode cannot be hot-removed.
  • Unfiltered Snapshot Tail Admission: Never admit `DcCkfRankReplacement` canonical candidates without filtering by surviving `owner_increments` after replaying `Removed`/`Cleared` events.
