7 / 7 · MY TAKE GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Production Deployment Guide: Where Phase 60 Optimizations Deliver Immediate ROI

✅ Good Fit (推荐生产落地场景)

1. SM120 Workstation & Edge MLA/GQA Serving: Enables 1.53x–1.61x faster FP8 prefill on GLM-4.7-Flash (`256/256`) and GLM-4.7 (`128/128` GQA) within 64 KiB dynamic SMEM.

2. Fine-Grained MoE with Non-256-Aligned $I$: Hopper MXFP4×FP8 MegaMoE K128 folded tiles recover up to 17.72% compute latency on $I=640$ (`640 % 256 != 0`).

3. Multi-Branch Agentic SWA Workloads: TensorRT-LLM `enable_branch_snapshot=True` commits SWA windows at radix fork points, saving 71.88% sibling prefill compute.

4. CUDA-Graphed MoE & Eval Sharing: Megatron live-row bounding (`-69.53%` work) + TorchTitan shared eval graph pool (`0 GiB` extra memory).

5. High-Rate KV Telemetry & RDMA Notification: NIXL v6 256B ring buffer (`51.9M/s`) and Mooncake post-success `wr_id` increment.

⛔ Not For / Watch Outs (不适用或需规避场景)

1. Unaligned SM8x 2:4 Sparse Metadata Shapes: Shapes violating $M \bmod 32 = 0$ (16-bit) or $K \bmod 2 = 0$ are non-bijective and now hard-fail in TileLang.

2. ROCm TP > 2 with `--disable-custom-all-reduce` in Deterministic Mode: Refused at startup because RCCL multi-rank reductions are not batch-invariant.

3. Dense 64-Token Paged FMHA on Rubin SM107 via `FlashInferTrtllmGenFmha`: SM107 cubins currently target `P128` (128-token Eagle3 draft pages); keep dense layers on SDPA.

4. Implicit V Stride on Sliced Fused Projections: Never pass sliced `[Q|K|V|Gate]` tensors to Context FMHA without explicit 16B-aligned `v_stride_in_bytes`.
