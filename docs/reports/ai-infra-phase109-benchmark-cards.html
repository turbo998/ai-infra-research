7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECT GUIDANCE & READINESS

When to Adopt Phase 109 Optimizations vs Where Guardrails Are Mandatory

✅ Good fit

  • Eager Small-Batch Serving & Dynamic Prefill: Caching GEMM plan keys (`59 us` vs `200 us`) and skipping unused cuDNN paged uploads (`1` vs `3` H2D copies) directly cut CPU launch bottlenecks.
  • NVLink Sequence Parallelism & Hybrid Mamba/MLA: Direct-write Ulysses kernels (`24` programs), NVFP4 MLA gather (`1.88x`), and FlashInfer GDN ring-buffer replay (`2.50x`) reduce memory traffic and MTP rollback overhead.
  • ROCm RDMA KV Transport & Multi-Tier L1/L2 Caching: Mooncake's exact KFD free-VRAM accounting (`#4657`) and LMCache `MemoryTags` (`#5919`) harden disaggregated KV pools on MI300X and NUMA hosts.

⛔ Not for

  • Blind TMA Dispatch on Consumer/Workstation Blackwell (SM120): Never assume SM100's 228 KiB SMEM limit applies to SM120 (`99 KiB` limit); FP32 gated TMA (`160 KiB`) must fall back (`TE #3600`).
  • Layer-0-Only TP Inference on Hybrid Models: Inferring TP solely from layer 0 fails on heterogeneous head counts (`[32, 48, 64]`); always compute cross-layer GCD (`vLLM #63857`).
  • Unprofiled 90% KV Allocation on Aggregated Helix: Skipping memory profiling without lowering KV fraction to `0.60` (`TRT-LLM #20759`) or reserving QSA workspace (`vLLM #60490`) risks prefill OOM.
