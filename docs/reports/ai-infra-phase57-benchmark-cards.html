7 / 7 · MY TAKEARCHITECT FIT & READINESS

When to Adopt Phase 57 Kernel, Allocator & Collective Optimizations

✅ Good Fit

1. Long-Context Checkpointed KDA Training: Immediate adoption of `weakref` workspace + `zero_init=False` saves `4.5..17.9 GiB/layer` and `28%` kernel time on GB300.

2. Disaggregated 128K FP4 Prefill (TRT-LLM): Gating `aux_stream` quantization at `1024` tokens preserves decode overlap while eliminating prefill `cudaFree` stalls.

3. Blackwell Per-Token NVFP4 MoE & MFSDPv2 MXFP8: `3.99x..4.52x` MoE speedup and `-50%` FSDP all-gather traffic.

4. Non-256 MoE Intermediates on Hopper (`I=640`): Folded `K128/N64` tactics recover `-17.72%` compute latency.

⛔ Not For

1. Unbounded Aux-Stream Async Quantization on Prefill: Never run multi-GB prefill quantization on an auxiliary stream without token-count gating (`record_stream` bloats allocator watermarks).

2. Python-Slice Padding Masks Inside CUDA Graphs: Static Python slices freeze at capture time and silently corrupt FP8/FP4 `amax` on partial-batch replay.

3. Applying `sm_103a` `_cg2` or `sm_100a` SVDQuant Tail-Split Blindly Across All Shapes: Keep shape-specific dispatch tables; non-matching shapes regress occupancy.
