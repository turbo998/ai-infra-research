7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS ARCHITECT GUIDANCE

Production-Ready Patterns for Blackwell/Rubin Kernels, MTP Serving & Disaggregated KV Handoff

✅ Good Fit (Recommended Adoption)

  • Split-KV Paged Context & MLA Decode: Enable static round-0 tile assignment (`num_ctas <= total_tiles_4`) and enforce `>= 1024` KV tokens per raised MLA split (`a8803f2c`, `9a2aea3f`).
  • Multi-Step MTP & QSA Prefill Serving: Fuse inter-graph metadata copies into `small_field_copy.cuh` and eliminate redundant D2D KV gather copies in QSA prefill (`b74fd392`, `148bea73`).
  • Large-Batch MoE & GLM5.3 Draft Caching: Use `int64_t` offsets in fused MoE router/aux-loss kernels (`268fdd54`) and enable `dspark` FP8 draft KV (`c6f6e6a9`, saving `5.38 GB/rank`).
  • Disaggregated `TP > KV-Heads` & Vision RoPE: Deduplicate replicated KV heads during prefill-to-decode handoff (`259f6a7c`) and keep ViT RoPE frequencies in `FP32` (`3ca52d95`).

⛔ Not For (Anti-Patterns to Avoid)

  • Tiny MLA KV Splits (< 1K Tokens/Split): Raising split counts when each split streams fewer than `1024` KV tokens amplifies combine bandwidth and hurts tail latency.
  • 32-Bit Signed Offsets in Large MoE Routers: Never use `int32_t` for `token * expert * stride` indexing when `T * E` scales to hundreds of millions of entries.
  • Dropping Grammar-Invalid Drafts Before Rejection Sampling: Filtering draft tokens prior to rejection sampling biases the target distribution in speculative decoding (`c4f2bb4a`).
  • Raw `new[]` Without RAII in RDMA Error Paths: Manual array cleanup after `postXfer` loops leaks memory whenever an early transfer submission fails (`7bd1d138`).
