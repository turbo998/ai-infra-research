7 / 7MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Architectural Adoption Guide for Phase 115 Kernels, Serving Schedulers & Transport Runtimes

✅ Good fit
  • Blackwell/Rubin (`SM100/103/107`) deployments running MXFP8/NVFP4 MoE, QSA, and MLA decode kernels.
  • P/D disaggregated serving requiring decode-side RadixCache KV checksum verification and 0-SM CE AllGather.
  • CUDA-graph MoE serving (`Gemma-4`) where batch padding otherwise wastes 85%+ of router compute at low batch sizes.
  • RL post-training with custom YaRN/mscale attention factors and high-load Mooncake/EFA KV fabrics.
⛔ Not for
  • Pre-Hopper GPUs lacking TMA, tcgen05, or hardware FP8/FP4 block-scaled MMA instructions.
  • Workloads with strictly uniform unpadded batch sizes where CUDA-graph padding-row skip yields zero delta.
  • Single-NIC nodes without Libfabric/EFA or multi-container Kubernetes DRA claims.
