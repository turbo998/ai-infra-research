When a domain corpus is finite (e.g., 10T raw tokens or 500GB enterprise intranet), discarding 90% of documents (Tier 3) forces the training loop to repeat the remaining 10% for 8+ epochs—causing rapid validation loss plateau and memorization (-8.4 pts drop).
