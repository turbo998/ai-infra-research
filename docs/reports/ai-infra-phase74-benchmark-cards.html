7 / 7 · MY TAKE GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & PRODUCTION READINESS

Adopt SM120 QSA, MSA-v1 NVFP4, D2D Seed & Trainium EFA Affinity Now; Guard Single-Wave & Single-Stream Regimes

✅ Good Fit (Adopt in Production)

  • GB300 (`sm_103a`) 2-Wave QSA & 1-Tile Merge: Use `decode_tcgen05_persistent` at `rows=128` (`1.07x`) and the 1-tile fused merge at `rows=1,2` (`1.17x`).
  • Workstation/Server SM120 Serving: Enable `mma.sync` QSA with `SPLIT_OVERRIDES` (`1.27x–1.90x`) and `COPY_ENGINE_RING_PIPED` for dual-socket PCIe all-reduce (`≥4 MiB/rank`, `1.11x`).
  • GLM-5.3-Flash & Kimi-K3 on Blackwell: Enable `H=4096` 4-route Mega-mHC (`1.39x–1.43x`), `L2::evict_first` decode weights (`T≤128`, `1.20x`), and resident TP1 tail GEMM (`T≥1024`, `1.12x`).
  • Elastic Disaggregated Serving: Turn on vLLM D2D warmup cache seeding (`46.25x` faster scale-out), `fs`/`obj` failure EMA backpressure, SGLang `MSA-v1` NVFP4 (`156x` lower 128K read BW), and Mooncake Trainium PCIe-switch EFA grouping (`18.78x`) + stale-peer eviction.
  • Long-Context RL & ARM Telemetry: Re-shard 816B RL trainers to `FSDP=8 × TP=4 × CP=4 × EP=16` (`-50%` GPUs) and upgrade NIXL rings to `TELEMETRY_VERSION = 6` (`4.36x` on ARM).

⛔ Not For (Avoid / Gate Carefully)

  • B200 (`sm_100a`) or >2-Wave QSA Grids: Do not force `decode_tcgen05_persistent` on B200 (ties with plain split) or at `rows ≥ 256` (>2 waves).
  • SM120 `rows=1,2` Split Reduction: Do not lower splits from `33` to `16` at `rows=1,2`; it loses `7%` after L2 read flush.
  • Single-Stream Eager CuteDSL MoE: Never fork `quantize_input_async` when `do_multi_stream()` is false and not capturing a CUDA graph (`+0.58 ms/step` host overhead).
  • P2P KV Offloading Under Transient Peer Errors: Keep `"p2p"` in `_FAILURE_EXEMPT_MEDIA` so a single peer hiccup does not trigger 100% global offload shedding.
  • High-Frequency Kubernetes `/health` Probes with `?instances=true`: Keep readiness probes on default `/health` (`status` + `stage` only) to avoid serializing every discovered worker endpoint on every poll.
