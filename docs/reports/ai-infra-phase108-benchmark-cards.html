7 / 7 · MY TAKE GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Production-Ready for Blackwell NVFP4 Prefill, THP Host KV Pools, and Composite ETP×EP MoE Serving

✅ Good Fit (When to Adopt)

  • Blackwell (`sm_100a`/`sm_103a`) Long-Context Prefill: Cake FMHA NVFP4 KV programs (#7512) when HBM bandwidth bounds TTFT at 8K–128K context lengths.
  • Multi-Hundred-GB Host KV Offload (HiCache): Pre-allocating 2MB/1GB huge pages with SGLANG_HICACHE_HOST_PARALLEL_ALLOC_THREADS=16 (#43978) to avoid 14s startup stalls and TLB thrashing.
  • Hybrid Attention + Mamba Disaggregated Serving: vLLM NIXL per-group region filtering (#62317) to avoid transferring cross-group SSM/Attention descriptors.
  • Large MoE Inference Under Tight HBM Budgets: Megatron-LM composite tp_ep (#4743) when single-expert weights exceed per-rank EP memory capacity.

⛔ Not For (When to Avoid)

  • Unconfigured OS Hugepages: Setting SGLANG_HUGEPAGE_SIZE=2MB without host /proc/sys/vm/nr_hugepages falls back with warnings; provision OS hugepages first.
  • Single-Process Dual CUDA + Intel XPU GDS: Mooncake TENT USE_XPU_GDS (#4904) cannot be linked alongside USE_CUDA in the same process due to libcufile.so symbol collision.
  • Backward-Pass Weight-Grad Training on Eval Fast Path: TE's single-group CuTeDSL DenseGEMM (#3666) without columnwise_data is strictly for no_grad / frozen_weights evaluation.
  • Throughput-First MoE When Determinism Is Mandatory: Enabling FORCE_MOE_KERNEL_DETERMINISTIC=1 (#20227) disables fused finalize reduction to guarantee bit-exactness.
