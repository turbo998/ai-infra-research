7 / 7 · MY TAKE Good Fit vs Not For & Readiness

Architectural Guidance for Solutions Architects: When to Deploy Phase 71 Kernel, Serving & KV Storage Optimizations

✅ Good Fit (Deploy Now)

  • Rubin (`sm_107a`/`SM107`) Multi-Wave mHC & Speculative MLA: Enable `tailpipe_wsmem` for $T \ge 3329$ ($\ge 5$ waves) and `cute-dsl-rubin-mtp` for $Q \in \{2, 4\}$ CUDA Graph (`total_q >= 256`) or long-context eager ($K \ge 8192$).
  • `SM120` Workstation / Edge Inference: Use `USE_VO_SPLIT` E4M3 FA2 decode (`head_dim=256`, `4x` register cut) and enforce TE's dynamic + `96 B` static SMEM check against the `99 KiB` per-block ceiling.
  • Blackwell Video & Long-Context Sparse FMHA: Use PrimsTS `VC-Attention-QK16` (`repair_tiles`) and TRT-LLM PrimsTS SageAttention VSA/SOL (`cosine >= 0.9987`).
  • RL Post-Training & Prefix KV Stores: Thread YaRN `softmax_scale` into vLLM rollout engines (`91.7x` KL drop) and use Mooncake `LastHitOnly` batch probes to prevent intermediate prefix lease pinning.

⛔ Not For (Avoid / Guard Against)

  • Short-Wave `mega_mhc` ($T < 3329$ on 208 SMs): Do not force `tailpipe_wsmem` when $\text{waves} < 5$ or $\text{splits} > 16$; shared-memory weight staging overhead is not amortized.
  • `SM120` TMA Gated FP8 Backward / FP32 Casts: `bwd_bf16` (`131,296 B`) and `fp32` (`164,064 B` / `229,600 B`) exceed `SM120`'s `99 KiB` opt-in SMEM limit and must fall back to non-TMA kernels.
  • Post-Concatenation Padding on Gated INT8-woq MoE: Never pad `w3_w1` only at the end; `w3` and `w1` must be 64-row padded individually before concatenation.
  • Unfiltered Hybrid KVCR Streams in Dynamo: Never run `kv_block_size` geometry checks before filtering `NonMainAttentionGroup`, or Mamba state blocks will falsely quarantine the worker's `CacheOwner`.
