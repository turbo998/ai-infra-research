7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Good Fit vs Not For & Production Readiness

✅ Good fit
• All FSDP2 (`fully_shard`), DeepSpeed ZeRO-3, and TP/PP training & serving startups
• Models ≥ 14B where 8-rank CPU instantiation exceeds host RAM
• Fast elastic checkpoint resume with `torch.distributed.checkpoint (DCP)`
⛔ Not for
• Calling `to_empty(device="cuda")` BEFORE `fully_shard()` (allocates unsharded model on GPU)
• Forgetting to re-initialize `persistent=False` buffers (`inv_freq`) after `to_empty()`
