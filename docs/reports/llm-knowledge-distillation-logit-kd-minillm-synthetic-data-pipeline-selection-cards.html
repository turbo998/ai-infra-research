Forward KL (`Mean-Seeking`) vs. Reverse KL (`Mode-Seeking`)

Forward KL: KL(P_Teacher || P_Student)
-> Forces small 8B student to cover ALL teacher modes
-> Student places probability mass in invalid valleys!
Reverse KL: KL(P_Student || P_Teacher) [GKD / MiniLLM]
-> Locks 8B student onto the teacher's highest-confidence
mode on student-generated trajectories!
