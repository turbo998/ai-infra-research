7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECT GUIDANCE & READINESS

Deploy Remainder-Folded NVFP4, Fused Paged-Experts & Libfabric Reconnect Replies Immediately; Guard Mixed Quantizers

✅ Good fit

  • Blackwell NVFP4 Decoding & Chunked Prefill: Enable `cake_mm_fp4` cluster-split quantization (`M=1..8, 130`) and remainder folds (`M=129..136, 257..272`) on B200/GB300.
  • Speculative Decoding at Large Vocab (`V >= 128K`): Defer `ChainSpeculativeSampling` residual reduction to eliminate `31/32` block barriers.
  • 8-GPU Disaggregated PD (Mooncake / MoRI / NIXL): Bind worker threads to `gpu_id` (`-2.94 GiB` on GPU 0), use 63-bit global `disagg_request_id`, and deploy NIXL `cm_thread_` reconnect replies.
  • CUDA-Graphed MoE Training: Use TE `kNVTEGroupedRowScaledNVFP4` for 2D uniform row-scaled splits (`<= 64` experts).

⛔ Not for

  • Mixed Tensor/Row-Scaled NVFP4 Splits: Do not force fused `nvte_group_quantize` across heterogeneous quantizer lists (causes `504 B` amax buffer overflow without the 5-guard fallback).
  • Unaligned Last Dimensions (`last_dim % 128 != 0`) or `> 64` Experts: Fused row-scaled NVFP4 kernel requires 128-element alignment and at most 64 tensors per group.
  • gRPC Token-Only Streams Requiring String Stop Words: Setting `output_text = false` skips detokenization; string stop conditions require text decoding.
