✅ Good Fit

  • Long-Context Kimi-K3 / DeepSeek-V4 Serving: Mandatory 64-bit attn_res offsets for T > 42,800 and L2 prefetch for LatentMoE decode tails.
  • GB300 / Rubin SM107a Prefill & GEMM: Cluster-of-8 mc42 NVFP4 GEMM for even tile counts at M=1793..2048 and 1-CTA cluster SSD scans.
  • Memory-Bound mHC & MTP Training: Immediate 15.75 GiB backward savings in Cake mHC and -62.5% chunked MTP loss memory in TorchTitan.

  • Odd Tile Counts on GB300 mc42: 2x2 pair-block multicast requires even token and weight tile counts; odd counts must use 2-CTA CLC ring kernels.

  • Hardware Claim Extrapolation: Offline analytical models (own-offline) do not substitute for multi-node IB/RoCE hardware profiling.
