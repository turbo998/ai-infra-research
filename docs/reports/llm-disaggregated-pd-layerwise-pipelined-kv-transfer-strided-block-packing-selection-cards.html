Good Fit vs Not For & Production Readiness

✅ Good fit
• Disaggregated Prefill-Decode (`PD`) serving (`vLLM KVConnector`, `SGLang`, `Dynamo`)
• Long-context (`8K–128K`) RAG and agent workloads over RoCEv2 / InfiniBand

⛔ Not for
• Waiting until all 80 layers finish prefill before initiating RDMA KV transfers
• Issuing separate RDMA Write WQEs for each individual 16-token PagedAttention block
