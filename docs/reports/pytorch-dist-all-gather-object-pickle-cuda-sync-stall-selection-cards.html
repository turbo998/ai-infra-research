7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Architect Verdict: Ban dist.all_gather_object from Per-Step Training & Serving Loops

✅ Good fit
• Pre-allocated [world_size, K] tensor + all_gather_into_tensor for per-step sequence lengths, token counts, and flags
• SharedMemory ring (`shm_broadcast`) for intra-node `TP` worker scheduling
• Dedicated `gloo` group for one-off checkpoint/init object exchange
⛔ Not for
• Calling all_gather_object or broadcast_object_list on an `NCCL` group inside the step loop
• Passing dicts containing unstripped `torch.Tensor` objects to `all_gather_object`
