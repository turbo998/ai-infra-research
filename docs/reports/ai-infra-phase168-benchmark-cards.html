2 / 7SCENARIO: FLASHATTENTION CAUSAL SLIDING WINDOW & DEEPEP INTERNODE GRAPH

Preserving Zero Right-Window Bound Cuts Scheduled KV by 33x While Internode CUDA Graph Cuts Dispatch CPU Overhead by 7.4x

Input Case (`max_seqlen_k=128K, window_left=4096, window_right=0` & `61-Layer` MoE Internode Dispatch)

Python's `(window_size_right or max_seqlen_k)` treated `0` as falsy and planned `135,168` KV tokens instead of `4,096`. Meanwhile, eager internode dispatch across 61 MoE layers cost `18.5 μs/layer` on CPU.

Decision 1 · FA Causal Sliding-Window Zero-Bound Fix (`#2624`) choice · Act automatically

Check `window_size_right < 0` explicitly so `0` remains `0` (`4,096` vs `135,168` planned KV tokens → 33.0x overplan reduction).

Decision 2 · DeepEP Internode Dispatch CUDA Graph (`#438`) score · Act automatically

Capture internode RDMA dispatch inside CUDA Graphs (`2.5 μs/layer` vs `18.5 μs/layer` across 61 layers → 7.4x CPU overhead reduction).

SM90 Backward Fix (`#2482`): Aligns `tile_hdim` to a multiple of `64` (`192` vs `160`) when `dKV_swapAB=True`, eliminating WGMMA `M=64` partition crashes.
