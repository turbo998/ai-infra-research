7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS ARCHITECTURAL DECISION GUIDE

Where Phase 89 Full-Stack Optimizations Deliver Immediate ROI vs Where Hard Constraints Apply

✅ Good Fit (Immediate Adoption)

  • Multi-Token Paged Decode & Speculative Tree Verification: PrimTS `Keeps Q128` (`5.6x` on GB300) and 2-CTA FP8 page-window prefetch (`2.40x` at `page=16, B=128`).
  • 64-Head Long-Context MLA Training on Blackwell: FA4 `gather_bwd_recompute_p=True` slashes peak HBM by `66.75%` (`41.2 → 13.7 GiB`) at `64k` tokens.
  • Multi-Node RL Rollout on vLLM ≥ 0.28: Omitting `data_parallel_start_rank` on `node_rank == 0` unblocks 128-node GB200 GRPO clusters.
  • Disaggregated KV & EP Fabrics: NIXL EP barrier Put-completion polling (`5/5` GB200 proxy pass), EFA unsolicited write recv disable, Mooncake Tiled COO resharding, and Dynamo `OnceLock` CRTC leaves.

⛔ Not For (Architectural Boundaries)

  • Non-Divisible Page Sizes in 2-CTA Dense MLA: `pages_per_k_tile` must divide `32` (`page_size ∈ {16, 32, 64, 128}` for 128-token K tiles).
  • Padded Q-Head Counts in FA4 Recompute-P: `num_head ∈ {24, 96}` still require `load-P` until padded `lse_log2` (`+inf`) and TMA rope views land.
  • Non-Portable Cluster Splits on SM107: `split_k ∈ {12, 16}` are `1.3x–11x` slower than `split_k ≤ 8` and must not be enabled.
  • All-to-All / EPLB on ROCm Monolithic W4A16: AITER W4A16 EP currently supports naive EP (`expert_map` masking) without EPLB or DP all-to-all.
