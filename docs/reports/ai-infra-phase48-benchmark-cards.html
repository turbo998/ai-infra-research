7 / 7 · MY TAKEGOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Production Adoption Guide for Phase 48 Upstream Kernel, Runtime, and Training Primitives

✓ Good Fit For

1. Rubin (sm_107) Qwen 3.5 Deployments: Single-op BF16 prefill/extend/decode via vendored FROST CuTe-DSL with zero cudnn library dependency.

2. Blackwell (sm_100a / sm_103a) Vendorable Kernels: Consuming prebuilt Cake DeepGEMM / MLA KV pack / NVFP4 attention .so artifacts without importing flashinfer.

3. 128K Long-Context Training on H100/Blackwell: Native-dtype chunked-loss gradient accumulation in TorchTitan saving >1 GiB HBM per rank.

× Not For

1. Causal or Groupwise NVFP4 Attention on sm_100a: cake_nvfp4_attention currently ships only the non-causal persistent-tile variant on sm_103a.

2. Multimodal Chunked Embeddings in vLLM: Chunked pooling splits visual token spans and is explicitly rejected with VLLMValidationError.

3. Slashes in NIXL DOCA Source IDs: Any '/' in NIXL_TELEMETRY_DOCA_SOURCE_ID fails exporter init by design.
