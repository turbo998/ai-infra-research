7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Production Adoption Guidance: When to Enable PDL, `bwd_grid_mult`, MXFP8 L3 & Batch Invariance

✅ Good fit

• Enabling `enable_pdl=True` on B200/GB200/B300/GB300 when QKV GEMM precedes fused RoPE+KV append.
• Setting `bwd_grid_mult=2` or `4` when Causal Conv1D backward shares GPU SMs with comm/side streams.
• Persisting MXFP8 KV pages (`FP8 + UE8M0`) in L3 HiCache (`1.9394x` smaller than BF16).
• Enabling Mooncake `MOONCAKE_ETCD_TLS_*` mTLS and GIL-free `registerMemory`.
⛔ Not for

• Allocating default Cake mHC workspaces lazily inside `torch.cuda.graph` capture without warmup.
• Pairing NIXL KV regions by positional index on heterogeneous PP or Hybrid Memory/Attention (HMA) models.
• Using `bwd_grid_mult > 1` on isolated single-stream training where Wave-1 already fills all SMs (-3% to -6% overhead).
