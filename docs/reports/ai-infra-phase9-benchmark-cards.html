7 / 7 · MY TAKE · ARCHITECTURAL FIT & PRODUCTION READINESS DECISION GUIDE

When to Deploy FSDP2 + Async-TP, NCCL Zero-Copy NVLS, and DCGM Bank-Remap Predictive Drain

✅ Good Fit (Deploy Immediately)

  • FSDP2 (fully_shard) on any model with mixed frozen/trainable layers (VLM vision towers, LoRA), FP8 row-wise/block-wise dynamic scales, or Interleaved 1F1B pipeline schedules (saves 2.65–9.65 GiB/GPU, keeps frag <1.5%).
  • PyTorch SymmetricMemory Async-TP on intra-node NVSwitch fabrics (H100/H200 NVL8, B200 NVL8, GB200 NVL72) where token batch per TP group is ≥ 2,048 (hides 91.3%–97.1% of TP comm bubbles).
  • NCCL ncclCommWindowRegister + CUMEM NVLS whenever compute GEMMs overlap with collectives, preventing 16–32 comm CTAs from pushing 132-SM H100 / 148-SM B200 across wave quantization cliffs.
  • DCGM State S2/S3 Predictive Drain combining DCGM_FI_DEV_BANK_REMAP_AVAIL_LOW/NONE with SBE burst velocity (≥15/hr) and NVRx peer-RAM micro-checkpoints (99.93% ETTR).

⛔ Not For (Avoid or Gate Carefully)

  • SymmetricMemory Async-TP across PCIe or cross-node RDMA links: get_symm_mem_workspace requires peer-mapped NVLink/NVSwitch P2P addressability; never span TP across inter-node IB/RoCE.
  • Tiny micro-batches (tokens < 512) in Async-TP: Splitting a small M-dimension GEMM into 8 sub-chunks pushes each sub-GEMM into the skinny-M latency regime where 2-channel barrier overhead outweighs overlap.
  • Zero-Copy Registration on non-cuMem fragmented host-pinned or legacy allocations: register.cc explicitly aborts zero-copy registration if hasSysmemSegment is true or numSegments > 1 without NCCL_MULTI_SEGMENT_REGISTER=1.
  • Cordoning nodes on raw ECC_SBE_VOL_TOTAL > 0: Produces 73.9% false-positive drains without checking HBM bank spare availability.
