7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS ARCHITECT DECISION GUIDE

Production Adoption Guide: Where Phase 12 Mechanisms Win vs Where They Do Not Apply

✓ Good Fit (Adopt Immediately)

  • PD Disaggregated EAGLE-3 / MTP (`L ≥ 4K`): Always run `_draft_extend_for_prefill` on Prefill nodes and transfer `1L` Draft KV (`+1.25%`) + `CaptureHiddenMode.LAST` seed (`8.62 KiB/rank`), remapping DSA physical slots on Decode before entering CUDA Graphs.
  • Mega-Cluster Synchronous Training (`≥ 1,024 GPUs`): Deploy `Megatron StragglerDetector` + `NCCL Profiler v5` (`ncclProfileKernelCh` `pTimer`) dual-sided telemetry to isolate thermal (`900 MHz`) and optical FEC (`3.0x`) grey stragglers in `3.70s` and swap hot spares in `4.20s` (`98.83%` 30-day Goodput).
  • Multi-Turn Agentic Serving (`32K–256K` tokens, `5s–120s` pause): Pair `LMCache` + `Mooncake` + `GdsBackend` (`cuFile`) across HBM3e, pinned DDR5, CXL/RDMA, and NVMe RAID0 (`18.91x` lower P99 TTFT, `-66.6%` token cost).

&⛔; Not For (Avoid / Anti-Patterns)

  • Shipping Full-Sequence BF16 Hidden States Over RDMA: Inflates PD RDMA traffic by `+30.0%` (`768 MiB/rank` at `128K`) while still stalling Decode batches with draft-extend compute.
  • Single-Metric NCCL Wait-Time Alerting: Using raw `ncclKernel_AllReduce` duration without subtracting peer arrival skew blames all `16,383` victim ranks instead of the `1` slow straggler.
  • Non-GDS POSIX Buffered Disk Offloading for Short Chunks (`< 7K` tokens): Without `cuFile` DMA (`9.5 GB/s` + OS page-cache copy), disk swap-in is slower than H100 FP8 recompute below $L_{\text{breakeven}} = 6,940$ tokens.
