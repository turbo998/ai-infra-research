3 Architecture Decisions Cut Norm+Collective Latency to 110.92 us and Save 190.62 GiB of 128K MLA KV Cache

三大核心架构决策：单核 GIN 融合降至 110.92 us，128K 稀疏 MLA 节省 190.62 GiB 显存
Architect Prompt / 架构师问题: "When scaling DeepSeek-V3/V4 class models across 8x8 Blackwell nodes with 128K contexts, how do we eliminate host launch barriers in ReduceScatter+RMSNorm+AllGather, prevent SM100 232,448 B SMEM overflow in fused MoE/mHC, and fit `32 x 128K` MLA KV caches on 8 GPUs?"
choice Decision 1: Fused RMSNormGIN + Gray-Code RailRing_LsaSTMC vs 3-Kernel Host Pipeline
Act automatically Confidence: 99.3%

ncclGin_WeakSignalInc fuses intra-node TMA SMem reduction and inter-node RDMA puts with RMSNorm in a single kernel, cutting 64 MiB latency from 234.57 us to 110.92 us (2.11x) and HBM traffic from 80.0 MiB to 64.0 MiB (-20.0%). Gray-code step index g^(g>>1) = [0,1,3,2,4,5,7,6] spreads all 8 rail readers across 8 distinct 256 KiB banks.

score Decision 2: SM100 MegaMoE 1-Sigma Tile & Asymmetric 24/8 Store Block + MegaMHC
Act automatically Confidence: 99.0%

Sizing block_m to E + sqrt(E) tokens with asymmetric store_block_m_l1=24, store_block_m_l2=8 shrinks smem_cd at block_m=240 from 20,480 B to 6,144 B (saving 14,336 B inside SM100's 232,448 B SMEM). Fused sm100_mega_mhc (dynamic_split_k=14) cuts HBM traffic by 40.09% (984.38 → 589.75 MiB, 1.92x speedup).

choice Decision 3: SM100 nvfp4_ds_mla (352B) + HiSparse Hot Cache + SparseMLAIndexGroup
Act automatically Confidence: 98.8%

Compresses per-token KV from 1,152 B (274.50 GiB) to 352 B (83.88 GiB, 3.273x) on SM100 or 528 B (125.81 GiB, 2.182x) on SM90/SM100. HiSparse hot buffer (2*topk=4,096 rows, 26,148 B SMEM) cuts 128K GPU HBM residency by 12.8x (7.8125%), while _convert_once eliminates 60/61 redundant index conversions (61x).

7 / 7 · MY TAKE · ARCHITECTURAL FIT & PRODUCTION READINESS BILINGUAL ARCHITECT GUIDANCE

When to Deploy GIN Symmetric Fusion, SM100 MegaMoE/MHC & Sparse MLA — and When Not To

架构师选型指南：何时启用 GIN 对称融合、SM100 MegaMoE/MHC 与稀疏 MLA，以及禁用边界

✅ Good Fit / 适用场景

  • Latency-Critical TP+DP Boundaries (`16–128 MiB`): Fusing ReduceScatter + RMSNorm + AllGather into a single ncclGin kernel removes host stream barriers and saves 2.11x latency and 20% HBM traffic.
  • Rail-Optimized Multi-Node AllGather: AllGather_RailRing_LsaSTMC with Gray-code step indexing (g^(g>>1)) eliminates step-0 bank contention across 8 NVLink peers.
  • Blackwell SM100 Fine-Grained MoE & mHC: DeepGEMM MegaMoE (1-sigma tile + 24/8 store blocks) and MegaMHC cut HyperConnection HBM traffic by 40.09%.
  • 128K+ Long-Context Sparse MLA & Hybrid GDN: Combining nvfp4_ds_mla (352B) or mxfp8_ds_mla (528B) with HiSparse hot caching (12.8x offload) and SparseMLAIndexGroup (61x dedup).

⛔ Not For / 不适用场景与反模式

  • Dynamic Unregistered Heap Tensors: ncclGin requires symmetric window registration (ncclCommWindowRegister); dynamic per-step heap allocations cannot use zero-host-launch GIN puts.
  • Unchunked Large-Query Prefills in HiSparse: Setting sub-chunk q > 8 for topk=2048 pushes coordinator SMEM past the 99 KiB limit; always bound chunked prefill sub-chunks to q ≤ 4 (89,892 B).
  • 576B TMA Loads Without 64B Over-Read Padding: Using fp8_ds_mla (584B) without 64-byte tail padding triggers out-of-bounds TMA faults when loading the 8B FP32 scale as a 64B chunk.
  • Pre-Blackwell (SM80/SM90) Deployment of MegaMoE/nvfp4_ds_mla: Both rely on SM100 UMMA/TMEM or native NVFP4 tensor instructions.
