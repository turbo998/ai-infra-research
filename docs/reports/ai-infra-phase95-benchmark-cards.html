2 / 7 · SCENARIO: BLACKWELL KERNELS & GPUNETIO RDMA LOCKING 3 STACKED ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS

Producer-Tail mbarrier Draining, Explicit K-Atom Unrolling & Immediate SQ Lock Release Eliminate Races and 8x–13x Bottlenecks

Input Scenario: Concurrent `cta_group::2` All-Gather Matmul on SM100, SM120 register-A (`is_gemm_rs`) block-scaled NVFP4 GEMM with $K > 64$, and 8-QP DOCA GPUNetIO RDMA ring submission.
1. Cake All-Gather Matmul 6-Stage Producer Tail (PR #6885)
choiceAct automatically (6/6 stages drained)

Leader CTA multicast `tcgen05.commit` signals both CTAs' `mma_done` mbarriers. Waiting on all 6 ring stages after the tile loop reduces in-flight arrives on exited peer CTA shared memory from `6` to `0` across all `7` CTA-pair programs.

2. TileLang SM120 Register-A K-Atom Unroll & Scale Selector (PR #3286)
scoreAct automatically (8.0x fewer SMEM loads)

`T.unroll(explicit=True)` eliminates local-memory spills across multi-atom `K` (`128/192/256`), while loading $\lceil 2/2 \rceil = 1$ SFA word + $\lceil 8/4 \rceil = 2$ SFB words per lane (`3` total) replaces `24` shared-memory loads per K-atom (`12.5%` residual traffic).

3. NIXL GPUNetIO Immediate SQ Lock Release (PR #2222)
scoreAct automatically (13.33x lock reduction)

Releasing the SDK GPU lock immediately after SQ slot reservation (`0.15 us`) prior to WQE preparation/submission (`1.85 us`) cuts critical-section duration from `2.00 us` to `0.15 us` (`92.5%` reduction).

Verification Invariant: Every bar width above is derived directly from `experiments/*_phase95/summary.json` (`6/6 = 100%`, `(24-3)/24 = 87.5%`, `(2.00-0.15)/2.00 = 92.5%`).
