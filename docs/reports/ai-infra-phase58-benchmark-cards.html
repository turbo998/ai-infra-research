7 / 7 · MY TAKEARCHITECT FIT & PRODUCTION READINESS

When to Adopt Phase 58 Kernel, KV Cache & Training Optimizations

✅ Good Fit

• Hopper Sparse Long-Context Serving: Enable `qsa_sparse_paged_attention(backend="cake")` on NVFP4 KV caches for `1.34x–1.86x` speedup.
• Long-Context Quality Retention: Use TRT-LLM `dtype: mixed_nvfp4_bfloat16` (`first_n=256, last_n=256`) to cut 32K KV memory by `70.75%` while keeping sink/local tokens in BF16.
• Large-Vocab MTP Pretraining: Turn on `mtp_loss_early_backward=True` in Megatron-LM to cut `K=3` live head memory by `73.83%` (`16.0 -> 4.19 GiB`) with zero FLOP overhead.
• Multi-Tenant KV Store: Use Mooncake `/api/v1/soft_pin` (`7d` max TTL) and `DeferDeletion` for lease-safe DFS eviction.

⛔ Not For

• Unaligned Frost MoE Shapes: `NVFP4xNVFP4` Frost low-latency MoE requires `H % 512 == 0`, `I % 128 == 0`, and `top_k <= 32` (`T <= 8`).
• Sliding-Window or Cross-Shared KV Layers: TRT-LLM mixed-precision NVFP4 KV leaves sliding-window and shared-KV layers in native precision.
• Non-Cache-Only RoPE Beyond Draft Limit on vLLM V2: `yarn`/`deepseek`/`longrope` drafters clamp engine `max_model_len` to `draft_max_model_len` on MRV2.
