7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECT'S VERDICT & READINESS

Combine Intra-Node NVLink Ulysses with Inter-Node ZigZag Ring, Precompile XGrammar Bitmasks, and Enforce NUMA-Pinned SAC Offload

Production architectural guidelines for solution architects designing 1M+ context training clusters and structured agentic inference fleets.

✅ Good Fit (Deploy in Production)

  • 2D USP (Ulysses <= H_kv × ZigZag Ring) for 256K–4M long-context training across multi-node NVLink + RoCE/IB clusters (98.54%–99.76% attention efficiency).
  • Per-document THDCPLayoutPlan ZigZag splitting when variable-length packed sequences (cu_seqlens) are trained under Context Parallelism.
  • XGrammar + vLLM V1 async bitmask overlap for high-concurrency JSON Schema / CFG tool-calling agents (37.7–93.8 µs exposed overhead across BS=32..128).
  • Operator-knapsack SAC + NUMA-bound pinned PCIe Gen5 / NVLink-C2C offloading to save 86.94% activation HBM at +2.53% step cost.

⛔ Not For (Architectural Anti-Patterns)

  • Setting SP_ulysses > H_kv across inter-node links: forces U / H_kv KV head replication and puts multi-hop RoCE All-to-All on the critical path.
  • Contiguous 1D Ring-Attention on causal LLMs: wastes nearly half (46.67%–49.22%) of attention FLOPs waiting on rank R-1.
  • Synchronous per-token regex/FSM or full FP32 logit masks at BS >= 32: inflates TPOT by +162.7%..+426.2%.
  • CPU activation offloading without AffinityMode.NODE NUMA binding or pinned memory: turns PCIe transfers into an 8.0 s step bottleneck.

Architect's Quantitative Checklist (Phase 10 Formulas)

1. ZigZag Work Invariant: W(r) = [(r + 0.5) + (2R - 1 - r + 0.5)] * (S / 2R)^2 = 2R * L_sub^2 (0% bubble)
2. Compact Bitmask Bytes: Bytes(B, V) = B * ceil(V / 32) * 4 = B * 16,032 B (32.0x smaller than FP32)
3. Knapsack SAC Ratio: eta_op = Bytes_Saved / Recompute_FLOPs (Recompute Pointwise > 3e8 B/GFLOP; Offload GEMM)
