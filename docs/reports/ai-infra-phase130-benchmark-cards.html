"In `GroupedQueryAttentionDecodePaged`, TMA K prefetches tile `s+1` before waiting on `tma_order_v_nbar` while TMA V reads `s - prefetch_iters - 1 .. s` (`prefetch_iters + 3 = 5` live slots). With `pt_stages = 4`, V for tile `s` loads pages of tile `s + 4`." — flashinfer@3c7e075 (#6299)

choice Act automatically · pt_stages = 5

7 / 7   MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS ARCHITECT DECISION GUIDE

When to Adopt Phase 130 Kernel, Serving & Disaggregated KV Optimizations

✅ Good fit
1. Blackwell MNNVL Multi-Stream Decode (`GLM-5.3-Flash` TP8): Fusing one-shot MNNVL all-reduce with 4-stream mHC post-map & 24-logit pre-norm partials (`allreduce_mhc_post_prenorm`) eliminates 89 kernel launches per decode step at `B=1..32`.
2. GB300 (`sm_103a`) Training & Context Prefill: Use 8-warp CTA programs (`w8rs`/`w8r`) for `4096->512`, `5120->512`, and `kv_a 6144->576` input gradients (`1.040x–1.050x`) and 4-stage FP8 K/V ring for paged context FMHA (`1.081x`).
3. CuTe Paged GQA Multi-Token Decode & vLLM MRV2 DBO: Enforce `pt_stages = 5` (`>= prefetch_iters + 3`) to prevent `s+4` V-page races, and clamp DBO token thresholds to `>= 2 * (1 + K_spec)` when pairing DBO with speculative decoding and DCP.
4. ROCm Mooncake PD & Batched Eigensolvers: Keep K and V in separate allocations on HIP (`not _is_hip`) and route `rocSOLVER` `syevd` workspaces through `getCUDADeviceAllocator()`.
⛔ Not for
1. B200 (`sm_100a`) 4-Stage FP8 Context FMHA: Do not force the GB300 4-stage K/V ring onto `sm_100a` B200; B200 retains the 3-stage ring where shared-memory/register balance is optimal.
2. SGLang Unified Memory + INT8 Mamba Checkpoints: Never combine `--enable-int8-mamba-checkpoint` with `--enable-unified-memory` or `--enable-lmcache`—the unified pool has no int8 Mamba checkpoint buffer (`#46972`).
3. Registering Slices of Pre-Registered RDMA Pools: Do not register slices of Store-RS's local scratch pool with Mooncake TransferEngine (`ERR_ADDRESS_OVERLAPPED`); allocate a dedicated registered region for zero-copy buffers (`#4899`).
4. Requiring Envoy `send_body_without_waiting_for_header_response` in Dynamo EPP: Unnecessary because EPP returns `RequestHeaders` only after full body receipt (`#17125`).
