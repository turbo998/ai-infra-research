Why Contextual BM25 Beats Dense Alone

Dense vectors compress 512 tokens into a single point, blurring exact alphanumeric codes like SKU-9941-B vs SKU-9941-C. Prepending the context header into BM25 combines exact token matching with document-level scope.

Eliminating Temporal Contamination

Annual 10-K reports share nearly identical boilerplate sentences across FY23, FY24, and FY25. Stamping [Doc: ACME FY2026 10-K] into every chunk eliminates cross-year retrieval mix-ups.

Robustness Verdict: S5 resolves both semantic coreference ambiguity (+34.4 pts) and lexical identifier precision (+43.2 pts) simultaneously.

Why Full ColBERT Saturates at High QPS

Searching 5.12 Billion compressed token vectors requires multi-step centroid pruning, inverted list gathering, and 2-bit residual decompression across `564 GB` of DRAM, creating severe memory-bandwidth contention above 300 QPS.

MY TAKE 7 / 7

Architect's Verdict: Combine Prefix-Cached Context Headers with Stage-2 MaxSim; Use Late Chunking for $0-LLM Streams

Production decision guide for choosing between Late Chunking (S2) and Prefix-Cached Contextual Retrieval + Reranking (S5).

✅ Good Fit (When to Adopt)

  • Enterprise Financial, Legal & Technical RAG (Use S5): Cuts Top-20 retrieval failures by 6.22x (22.4% → 3.6%) and wrong-year hallucinations by 20.4x.
  • High-Throughput Zero-LLM Ingestion (Use S2 Late Chunking): Halves retrieval failures (22.4% → 10.6%) at $0 LLM token cost by pooling after 16K self-attention.
  • Mixed Exact Code/SKU + Semantic Search: Contextual BM25 + Stage-2 MaxSim hits 97.2% exact match accuracy in 34.2 GB RAM.

⛔ Not For (When to Avoid)

  • Uncached / Shuffled LLM Ingestion (Avoid S4): Never generate chunk context headers without KV Prefix Caching ($148k cost trap).
  • Full-Corpus Multi-Vector Indexing on >5M Chunks (Avoid S3): 564 GB RAM footprint is 16.5x larger than Stage-2 reranking with lower accuracy on coreferences.
  • Short Self-Contained FAQ Snippets (<200 tok): Standard hybrid search is sufficient when no cross-chunk context exists.

Pre-Flight Production Readiness Checklist

1. Keep <document> strictly at prompt start.  |  2. Route same-doc chunks sequentially to the same worker.  |  3. Index context headers into BOTH BM25 and Dense stores.
