MY TAKE · ARCHITECTURAL FIT, ANTI-PATTERNS & PRODUCTION READINESS 7 / 7

Deploy FlashMLA + Ring CP, CuMem Sleep L2, and PPCIE; Avoid Pure Ulysses on MQA and Multi-GPU TP Under Single-GPU CC-On

✅ Good Fit (Production-Ready Patterns)
  • 1M-Context MLA Models (`h_kv=1`): Pair FlashMLA V4.1 FP8/FP4 (`528B`/`288B`) + DSA (`topk=2048`) with Ring Attention CP (`1,008 MiB/layer`, fully overlapped) or Hybrid Intra-Node Ulysses + Inter-Node Ring.
  • Hybrid Mamba-Transformer Prefix Caching: Use SGLang MambaCheckpointPool with symmetric int8 storage (`1.8832x` slot density) and checkpoint interval 2,048..8,192 tokens for multi-turn Agentic workloads.
  • Co-Resident 80 GiB RL Post-Training: Combine vLLM CuMemAllocator Sleep Level 2 (`0.038 s` unmap/remap, `58.5 GiB` peak HBM) with verl BucketedWeightSender (`512 MiB` CUDA IPC, `0.0835 s`).
  • Disaggregated RL & Long-CoT Rollout: Enable multi_sender=True NVLink relay (`3.98 s`) or DeltaShardedCheckpointEngine (`2.06 s`, `1,120 MiB` bound) with staleness-bounded async queues (`8.5%` bubble).
  • Multi-GPU Confidential AI (`TP ≥ 2`): Enforce 8-GPU + 4-NVSwitch PPCIE with local RIM/OCSP caching (`11.68 ms` TPOT, `99.89%` parity).
⛔ Not For (Known Anti-Patterns & Traps)
  • Pure Ulysses CP on MLA/MQA (`CP > h_kv`): Forces 8x KV head replication across ranks, inflating per-layer All-to-All volume to 7,616 MiB on the critical path.
  • `expandable_segments:True` with vLLM `CuMemAllocator` or RDMA Master: Breaks `CuMemAllocator` assertions (`cumem.py:144`) and causes NCCL RDMA registration failures unless Master allocates via `cupy.zeros`.
  • Byte-Threshold Flush in FSDP/MoE Delta Sync: Flushing delta queues by local byte count deadlocks NCCL collectives across skewed ranks; always flush by entry count (`_GatherQueue`).
  • Multi-GPU TP under Single-GPU `CC-On` Mode: Disables direct NVLink P2P, forcing every AllReduce through CPU bounce buffers (`4.2..9.2 GB/s`) and degrading TPOT by `1.62x..2.37x`.
  • New Deployments on `nv-local-gpu-verifier`: Python verifier reaches End-of-Support on 2026-09-15 (`cc_admin.py:84`); migrate to C++ `NVIDIA/attestation-sdk`.
