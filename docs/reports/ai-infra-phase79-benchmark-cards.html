7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS PRODUCTION ARCHITECTURE GUIDANCE

Architectural Guidance: Adopt Zero-Sync Shape Dispatch & Suffix Checksums; Guard Register & Fence Invariants

✅ Good Fit (Recommended Adoption)

  • Sparse MoE MXFP8 QuantizationUse `row-tiled` when $B\cdot M \ge 16,384$ (`1.41x–1.94x` speedup, zero host sync).
  • Rubin R200 (`sm_107a`) QSADeploy `cake_qsa_nvfp4` split `tcgen05` decode + merge on all hosts (`1.33x–2.76x`).
  • Disagg Decode Radix CacheEnable `--disaggregation-enable-kv-checksum` with suffix `[start_idx, end_idx)` digest.
  • GLM-5.3 / DeepSeek-V3.2 MLAEnable `skip_scale=True` when `mla_k_scale == 1.0` (`1.2%–3.0%` E2E TPOT win).
  • Multi-Thread NIXL StorageRely on `scopedCudaDevice` for worker threads without an active `CUcontext`.

⛔ Not For (Avoid / Boundary Cases)

  • Dense Full-Mask Large MXFP8Do not expect speedup from `row-tiled` at 100% mask density (`0.973x–0.990x` BW bound).
  • Fused Cooperative QSA on R200Do not use cooperative fused QSA on `sm_107a`; split form is faster at every row count.
  • GQA-Ratio-Only MLA CachingNever key MLA kernels solely on `nheads_per_kv`; `(64,1)` and `(128,2)` will collide.
  • Uncapped `setmaxnreg.dec`Never emit `setmaxnreg.dec` above `65536 / threads_per_cta` (`128` at 512 threads).
  • LoRA + MegaMoE in vLLMIncompatible backend combination; explicitly rejected at engine validation (`#63496`).
