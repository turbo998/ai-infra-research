7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECTURAL FIT & READINESS

Production Adoption Guide: When to Enable Each Phase 76 Optimization

Clear architectural boundaries separating immediate production wins from workload-specific or hardware-scoped paths.

✅ Good Fit (Immediate Production Value)

16×GB300 NVL72 EP16 MoE: Single fence.acq_rel.sys + relaxed atomics & per-warp 6 KiB row copy yield 1.13x–1.18x with bitwise-identical output.
Kimi-K3 TP12 Tail (M=256..960): Routing medium batches through k3_ess:grouped gives 1.06x–1.08x on GB200/GB300 with zero kernel changes.
SM120 Inference & CUDA Graphs: Device-side FP64→FP32 alpha removes 2 host syncs/call (~60x smaller launch gap) and unblocks dynamic scales in CUDA Graphs.
Multi-Instance Shared-FS KV Offload & Paged Experts: vLLM idempotent EEXIST handling and SGLang 12-format 16B/4B/2B paged experts eliminate production crashes.
RL MoE LoRA Sync (veRL + vLLM): Single-block pinned CPU staging cuts 16,000-tensor bucket receive latency by 5.4x (705 → 131 ms).

⛔ Not For (Known Boundaries & Limits)

Non-SM103 or Unaligned NVFP4 Attention: cake_nvfp4_attention ships exclusively for sm_103a, noncausal, D=128, and S % 512 == 0.
Large-Batch GLM-5.2 Dense MLP (M > 8): glmDenseMlpSmallM is specialized for weight-streaming M ≤ 8 decode; M > 8 should use standard Tensor Core GEMMs.
Unchecked Ragged MQA Backward Packing: Enabling pack_gqa backward without critical-path gating serializes long causal sequences in ragged packs (3946 vs 2297 us).
Undersized Host Mamba Pools in HiCache: Without watching sglang:hicache_host_mamba_used_states, host Mamba LRU eviction silently invalidates cached host KV prefixes.
5D Wrap-Around TMA on Unmapped Tails: Never use unbounded wrap-around TMA descriptors near allocation boundaries (~128 KiB hardware over-prefetch).
