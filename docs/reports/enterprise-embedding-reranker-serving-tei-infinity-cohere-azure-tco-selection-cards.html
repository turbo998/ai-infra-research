Why Flattening 50 Variable-Length Chunks into a 1D Stream Multiplies Throughput 6.8x

One 512-token chunk in a 50-chunk batch forces 49 short 180-token chunks to be padded to 512. Concatenating all 50 chunks into a 1D unpadded tensor (`[1, 9332]`) eliminates every wasted zero multiplication.
