7 / 7 MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & PRODUCTION READINESS

Adopt Swap-AB Decode BMM, Long-Context NVFP4 Attention & NVLink-Bounded EP=16 RL Topologies

Practical architectural guidance synthesized from Phase 87 source audits and deterministic modeling.

✅ Good Fit (When to Adopt)

  • Blackwell Small-Batch Decode BMM: Route $K=1024, M \in [8,33], \text{batch} \in [1,4]$ `bmm_bf16` calls to `tcgen05` Swap-AB (`1.03x–1.22x` on GB300).
  • Long-Sequence Dense MHA ($S \ge 32\text{K}$): Use Cake NVFP4 Attention on B200/GB300 (`1.03x–1.08x` vs FA4 BF16) and across all lengths on RTX PRO 6000 (`sm_120a`, `1.41x–1.84x` vs cuDNN SDPA).
  • Hybrid Mamba + DCP Serving: Align chunked prefill truncation via $\operatorname{lcm}(\text{align}, G_{\text{mamba}})$ so every intermediate cut donates DCP checkpoints.
  • GB300 Large MoE RL Training: Pair `EP=16` (within a 4-host NVLink domain) with `CP=4` Ulysses attention to shrink expert FSDP groups (`64 -> 8`) and halve trainer GPU count (`256 -> 128`).

⛔ Not For (Where to Avoid)

  • Short-Sequence NVFP4 Prefill ($S \le 4,800$) on B200/GB300: On-GPU statistics + smoothing/quantization kernels outweigh Tensor Core savings (`0.72x/0.70x` vs FA4 BF16).
  • Unchecked 32-Bit Varlen/Ulysses Indexing: Never launch SM120 varlen attention above `299,520` padded tokens (at 56 heads) or Ulysses unscatter without `int32` product guards.
  • MegaMoE Kernels with Active LoRA Adapters: `deep_gemm_mega_moe` and `aiter_mega_moe` do not apply LoRA deltas and must be rejected at config validation (`vllm#63496`).
  • Cross-Rack Expert FSDP (`EP=4, FSDP=64`): Cross-rack BF16 All-Gather and FP32 Reduce-Scatter on routed experts dominate step time.
