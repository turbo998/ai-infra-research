7 / 7 · MY TAKEGOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Production Adoption Guidance for Phase 49 Kernel, Serving & RL Training Upgrades

Actionable architectural recommendations synthesized from the 20 upstream commits studied in Phase 49.

✅ Good Fit (推荐适用场景)

  • Low-Batch MoE Decode (T ≤ 8) on Blackwell/Rubin: Enable frost_low_latency (`#6626`) to autotune TC128/TC256 + 16 FMA warp-width pairs on a single shared MXFP4/MXFP8 weight layout.
  • Deterministic Training RMSNorm (`H=1536, 4096`): Use cake_rmsnorm_train (`#6939`) row-window backward with fixed-order tree `dweight` reduction and self-resetting flip barriers.
  • Disaggregated Chat Serving with Tool Calls: Use vLLM `/v1/generate` with output_mode="derender" and `ParseContext` (`#60082`) plus Dynamo's `` chunk splitter (`#16965`).
  • Multi-Stage RL Post-Training & FSDP MoE: Combine TorchTitan's `loss_kwarg_mbs` PP support (`#3980`) and TE JAX's FSDP gated-shard SwiGLU layout fix (`#3638`).

⛔ Not For (不适用或受限场景)

  • High-Token Prefill MoE (T > 8) on Frost FMA: `FrostLowLatencyConfig` enforces `1 ≤ max_num_tokens ≤ 8` and `top-k ≤ 32` for FMA tactics; large prefill batches should stay on grouped GEMM / CuteDSL tiles.
  • Streaming `/v1/generate` with `output_mode="derender"`: Streaming derender (`stream=True`) and Rust-frontend `output_mode` are explicitly rejected (`400 Bad Request`) in `vllm#60082`.
  • TorchTitan RL with Context Parallelism or GraphRuntime PP Per-MB Loss: Context Parallelism (`cp > 1`) and RDMA microbatch prefetching with PP remain gated off in `torchtitan#3980`.
  • Uncapped Remote/Base64 Video Payloads (> 200 MiB): TRT-LLM (`#19920`) enforces a strict 200 MiB (`_MAX_RESPONSE_BYTES`) cap across both offline and online video loaders.
