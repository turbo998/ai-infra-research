7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & PRODUCTION READINESS ARCHITECTURAL GUIDANCE · PHASE 54 SYNTHESIS

Adopt Multi-Wave Decode & Watermark Prefetch for Skewed Serving; Keep Single-Wave for Short Uniform Batches

架构师选型建议：明确 Phase 54 各项内核、推理调度与 KV 传输优化的最佳适用场景（Good Fit）与不适用边界（Not For）。

✅ Good Fit (推荐采用场景)

  • SM120 MegaMoE + CUDA 13.2–13.4: Mandatory when compiling nccl_device.h GIN kernels near the 168-register limit across EP 4–32 and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (E384).
  • Skewed / Long-KV GQA Decode (`B=25..148` on B200): Enable FLASHINFER_TRTLLM_GEN_MULTI_WAVE_KV_SPLIT=1 when single-wave splitting drops to <= 2 splits and tail latency is dominated by long requests.
  • DeepSeek-V4.1 Long-Window & Async RadixCache: Always precompute WindowLayout.history_src, cache index-k slots per ratio, and fence UnifiedCacheLinker behind forward_stream.
  • LMCache Mixed-Granularity L2 Prefetch & Cross-Layer KV: Use --l2-prefetch-inflight-watermark=0.5 and stride-aware VmmCudaIPCWrapper registration.
  • Hybrid CPU↔GPU Disaggregated Clusters: Enable Mooncake TCP dram_to_gpu on CPU nodes and NIXL 64-bit request/execution telemetry.

⛔ Not For (不适用或需规避场景)

  • Generic Host/Stack Pointers with _CCCL_ATOMIC_UNSAFE_AUTOMATIC_STORAGE: Never define this macro in translation units that apply cuda::atomic_ref to thread-local stack variables.
  • Short Uniform KV Decode (`kv_len <= 1024`) or MLA Decode: Multi-wave KV split adds reduction CTA overhead when KV caches are short and uniform (keep opt-in flag off for short-KV workloads).
  • Active rows > 32,640 in SM120 MegaMoE: Exceeds the 15-bit signed high-halfword ABI limit (0x7FFF); chunk or split larger token batches before launch.
  • Arbitrary Python MoE Simulators on Monolithic Kernels: Monolithic TRTLLM kernels only support static bias injection (LoadBalanced); other simulators must fall back to decomposed MoE.
