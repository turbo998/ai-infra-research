7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECTURAL FIT & READINESS

架构师选型建议：何时启用局部编译图预算、C++ 页聚合传输与有界重放拆分

✅ Good Fit / 推荐采用场景

  • Per-Head Norm & Gate Regional Compile: Models with narrow per-head norms (head_dim=64) where eager ATen achieves <6% bandwidth efficiency; set recompile_limit >= 16 when shared across FP32/BF16 residual streams.
  • Page-Gathered RDMA Batching (_pages): Long-context PD disaggregation with F x P > 10,000 KV descriptors to bypass pybind11 list unboxing.
  • ROCm Bounded-Replay Source-KV Split: Hybrid SWA/full-attention models (DeepSeek-V4.1) on MI300X/MI350X using fused mHC boundaries.

⛔ Not For / 禁用或需规避场景

  • Bitwise-Identical Training Checkpoints: Regional torch.compile reorders reductions and contracts FMAs; do not mix eager and compiled norm regions when bitwise determinism is required.
  • Internal CUTLASS MoE with All-to-All / Unswizzled Scales: Bundled non-OSS MoE builds lack GEMM all-to-all and unswizzled SF support; auto-fallback to AllGather/ReduceScatter or build with USING_OSS_CUTLASS_MOE_GEMM.
  • SM103 Single-Token H=96 FP8 MLA at B < 8: Keep on trtllm_gen below the measured batch floor of 8.
