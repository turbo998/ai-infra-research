7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Architect Verdict: Good Fit vs. Not For & Production Readiness

✅ Good fit:
• **`PostgreSQL + pgvectorscale`**: `<10M–20M` chunks when your app already uses Postgres.
• **`Azure AI Search / OpenSearch`**: `10M–100M` chunks needing turnkey M365/Entra RBAC & semantic ranker.
• **`Milvus / Zilliz` or `Qdrant`**: `50M–1B+` high-QPS multi-tenant vector workloads.
⛔ Not for:
• Applying RBAC security filters in Python *after* fetching global Top-K from HNSW.
• Running dense-only vector search without BM25 lexical fusion on technical/SKU corpora.
