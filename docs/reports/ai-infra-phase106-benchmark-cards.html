7 / 7 · MY TAKE Architectural Fit & Readiness

Production Adoption Guide: Where Phase 106 Optimizations Deliver Immediate ROI

✅ Good Fit

  • Long-Context CP Training (TE): Double-buffered P2P KV rotation + online LSE merge saves 81.25% temp memory at CP=8 and scales as $O(1)$ with context parallelism.
  • Blackwell & Hopper Serving (FlashInfer): 11-tier dynamic SMEM finalize spreading (`3.0x`), Swap-AB M=64 tiles (`1.43x`), and SM90 MSA CuTe DSL kernels (`2.50x`).
  • ROCm MI350X & RDNA Serving (SGLang/vLLM): Fused QK Gemma RMSNorm + RoPE (`2.15x`), calibrated FP8 KV scales (`535.56x` lower MSE), and folded Kimi-K3 `q_b_proj + W_UK` (`1.71x`).
  • RL Rollout & MoE Resharding (Megatron/Mooncake): NCCL UBR grad pool offload (`16 GiB` freed) and shared arena dedup (`322.22x` faster validation).

⛔ Not For

  • Unaligned Output Pointers on Swap-AB M=64: Tensors with out.data_ptr() % 32 != 0 cannot issue 32-byte vector stores and must route to the fallback tile.
  • Ragged Varlen on SM90 MSA Surface: The Hopper MSA serving surface requires paged interleaved FP8 E4M3 KV caches (`topk=16`) and raises on ragged varlen inputs.
  • Blind PDL on Read-First Chain Heads: Launching kernels that read caller `topk_ids` before `griddepcontrol.wait` under PDL causes stale-routing data races.
  • Small GTP Axes (gtp_remat <= 2): All-to-All + local FP32 sum is automatically bypassed when the axis is $\le 2$ or when `--accumulate-allreduce-grads-in-fp32` is already active.
