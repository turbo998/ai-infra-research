7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Good Fit vs Not For & Production Readiness

✅ Good fit
• All PyTorch 2.x Transformer training and inference models using `SDPA`
• Long-context (`4K–128K`) pipelines where `MATH` fallback causes immediate OOM
• Custom attention masks (Tree Attention, Document Packing) via `FlexAttention`
⛔ Not for
• Passing materialized `[B, 1, S, S]` causal/padding masks into `SDPA`
• Leaving `SDPBackend.MATH` enabled on long-context production workloads
CSP self-contained · Zero external assets · Ready for main branch archive.
