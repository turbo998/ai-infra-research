Decision 1 · SM103 `decode_tcgen05_persistent` + One-Tile Merge (`flashinfer@131f85942e`) choice Act automatically · 1.07x–1.15x

Replaces `256` two-wave CTAs (`33` tiles each) with `152` persistent CTAs walking `55–56` tiles (`walk_splits = 2`), while `merge_tcgen05` fuses 4×`16x256` UMMA steps into one `64x256` tile (`rows=1` NHD `11.65 → 10.21 us` 1.14x; `rows=128` HND Graph `72.37 → 67.74 us` 1.07x).

Decision 2 · SM120 `mma.sync` Decode & 188-SM `_SPLIT_OVERRIDES` (`flashinfer@09a77503f0`) score Act automatically · 1.89x Eager / 1.90x Graph

Lowering splits at `rows=4, 8, 16, 32` from `[33, 23, 11, 5]` (`264–368` CTAs) to `[16, 11, 5, 3]` (`128–192` CTAs) keeps 1 CTA/SM on `188` SMs, cutting `rows=4` NHD from `28.59 → 16.13 us` (1.77x) and `rows=128` HND from `320.93 → 169.62 us` (1.89x).

Decision 3 · Mooncake `FI_HMEM_NEURON` + PCIe Switch Affinity (`Mooncake@f88d94d1bc`) noul Act automatically · 231.16 GB/s (18.78x)

Disables libfabric SHM (`0.07 GB/s` uncached BAR `memcpy` → `19.23 GB/s` EFA loopback, 274.7x) and pairs each `/dev/neuron` with switch-local EFA NICs (`2.5 → 19.2 GB/s` per chip, `12.31 → 231.16 GB/s` across 16 chips).

7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS PRODUCTION ARCHITECT RUBRIC

Adopt SM103/SM120 QSA, SGLang NVFP4 KV, and Mooncake Trainium EFA immediately; gate Rubin SM104 small-batch fusion and decode Radix checksums

✅ Good Fit (Deploy Now)

  • Blackwell Datacenter & Workstation Serving: Enable `cake_qsa_nvfp4` (`1.07x–1.15x` on GB300, `1.89x` on 188-SM RTX PRO 6000) and `cake_deepgemm_mega_mhc` (`1.426x` on B200) for DeepSeek/MiniMax/Kimi workloads.
  • 8× SM120 PCIe Gen5 Nodes: Use `IpcVariant.COPY_ENGINE_RING_PIPED` for all-reduce payloads $\ge 32\text{ MiB}$ (`chunk >= 4 MiB`) across dual-socket hosts (`1.111x` at `96 MiB/rank`).
  • Long-Context SGLang Disaggregated Serving: Deploy `minimax_m3_nvfp4` (`72 B/tok/head` KV, `3.56x` compression), 2-CTA `page_row_copy` (`51–52 GB/s`), and `kv_layout_signature` for heterogeneous prefill/decode TP widths.
  • AWS `trn2.48xlarge` & `p5en.48xlarge` PD Clusters: Upgrade Mooncake for headerless `FI_HMEM_NEURON` (`231.16 GB/s`) and `stableHostNicKey` stale-peer eviction (`peer_map_=1`).
  • High-QPS Dynamo + vLLM Fleets: Turn on `--freeze-gc-after-init`, `LOCAL_FLUSH_TOKENS=64` metrics batching, D2D `SeedTransport`, and `ENOSPC` store-failure backpressure.

⛔ Not For (Avoid / Gate Carefully)

  • Persistent-Walk QSA on SM100 (`GB200/B200`): Do not force `decode_tcgen05_persistent` on `sm_100a`—tile coordinate division overhead slows `rows=64` by `9–10%`.
  • Fused Indexer Quant+Scatter on Rubin `sm_104` or High Token/Block: Keep `invokeIndexerKCacheQuantAndScatter` disabled when `sm == 104` or `max_Num_Tokens_Per_Block > 8`.
  • Whole-Prompt Disagg KV Checksum + Decode Radix Cache: Never combine `--disaggregation-enable-kv-checksum` with `--disaggregation-decode-enable-radix-cache` (suffix-only transfers falsely fail full-prompt digests).
  • Libfabric SHM on AWS Neuron Hosts: Never leave `FI_EFA_ENABLE_SHM_TRANSFER=1` on Trainium—it crawls at `0.07 GB/s` over uncached PCIe BAR `memcpy` vs `19.23 GB/s` EFA loopback.
  • Async Strided D2H on `torch_mlu 1.26.1`: Do not use `non_blocking=True` when `skip_prefix_n_blocks > 0` leaves a non-contiguous host slice.
