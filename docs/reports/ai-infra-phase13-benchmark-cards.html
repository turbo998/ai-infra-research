7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECT DECISION GUIDE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & PRODUCTION READINESS

Architect's Verdict: When to Lock Batch-Invariant Kernels, Deploy RAS DAG Triage & Size NVL144

✅ Good Fit (Deploy Immediately)
  • RL Rollout & Policy Verification (`GRPO`/`PPO`): Enable Batch-Invariant mode (`num_splits` fixed tile, `Split-K=1`) to eliminate off-policy logit drift between rollout servers (`vLLM`/`SGLang`) and training workers at only `+4.2%–8.6%` latency cost.
  • Regulated Financial / Legal / Medical Audit APIs: Guarantee bitwise identical token outputs (`0 ULP`) regardless of concurrent user traffic (`BS=1` vs `BS=128`) or Chunked Prefill split boundaries.
  • `1,024+` GPU Synchronous Training Clusters: Enable `TORCH_FR_BUFFER_SIZE=2000` and `NCCL RAS` daemon (`port 28028`) with automated Wait-For DAG triage to isolate single-node hangs in `< 650 ms` without killing healthy peers.
  • `1T+` Sparse MoE & `1M` Context Serving: Consolidate `EP=64` inside `GB200 NVL72` or `VR200 NVL144` scale-up domains (`$1.23–$1.79 / 1M tokens`) and shift PD disaggregation ratios from `4P:1D` toward `1P:1D`.
⛔ Not For (Avoid / Anti-Patterns)
  • Relying on `torch.use_deterministic_algorithms(True)` Alone for Serving: Standard CUDA determinism only guarantees repeatability at identical batch tensor shapes; it does NOT prevent `BS=1` vs `BS=33` Split-KV/Split-K drift.
  • Unconstrained Creative Chat at `BS=1..4` (`temperature > 0.7`): Where stochastic sampling is desired and ultra-low single-stream latency matters most, disabling Split-K/Split-KV wastes `~8.6%` small-batch speed for unneeded bitwise invariance.
  • Blind Watchdog Job Kill on `WorkNCCL` Timeout: Tearing down all `16,384` GPUs when `16,383` ranks report timeout wastes `15+ minutes` per incident and leaves grey/stalled NIC edges undiagnosed.
  • Deploying `1T MoE (EP=64)` Across Multi-Rack `NVL8` over `400G RoCE`: Incurs `31.20 ms/tok` EP communication overhead (`10.04×` higher token cost than `VR200 NVL144`).
