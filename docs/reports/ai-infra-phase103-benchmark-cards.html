7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECT GUIDANCE & READINESS

When to Deploy Blackwell W4A16 MegaMoE, Speculative Disaggregated Serving, and MFSDP v2 Muon

✅ Good fit

  • Low-batch Blackwell MoE decode & EP8 prefill: Use `sm103_bf16_nvfp4_bf16_cake` (`1.5912x` at `T=1`, `1.0839x` at EP8 `T=4096`) and `mm_bf16_fp4` 8-CTA split-K (`16x` less DSMEM).
  • TP-colocated MoE CUDA Graphs: Enable SGLang DeepEP v2 `_masked_token_capacity` to reclaim up to `1,736 MiB` per graph at small batch sizes.
  • Speculative + Disaggregated PD (Together / Fireworks): Pair DSpark simulated-acceptance bonus fix (#46404) with HiCache load-back early-send gating (#44081).
  • Distributed Muon & Row-Only MXFP8: Use TE row-only MXFP8 casts (`-50%` FP8 writes) and Megatron MFSDP v2 `FsdpMuon` P2P gather/scatter (`8.0x` less comm).

⛔ Not for

  • Unconditional Cake W4A16 across all EP8 batch sizes: At `T=8..512` on EP8, autotuned `sm100_bf16_nvfp4_bf16_cutedsl` is `6–9%` faster—use a shape-aware dispatcher.
  • ROCm `aiter` sparse MLA prefill at 128 heads/rank: Gate prefill at `<= 64` heads/rank and fall back to gather + OPUS above 64 heads.
  • Attention-DP DeepEP v2 slab shrinking: When `attn_dp_size > 1`, remote ranks can route full `max_dispatch` batches; keep full slab capacity.
  • Pure SRAM chips (Groq/Cerebras) for multi-TB long-context KV pools: Use SRAM engines as low-latency draft generators or short-context deterministic tiers, not high-concurrency long-KV stores.
