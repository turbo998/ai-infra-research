7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & PRODUCTION READINESS ARCHITECT DECISION GUIDE

Adopt Streaming Muon, Zero-Copy Mooncake VMM & 2-Phase PD+DP Immediately; Gate Rubin NVFP4 MLA on `Batch ≥ 4`

面向生产推理与训练集群的落地方案：明确哪些场景可直接全量开启，哪些场景需配置回退门禁。

✅ Good Fit (立即采纳场景)

  • Rubin (`sm_107a`) Medium/Large-Batch MTP Decode: `Q=4, B >= 4` with `64K–128K` context achieves `1.09x–1.25x` speedup over TokenSpeed MLA 2 with zero-allocation CUDA Graph replay.
  • SGLang Elastic PD+DP Clusters: Two-phase `prepare_only=True` preflight safely enables online Prefill ↔ Decode switching with `--enable-dp-attention` and zero split-brain teardown risk.
  • TRT-LLM Disaggregated Serving + Mooncake Store: `stage_through_host=False` zero-copy VMM splitting + symmetric ADP `kv_event_allgather` eliminates multi-rank hangs and saves `1,024 MiB` host DRAM per 8-GPU node.
  • Megatron-LM Layer-Wise Muon & TE NVFP4 Overlap: `--muon-ns-batch-size 4` slashes transient FP32 HBM by `5.67x`; TE `cuBLASMp >= 0.11.0` enables zero-sync NVFP4 comm+GEMM overlap when `(N/tp_size) % 128 == 0`.

⛔ Not For (不适用或需门禁场景)

  • Single-Request (`B=1`) Or High-Q (`Q=8, B≥16`) Rubin MLA: Cake is `0.90x–0.94x` slower at `B=1` and `0.987x` geomean on `Q=8`; dispatch `B=1` and `Q>=8` to TokenSpeed MLA 2.
  • SGLang Local Control Broadcast Mode: `--enable-dp-attention-local-control-broadcast` lacks a global preflight barrier across DP ranks and is explicitly rejected for PD role switching.
  • Unaligned NVFP4 TP Shards in `cuBLASMp`: Linear layers with `(N / tp_size) % 128 != 0` cannot use split P2P overlap and silently fall back to `CUBLASMP_MATMUL_ALGO_TYPE_DEFAULT`.
  • Host-Shared / Non-VMM GPU Pointers Without `cuMemCreate` Lookup: Unresolved virtual ranges fall back to whole-span registration (`unresolved_ranges`) and cannot cross physical allocation boundaries.
