7 / 7 MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Deploy `cold-chain:8`, Single-Entry FFI, and Phase-Specific MXFP8 Immediately; Keep Recompute Dual-Plane & Small-BH GDP Guards

Architectural guidance for Solution Architects designing Blackwell/ROCm training and disaggregated serving clusters.

✅ Good Fit (Deploy Now)

  • Sub-10 us Kernel Autotuning (`cold-chain:8`): Essential whenever single-kernel execution (`2.8–6.4 us`) is smaller than or comparable to the CUDA-graph launch floor (`~6.38 us`) and cluster first-launch overhead.
  • Multi-Kernel Low-Latency MoE (`T=1`): Compiling one `@cute.jit` TVM-FFI host launcher per tactic slashes CPU enqueue overhead (`4.22x–8.75x`) when GPU execution is only `10–27 us`.
  • Non-Recompute MXFP8 FSDP2 Training: Phase-specific plane gathering cuts All-Gather traffic and unsharded plane memory by `50.0%` across forward and backward passes.
  • GB300 NVLink72 816B RL Training: Using `CP=4 x EP=16 x FSDP=8` keeps `EP=16` inside a 4-host NVLink domain and raises `131K` MFU to `28%` on `50%` fewer GPUs.
  • High-Throughput RDMA & IPC Telemetry: Reading the clock once per `ibv_post_send` chain (`-69.84%` lock hold) and aligning ring headers to `256 B` (`TELEMETRY_VERSION=6`).

⛔ Not For (Boundary Conditions)

  • Two-Head Cake GDP at Low Wave Occupancy (`B*HV < 32*sm`): Halving CTAs when waves are under-occupied starves SMs; keep the single-head `l8r4` or `splitv8_tile8` (`B*HV ≤ 160`) kernels there.
  • Unchecked Fast `tanh` SiLU for $x < -4$ ($h < -2$): Never use `h + h * tanh.approx(h)` without the warp-vote fallback (`min(h) < -2`), or catastrophic cancellation exceeds `3900%`.
  • Single-Plane MXFP8 Unshard Under Activation Recompute: When backward recomputes forward activations from the same unshard, both rowwise and columnwise planes are required simultaneously.
  • 64-Bit Inner Head Strides on ROCm `gfx942`: Do not type bounded head strides (`head_size ≤ 256`) as `tl.int64` in high-register-pressure Triton kernels; advance base pointer in 64-bit and keep head offsets in `int32`.
  • Dynamo `--freeze-gc-after-init` on Encode/Headless Workers: Explicitly rejected because headless/encode lifecycles allocate dynamic Python objects post-init.
