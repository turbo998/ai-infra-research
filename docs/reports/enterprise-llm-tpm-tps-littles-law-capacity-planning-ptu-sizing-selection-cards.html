4 / 7 · UNDER THE HOOD

Forward & Reverse Capacity Planning Formulas

# 1. DAU -> Peak RPS -> Raw & Weighted TPM
RPS_peak = (DAU * turns_per_day * k_peak) / 86400
TPM_in = RPS_peak * N_in * 60; TPM_out = RPS_peak * N_out * 60
TPM_ptu = TPM_in + w_out * TPM_out # w_out = 4..8

# 2. Little's Law Active Concurrency (B_active)
E2EL = TTFT + (N_out - 1) * TPOT
B_active = RPS_peak * E2EL
Longer outputs (`N_out`) or slower `TPOT` linearly inflate `B_active` KV memory!
