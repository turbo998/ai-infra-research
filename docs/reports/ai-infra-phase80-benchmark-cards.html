7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECTURAL GUIDANCE & READINESS

Adopt Sentinel-Driven NVLink Returns, Pre-Pool Draft Layout Resolution & Child-Quantizer Globbing Immediately; Guard Against NaN Payload Collisions

✅ Good Fit (Recommended Production Adoption)

  • 16-Rank NVL72 MoE Decode (`CakeMxfp8MegaMoeEp16`): Use `st.relaxed.sys` + `0xFFFFFFFFu` sentinel polling and deferred FC1 prefetch for `1.046x–1.051x` lower latency at 16–64 tokens/rank.
  • Long-Context NVFP4 MLA on GB300/B200: Enable `nanosleep(200)` dequant pacing and `sm_103a` proxy-fence elision (`1.056x` at 128K KV) with `current_device()` workspace allocation.
  • Hopper MiniMax-M3 & DFlash Speculative Decoding: Store `index_k` in `float8_e4m3fn` (`-50%` cache/BW), enforce 64-bit `tile_ptrs`, and resolve `DraftParallelSpec` before KV pool sizing.
  • Blackwell FA4 Decode & CUTLASS CLC GEMM: Enable S/P TMEM ping-pong (`s_p_o_stage=2`) on SM100/SM103 and epilogue-local tile counters in dynamic CLC GEMMs.
  • veRL + vLLM FP8/MXFP8 RL Rollout Alignment: Use `f"{key}.*"` child-quantizer ignore rules in Megatron ModelOpt QAT and reverse-order layer remapping in vLLM draft models.

⛔ Not For (Known Boundaries & Guardrails)

  • Unconstrained Non-Canonical NaN Payloads in EP16: Do not feed activations that can emit paired `0xFFFF` negative quiet NaNs into `0xFFFFFFFFu` sentinel buffers, or `cake_megamoe_wait_route_terms` will spin waiting for arrival.
  • Large-Batch Kimi-K3 TP12 Tails (`M > 8`): Do not force `k23_fused` beyond `K23_MAX_TOKENS = 8`; switch to grouped ESS for `9 <= M <= 960`.
  • FA4 `hd256` 2-CTA with CLC or 1-CTA Metadata: Never enable CLC on `hd256` 2-CTA tiles (causes illegal instruction/hang) or switch to 2-CTA when reusing caller-built 1-CTA `scheduler_metadata`.
  • DFlash CUDA DP Attention with `CP > 1`: `--attn-dp-size > 1` for DFlash currently requires `attn_cp_size == 1` and `--enable-dp-lm-head`.
