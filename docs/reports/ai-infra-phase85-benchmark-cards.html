MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS 7 / 7

Production Guidance: Adopt CuteDSL SM10x/SM107 & DeepGEMM QAT Now; Gate 32-Bit ROCm & Unaligned MXFP8

✅ Good Fit (Immediate Production Adoption)

  • Blackwell / SM107 Decode & MoE: Use `cake_gated_act_mxfp8` (`B*K ≥ 4,096`), `cake_kimi_k3_fused_router` (`T ≤ 128`), `cake_qsa_nvfp4` (`red.global.sys.add.v4.bf16`), and `cake_all_gather_matmul` on symmetric NVLink domains.
  • RL Low-Precision Rollout (`veRL`): Pair Megatron ModelOpt QAT (`MXFP8` / `MXFP4` / `NVFP4`) with `compressed-tensors` trainer export and vLLM/SGLang `deep_gemm` rollout (`5.64x` lower MXFP4 KL).
  • Disaggregated PD & Long-Context KV: Enable Mooncake batch `/api/v1/soft_pin` (`4,096` keys/call, `7-day` max TTL), SGLang EAGLE HiCache bigram hash restoration (`0ff67ee7`), and NIXL Trace V2 `pushContext`.
  • FA4 Blackwell / GB300 (`SM100/SM103`): Deploy 2-CTA direct register `dQaccum` (`64 KB` SMEM saved) and `hd128` `65536 // threads_per_cta` register cap.

⛔ Not For (Enforce Hard Guards)

  • Sub-4096 MXFP8 Shapes: Do not route `B*K < 4,096` or `N % 128 != 0` tensors into `cake_gated_act_mxfp8` (requires 128-element `float4` alignment).
  • ROCm >2B-Element KV Caches with i32 Pointers: Never force `use_32bit_indexing = True` in `TRITON_ATTN` when `num_blocks * block_size * num_kv_heads * head_size > 2^31 - 1` (`a55937f2`).
  • Hybrid Chunked-Local Models with `SimpleCPUOffloadConnector`: Blocked in vLLM (`65bc6983`) until cross-attention block alignment is generalized.
  • Un-QAT'd MXFP4 RL Rollout: Direct PTQ MXFP4 rollout without Megatron ModelOpt QAT suffers `5.64x` higher `k3_kl` (`0.00790` vs `0.00140`).
