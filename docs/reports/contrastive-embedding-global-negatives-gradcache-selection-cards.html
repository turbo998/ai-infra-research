MY TAKE

Use cache replay to fit a global objective, not to promise free throughput.

SYNTHETIC DEMO · independently_reproduced=false · no trained model

✅ Good fit

Large in-batch negative objectives.

Activation-bound encoders.

Deterministic replay controls.

Chunked score rows.

Verified distributed gradients.

⛔ Not for

Assuming DDP automatically pools negatives.

Detached remote targets as exact training.

Mutable weights between passes.

Uncontrolled dropout/BatchNorm.

Ignoring false negatives or quadratic logits.
