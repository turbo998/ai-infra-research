EN: Above `B >= 128` with `ck=512`, `decode.py` disables Split-K (`split=1`, `0 B` workspace), activates `dequant_warps=16, phase_chunks=2, use_ws=True`, and computes `waves = ceil(160/148) = 2`, `persistent_ctas = ceil(160/2) = 80`.
ZH：当 `B=160 > 148 SMs` 时，将持久化 CTA 设为 80，使 2 个波次严格均衡（消除尾波仅 12 CTA 的 54.05% 低利用率），且 `compact_metadata=True`。

Act automatically · 1.716x Speedup
EN: Partitioning `dim_size=4608` into 16 whole blocks (`8 × 512` KV latent + `8 × 64` RoPE key) prevents the 4 mid-matrix splits caused by naive 6-rank sharding, while `compute_dtype=bfloat16` cuts redistribution traffic by 50% (`8.757ms -> 5.103ms`).
ZH：按 `(512, 64)` 周期整块分片保护了 Newton-Schulz 正交化边界；在 GB200 `EP=72` 下选择 `capacity_factor=0.25` 仅需 `4,033.12 MiB`（占满额 `16,132.5 MiB` 的 25%）。

Confirm Headroom · 180 ms Flip
EN: `handle_pd_role_switch` checks `available_graph_gb (6.0) >= decode_cuda_graph_memory_gb (2.5)` before `teardown_disaggregation()`. If headroom is insufficient (`2.2 < 4.0 GB`), it aborts early with `safe_to_restore=True`.

Key Robustness Takeaway · 鲁棒性核心发现

EN: Low-bit hardware primitives (`e2m1x2`, `BlockShard`, page-aligned HiCache) fail silently when upstream compilers or sharding frameworks apply seemingly equivalent transformations (reciprocal multiplication `x * (1/s)`, uniform row slicing `Shard(0)`, or unaligned prompt offsets). Explicit invariants (`tl.div_rn`, `enable_fp_fusion=False`, `BlockShard.block_start`, and `prefill_offloaded_len = (L // page) * page`) are mandatory.
ZH：低比特硬件原语对看似等价的代数/切分变换高度敏感：倒数乘法会破坏 E2M1 半值舍入，均分切行会切断融合投影矩阵，未对齐偏移会导致 HiCache 出现 38-token 断洞。

7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS ARCHITECT DECISION GUIDE · 架构师选型指南

Architect's Verdict: When to Deploy Frost Mixed MXFP8/FP4, TorchTitan FlexShard & SGLang Hot Role Switch

✅ Good Fit (推荐使用场景)

  • SM100 Long-Context Sparse Decode (DeepSeek-V4.1): Combining 128-token MXFP8 sliding window with $K \le 512$ FP4 global cache cuts KV bandwidth by 67.19% while preserving local token fidelity.
  • Fused Heterogeneous Projections Under Muon: Models like Kimi (`wkv_a` with `(512, 64)` KV latent + RoPE blocks) require `BlockShard(0, (512, 64))` and `compute_dtype=bfloat16` to avoid mid-matrix orthogonalization corruption.
  • GB200 NVLink72 MoE Training (`EP=72`): Using `HybridEP` with `non_blocking_capacity_factor=0.25` eliminates `cudaStreamSynchronize` D2H stalls, enables `torch.compile`, and saves 12.1 GiB per GPU.
  • Diurnal / Bursting Prefill-Decode Clusters: SGLang's runtime `handle_pd_role_switch` rebalances Prefill ↔ Decode capacity in ~180 ms without reallocating the token KV pool.

⛔ Not For (不适用或需规避场景)

  • Pre-Blackwell GPUs or Multi-Token Decode (`seq_len_q > 1`): `deepseek_v41_decode` strictly requires SM100 (`(10, 0)`), `page_size=64`, and single-token decode (`Q [B,1,64,512]`); MTP and SM103 are not yet in scope.
  • Concurrent Multi-Stream Execution of One `_Plan`: Reusing a single `_Plan` instance concurrently across streams corrupts plan-owned `out`/`lse`/`workspace` buffers.
  • Unbalanced MoE Routing Without `_over_budget` Monitoring: Setting `non_blocking_capacity_factor < 1.0` under severe expert collapse drops excess tokens; monitor sticky `_over_budget`.
  • PD Role Switch With Staging Buffers or Non-Idle Draining: `SGLANG_DISAGG_STAGING_BUFFER` and in-flight requests are rejected; failures during `teardown_disaggregation` require pod restart (`_pd_role_switch_unhealthy=True`).
