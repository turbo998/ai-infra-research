4 / 7 · UNDER THE HOOD

Task Vectors, `DARE` Rescaling & `TIES` Sign Election

Task Vector: tau_k = W_k - W_base
1. DARE: Drop 85% small deltas -> tau'_k = m * tau_k / (1 - p)
2. TIES Sign Election: gamma = sgn(sum_k tau'_k)
3. Disjoint Merge: Average only deltas where sgn(tau'_k) == gamma

Dividing by `(1 - p)` preserves expected layer activation variance while dropping 85% of coordinates eliminates cross-model collisions!

Executes out-of-core on CPU or a single GPU in ~20 minutes
