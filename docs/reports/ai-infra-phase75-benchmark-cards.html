7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS PRODUCTION ARCHITECTURE GUIDE

Adopt Packed Norm-Combine, N-Split Tails, and Packed IPC Keys Now; Gate W4A16 Split MoE Around T=128

✅ Good Fit (Recommended Production Paths)

  • 8×B200 / 8×B300 Multi-GPU Residual + RMSNorm: Enable `cake_fused_norm_combine` (#7330) across all token buckets (`1.048x–1.107x`).
  • Decode (`T ≤ 64`) & Large Prefill (`T ≥ 512`) W4A16 MoE: Route `BF16 × NVFP4` experts to `cutlass_fused_moe(backend="cake")` (`1.054x` @ `D1`, `1.047x` @ `G8`).
  • Blackwell NVFP4 Out-Projection Tails: Enable `256x128` N-split tail (#7314) whenever `M ≥ 32` and partial last wave `≤ 0.5` (`1.021x` GB300 / `1.042x` GB200, bitwise identical).
  • Multimodal CUDA Graphs & ROCm KV Views: Use TRT-LLM `(T+1, C+1)` isolated dummy context (#20213) and vLLM `set_triton_ptr_range` (#60084) for `< 2 GiB` layer slices.
  • Multi-Process KV & RL Transfer: Deploy LMCache big-endian `uint32` token keys (`9.0x` smaller, #5150) and Mooncake 3-tier HCA affinity (#4631).

⛔ Not For (Known Anti-Patterns & Guardrails)

  • Unconditional W4A16 Split MoE at `T ≈ 128`: Do not pin `backend="cake"` at `D128` (`0.949x`) or `G128` (`0.923x`); dispatch mid-batch tokens to monolithic CUTLASS.
  • Unchecked `> 299,520` Token Varlen on SM120: `MiniMaxH3VarlenPlan` uses signed 32-bit offsets (`heads=56, D=128`); `≥ 299,521` tokens must shard via CP or 64-bit kernels.
  • IEEE Division in MiniMax NVFP4 KV Kernels: Never replace `rcp.approx.ftz.f32` with exact IEEE divide (`~0.1%` nibble drift against FlashInfer reference).
  • Full-Prompt KV Checksum + Decode RadixCache: Incompatible on suffix-only disaggregated KV transfers (#39729); keep checksum disabled when decode radix cache is on.
  • Asymmetric MLA Head Dim on PyTorch SDPA Fallback: `QK != V` head dims must not silently fall back to standard SDPA (#5546).
