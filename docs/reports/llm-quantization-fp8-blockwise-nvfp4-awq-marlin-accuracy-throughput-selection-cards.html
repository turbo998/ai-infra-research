2 / 7 · SCENARIO: WHY DEEPSEEK USES `1X128` & `128X128` BLOCK FP8

Isolating Activation Outliers Without Losing `2x` Hopper/Blackwell TensorCore Speed

In SwiGLU and MoE layers, a few outlier channels reach `100x` the magnitude of normal channels. A single per-tensor FP8 scale squashes normal channels into underflow.

  • choice (Fine-Grained Tile/Block Scaling): Act automatically — Scale activations per `1x128` token group and weights per `128x128` sub-block (`99.8%` accuracy retention!).
  • choice (Hardware-Matched Format Selection): Act automatically — Use `W4A16 Marlin` on `A100`, `Block FP8` on `H100/H200/L40S`, and `NVFP4` on `B200/GB200`.
  • score (Batch-Size Crossover Gate): Act automatically — Use `W4A16` only when `B <= 16`; use `W8A8 FP8` when `B >= 24`.
