7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECTURAL FIT & READINESS

My Take: Adopt Unified QK-RoPE Regions & HA Drain; Gate NVL-Only Fused EP & Unclamped Gathers

✅ Good Fit (Recommended Production Patterns)

  • FlashInfer Cake Cooperative RMSNorm Grad (`0d062af`): Ideal for large-batch pretraining (`T > 1024/256` on B200/GB300, `T > 2048/1024` on R200) requiring deterministic FP32 `dweight` reductions.
  • TorchTitan `muse_spark_attention_qk` (`8f8dbfb`) & Cuspy CUPTI (`b93d5c0`): Essential for low-latency decode (`3.75x`) and full CUDA Graph kernel/NCCL-event observability (`key_by="exec"`).
  • Mooncake HA SIGTERM Drain (`3db34ce`) & NIXL `eventfd` (`8a78b33`): Mandatory for zero-OpLog-loss rolling HA restarts (`SA_ONSTACK` + 15s watchdog + `_exit(0)`) and high-thread-count UCX engines.
  • SGLang `gfx1250` EP (`89860fb`) & LMCache Kimi Linear (`e7a4f23`): Enables W4A8 MXFP4 EP on AMD `gfx1250` and zero-copy Mamba `conv` (`slice_axis=1`) KV transfers.

⛔ Not For (Anti-Patterns & Hard Boundaries)

  • Multi-Node IB EP with `NVTE_EP_FUSED_PREPARE_DISPATCH=1` (`b7db08f`): Restricted strictly to a single NVLink domain; also unsuitable if downstream code assumes rank-scan row ordering inside an expert block.
  • Fused Shared Experts under `gfx1250` W4A8 EP (`89860fb`): Explicitly raises `NotImplementedError` when `moe_ep_size > 1` and `num_fused_shared_experts > 0`.
  • Co-running `nsys` / `dynolog` with Cuspy Graph Annotations (`b93d5c0`): Cuspy holds an exclusive process-wide CUPTI subscription from the first CUDA graph capture until exit.
  • C++ Stack Unwinding on Planned Mooncake HA Exit (`3db34ce`): Destroying the coordinator revokes the etcd lease synchronously; always exit via `_exit(0)` after the 9-stage drain.
