7 / 7 · MY TAKEGOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Architectural Fit, Deployment Boundaries & Release Readiness

✅ Good fit

  • SM120 / Blackwell MoE Serving: Symbolic cute.sym_int() static MoE eliminates multi-second per-batch-size JIT stalls while NVFP4 direct micro accelerates M <= 8 decode.
  • Long-Context Sparse MLA (vLLM): Chunking trtllm-gen calls at 65,535 tokens safely scales past 128K batched tokens while halving DCP workspace at 131K.
  • Disaggregated KV & RL Refit: Allocator-boundary RDMA pins in SGLang, single-DP round-robin refit in Megatron-LM, and VeOmni FP8 rollout quantization in verl.

⛔ Not for

  • Multi-Wave Grouped FP8 Shapes: The 128x64 KG1 route applies only when M > 32 and ceil(M/128)*ceil(N/64) <= SM_count; multi-wave shapes retain 128x128 tiles.
  • Variable-Q Prefill under CUDA Graphs: fixed_uniform_q_len=True is strictly for uniform-query decode; variable-length prefill/POD must keep fixed_uniform_q_len=False.
  • Non-HCCS Device Buffers: Mooncake's 2 MiB alignment check must not be applied to hccs:host or roce:* transports.
