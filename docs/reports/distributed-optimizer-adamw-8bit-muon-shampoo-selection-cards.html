Why Decoupled Weight Decay & RMS Scaling Are Mandatory for Trillion-Token Muon Runs

Because every orthogonalized update $U V^\top$ has fixed spectral norm $\approx 1$, running Muon without decoupled weight decay causes weight Frobenius norms $\|W\|_F$ to grow monotonically over trillions of tokens, eventually saturating attention logits (`0.88x` collapse). Adding Decoupled Weight Decay + $0.2\sqrt{\max(m,n)}$ RMS scaling (Tier 4) bounds weight norms identically to AdamW across 10T+ tokens 🏆.

Robustness Conclusion: Never run Muon on trillions of tokens without decoupled weight decay and hybrid AdamW routing for Embeddings/LM-Head.

7 / 7 MY TAKE · ARCHITECT DECISION GUIDANCE & PRODUCTION READINESS

Good Fit vs. Not For: Where to Deploy 2D Distributed Muon in Production

Practical architecture rules for AI Solution Architects and pretraining infrastructure teams.

✅ Good Fit (When to Adopt Tier 4)

  • Foundation Model Pretraining & Large-Scale CPT (Tier 4): Cuts training tokens required to reach target validation loss by 27.5% (1.38x efficiency) while halving optimizer VRAM.
  • Large MoE Models With Massive Expert Weights (Tier 4): Saving 6 Bytes/Param across hundreds of routed 2D expert matrices frees terabytes of HBM.
  • Single-Node Memory-Limited SFT (Tier 2): Use 8-Bit blockwise AdamW when only VRAM reduction is needed on short SFT runs.

⛔ Not For (When to Avoid)

  • Applying Muon to Token Embeddings, LM Head, or 1D RMSNorm: Always keep lookup tables and 1D vectors on AdamW.
  • Orthogonalizing Fused QKV Tensors Without Splitting: Always slice Q, K, and V heads/projections before calling newtonschulz5.
  • Naive Replicated AllGather Across FSDP Ranks: Always partition orthogonalization ownership across FSDP ranks (param_idx % dp_size).

Day-1 Production Muon Integration Checklist

1. Route param.ndim == 2 (excluding embed/lm_head) to Muon; route all other parameters to AdamW.
2. Scale Muon updates by 0.2 * math.sqrt(max(m, n)) and enable decoupled weight decay (e.g., wd=0.1).
3. Transpose tall matrices (m > n) before the 5-step quintic loop to minimize Gram matrix size.
4. Assign each 2D parameter to a single FSDP owner rank per step to avoid redundant compute.
