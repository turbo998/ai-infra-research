7 / 7MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Architectural Guidance for Solution Architects Deploying Blackwell & Disaggregated KV

✅ Good Fit
  • Small-Batch MoE Decode on NVLink: Use FlashInfer Lamport transport (T <= 159) for 2.54x–2.77x dispatch/combine speedup without system fences.
  • SM120 W4A16 MXFP4 & Sidecars: Upgrade to b12x E8M0 bias 71 and -1 top-k sum filtering to prevent subnormal underflow and NaN corruption.
  • Hybrid SWA + Mamba Serving: Adopt SGLang's unified MultiEndedAllocator + HiCache recurrent event forwarding and Mooncake's configurable max_blocks_per_context.
⛔ Not For
  • Large Prefill All-to-All via Lamport: Above 159–512 tokens or 64 MiB per rank, polling every 16B vector saturates NVLink/L2; keep flag + release/acquire fence mode.
  • Unaligned Ascend HCCS Buffers: Mooncake AscendDirectTransport strictly rejects device buffers not aligned to 2 MiB (2,097,152 B).
  • Unpadded Sparse MMA Tails in TileLang: K tiles not divisible by micro_size_k are rejected at compile time.
