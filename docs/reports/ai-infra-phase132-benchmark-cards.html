Eliminating Multi-Launch Split-K Overhead on GB300 & TP8 Chunk Over-Splitting on MI300X

Input Case: High-concurrency MoE decode & model load across GB300 (sm_103a), B200 (sm_100a), and MI300X (gfx942).

1 · FlashInfer Kimi-K3 GB300 Resident Fused Decode (2e1789d) score Act automatically

Replaces 2-launch split-K + finalize at TP=8, f_b M=257..512 with a single 32-token 144-CTA resident kernel accumulating FP32 partials in SMEM (5.472 → 4.640 μs, 1.1793x speedup).

2 · vLLM ROCm gfx942 Kimi-K3 MXFP4-to-INT4 Value-Sized Chunking (8a2a5e4) choice Act automatically

Sizes conversion chunks by unpacked values (_K3_INT4_CONVERT_CHUNK_VALUES = 1 << 27, 512.0 MiB peak FP32), cutting TP=8 chunk launches per expert tensor from 64 → 8 and requant time from 14.55 → 3.00 s (4.85x).

3 · Megatron-FSDP Backward Recompute Weight Prefetch (90e4049) score Act automatically

Registers recomputed inner modules into recompute_buckets so backward weight prefetch runs before activation recomputation, cutting exposed AllGather stall from 14.8 → 1.8 ms (1.1646x step speedup).

Architectural Takeaway: Sizing chunking/scheduling loops by actual per-rank unpacked elements and registering nested recompute dependencies eliminates hidden launch and AllGather bubbles.

7 / 7 · MY TAKEARCHITECTURAL FIT & READINESS

Where Phase 132 Optimizations Deliver Immediate Production Value

✅ Good Fit

  • Blackwell B200/GB300 MoE & Long-Context Decode: Probed SM-pair die splitting, 32-token resident Kimi-K3 FP8 decode, and fused 2-pass DSA radix selection directly cut tail latency.
  • RL Rollout & Hybrid Disaggregated Serving: SGLang subset weight hashing (`check_weights`) + `prefill_weight_versions`, vLLM HiSparse host-page reservation, and TRT-LLM Attention/SSM `CacheGroupId` policies harden online RL and P/D disaggregation.
  • Large-Scale MoE/CSA Training & RDMA/NVMe Transport: Megatron-FSDP recompute prefetch, DSv4 CSA in-place RoPE (`-50%` activation), and Mooncake/LMCache/NIXL transport fixes prevent OOMs and UAF crashes.

⛔ Not For

  • Assuming Equal SM Counts Across Dual-Die B200: Hard-coding `total_sms / 2` causes wave tail skew when dies have unequal harvested SM pairs (`35` vs `39`).
  • Chunking Tensor Conversions Only by Outer Row Count: Under high TP (`TP=8`), row-only chunking over-splits narrow per-rank shards into tiny kernels; always bound by total unpacked elements (`1 << 27`).
  • Running Unverified `aiter` ASM v3 FA Backward at `seqlen_q=1` on MI355X: Must route `seqlen_q==1, hdim 65..128` to CK-tile until upstream ASM kernel fixes land.
