7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS Architect Decision Guide

Production Adoption Guide for Phase 113 (Priority 122) Kernel, Serving & Training Fixes

✅ Good fit (Adopt Immediately)

  • Blackwell & Hopper Serving: Upgrade FlashInfer to get `n_l2_group=8` on `K=7168, expected_m<=1` DeepGEMM routes (`4d018bcd12`), `cake_blackwell_softmax` cached-cluster/persistent routes (`1.283x`, `510e514b25`), and `cake_tinygemm2_tcgen05` (`8d9c839db0`).
  • Ampere SM80 Low-Latency MoE: Enable `FrostLowLatencyConfig(max_num_tokens=8)` on A100 (`sm_80`) for BF16/FP16 SwiGLU/ReLU2 (`0e406f333b`).
  • Tiered KV Offload & Multi-Tenant LMCache: Enable vLLM KVCR `align_sequence` (`e3883b7a7e`) and LMCache `request_configs` lookup key tagging (`e45d4d5a85`) to prevent root prefix eviction and cross-LoRA cache pollution.
  • VLM & Deterministic Blackwell Training: Pull Megatron `3ca52d95d6` (FP32 vision RoPE) and `62c063f2b7` (`Ring,NVLS` on SM100), plus TE `6d6a500860` (skip FA4 on SM120 deterministic backward).

⛔ Not for (Boundary Conditions)

  • Quantized FROST on SM80: Ampere SM80 FROST (`0e406f333b`) supports BF16/FP16 only; FP8/NVFP4/MXFP4 weights require SM90+ or SM100+.
  • Hopper `NVLS` under `--deterministic-mode`: Megatron `62c063f2b7` intentionally continues to reject `NVLS` on Hopper (`sm_90`) because Hopper NVLS switch reductions are not bitwise deterministic.
  • Unvalidated Alias Paths on Cached DFS Shards: Mooncake `d782a5998f` skips path normalization only when the descriptor string exactly equals the open-time validated path; symlinks or altered paths still trigger full validation.
  • Skipping Per-Token Decode When `logprobs` Is Requested: Dynamo `cffc4fa75c` only skips per-token UTF-8 decoding when `log_probs.is_none()`.
