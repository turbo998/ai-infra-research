MY TAKE · ARCHITECTURAL FIT, ANTI-PATTERNS & READINESS 7 / 7

Deploy Rail-Isolated CPO, BAR1-Mapped 3FS USRBIO & Co-Scheduled GDS; Never Mix Unthrottled UFFD with KV Offloading

✅ Good Fit (Production Patterns)
  • CPO + ELSFP with 8-Rail Orthogonal Mapping: Cuts 100K-GPU optics power from `7.92 MW` to `2.16 MW` (`0.72 MW` with OCS Spine replacement) while isolating `ELSFP` 8-port failures to at most `1/8` bandwidth per node.
  • MEMS/Piezo OCS for Job-Level & Fault Reconfiguration: Eliminates Spine O-E-O transceivers and lifts 100K sync goodput to `96.93%` when switching occurs outside active microsecond collectives.
  • 3FS `hf3fs_usrbio` + GPUDirect BAR1 Zero-Copy: Bypasses the Linux FUSE 400K IOPS spinlock and host DDR bounce, delivering `90.0 GB/s` KV reload (`1.265 s` for `100 GiB`) and `17.986 s` `1 TiB` checkpoints.
  • Tool-Duration-Aware KV Tiering (`t_tool >= 2.56 s`) + 35% UFFD Cap: Offloads `4.0 GiB` Agentic KV via GDS while throttling Firecracker UFFD page-fault storms, boosting active rollouts `13.23x–25.58x`.
  • Token-in-Token-out (`prompt_ids`/`response_mask`): Preserves 100% prefix KV reuse across multi-turn `verl` `ToolAgentLoop` rollouts.
⛔ Not For (Anti-Patterns to Avoid)
  • Plugging Same-Node NICs into One `OE` or `ELSFP`: SONiC `deinit_oe_sibling_pports()` disables all sibling ports (`1.6–6.4 Tbps`) simultaneously on low-power/fault events, severing multi-rail redundancy.
  • Per-Layer Dynamic OCS Switching in MoE All-to-All: MEMS switching (`15.0 ms`) is `12.5x` slower than a `1.2 ms` MoE layer compute window, collapsing utilization.
  • Running GDS Across PCIe Bridges with ACS Redirect Enabled: Forces BAR1 P2P TLPs through the CPU Root Complex (`BASE_PCI_DISTANCE_CROSSRP`), destroying P2P DMA throughput.
  • Unthrottled Firecracker UFFD Alongside GDS KV Swap: A `500 VMs/s` UFFD storm consumes `50.33 GB/s`, inflating `4.0 GiB` KV swap to `2.139 s` (`136.92 GiB·s`, worse than HBM pinning).
  • Blind `UFFDIO_COPY` Retry on `-EAGAIN`: Deadlocks KVM vCPUs unless pending `UFFD_EVENT_REMOVE` balloon events are drained first.
