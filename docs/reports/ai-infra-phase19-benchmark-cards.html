2 / 7 · SCENARIO: SM100 BLACKWELL FA4 TMEM & MUFU OFFLOAD CASE DEEP DIVE

512-Column TMEM In-Place Aliasing Eliminates 64 KiB SMEM Spill While Packed FFMA2 Emulation Breaks the 1,997 TFLOPS MUFU Wall

INPUT CONTRACT: B200 (148 SMs @ 1.83 GHz, 256 KiB TMEM = 128 lanes × 512 cols, 65,536 regs/SM). Tile = 128×128×128 (8,388,608 FLOPs/tile; TC ideal = 1,024 cycles BF16 / 512 cycles FP8; MUFU base = 682.7 cycles/tile).
Decision 1 · TMEM 512-Col In-Place P0/P1 Aliasing (choice: TS vs SS MMA) Act automatically · 0 B SMEM P

S0 [0..128) & S1 [128..256) hold QK scores; Softmax0/1 write V0/V1 stats at cols 0/128 and quantized P0/P1 at cols 32..64 & 160..192 directly inside S0/S1. to_tiled_mma_sm100_ts reads P from TMEM, freeing 64.0 KiB SMEM so 164.0 KiB remains for 4-stage KV TMA buffers.

TMEM Utilization: 512 / 512 cols (256.0 KiB = 100.0%) Register File: 65,280 / 65,536 regs (99.61% CUTLASS) · 61,184 (93.36% FA4 BF16)
Decision 2 · Hybrid FFMA2 Polynomial ex2_emulation_2 + Lazy rescale_threshold (score) Act automatically · 2,790.1 TFLOPS FP8

Pure hardware MUFU.EX2 stalls FP8 at 1,997.0 TFLOPS (45.0% SOL). Offloading 32.5% of exp2 (`ex2_emu_freq=8`) to degree-3 Horner fma_packed_f32x2 (`max_rel_err = 8.8e-5`) + skipping Correction TMEM rescaling when acc_scale_ >= -4.0 reaches 2,790.1 TFLOPS (62.9% SOL).

B200 FP8 SOL%: 62.9% (2,790.1 TFLOPS) vs 45.0% (1,997.0 TFLOPS) B200 BF16 SOL%: 95.8% (2,125.3 TFLOPS)
Decision 3 · SM103 (B300) Hardware exp2 & tcgen05.ld.red Row-Max (choice) Confirm Arch Target · 3,200.0 TFLOPS

On Arch.sm_103f (B300), native hardware exp2 doubles throughput (`ex2_emu_freq=0`) and tcgen05.ld.red computes row max inside the TMEM controller during S load, reaching 3,200.0 TFLOPS (72.1% SOL).

SM103 B300 FP8 SOL%: 72.1% (3,200.0 TFLOPS) Software fmax tree eliminated via update_row_max_precomputed

7 / 7 · MY TAKE · ARCHITECTURAL FIT & PRODUCTION READINESS DECISION GUIDE

When to Deploy SM100 FA4 Emulation, MLA Host Dedup & Cached Async DCP — and Where They Break

✅ Good Fit (Deploy Immediately)

  • SM100 B200 Long-Context Training & Prefill: Enable 512-col TMEM P0/P1 in-place aliasing (`TS` MMA), 16-warp role specialization, ex2_emu_freq=10 (BF16) / 8 (FP8 causal), and rescale_threshold=4.0 to reach 2,125–2,790 TFLOPS.
  • DeepSeek-V3/V4 MLA Multi-Turn Serving on TP > 1: Enable MLAHostDedupBroadcaster so only Rank 0 allocates Host DDR5 and broadcasts layerwise over NVLink (8.0x unique prefix capacity, 1.621x faster H2D load).
  • Hybrid Mamba/SWA + Full-Attention Prefix Caching: Intersect restorable_prefix_pages across all TP ranks and enable StoragePrefetchRetries queue-head cancellation.
  • 1K–16K GPU FSDP2 Checkpointing: Instantiate a persistent DefaultStager(StagingOptions(True, True, True, True)) across the entire job lifetime so WeakIdKeyDictionary reuses pinned+shared CPU storages (0.048 s stall).

⛔ Not For (Avoid / Use Fallback)

  • Aggressive ex2_emu_freq=8 on Non-Causal FP8 or SM103 (B300): On B200 non-causal FP8, freq=8 regresses throughput (0.94x due to FMA pipe contention); on SM103 B300, native hardware exp2 is already fast, so keep ex2_emu_freq=0 and enable tcgen05.ld.red.
  • MLAHostDedupBroadcaster with MLATokenToKVPoolFP4 or Disjoint Remote Storage: Excluded by mla_host_dedup_eligible because per-rank FP4 scale buffers are not covered by the broadcast, and non-compatible storage backends require rank-local host buffers.
  • Per-Checkpoint Recreated DefaultStager or Odd-World-Size _ReplicationStager: Calling stager.close() every save triggers a 3.737 s cudaHostRegister storm; _ReplicationStager requires an even world_size and 2x host memory headroom.
