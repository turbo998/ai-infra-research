7 / 7 · MY TAKE Architectural Guidance & Readiness

When to Adopt Phase 110 Optimizations vs Where Guardrails Still Apply

✅ Good Fit (Immediate Production Value)

  • Blackwell & Hopper Low-Latency Serving: Deploy FlashInfer `#7439` (Kimi-K3 MLA), `#7497` (TP12 reduce tail), `#7462` (MoE AR union), and `#7444` (norm+combine) for immediate 3%–16.5% kernel latency reductions.
  • Disaggregated & Hybrid SWA KV Pools: Enforce SGLang `#46700` SWA prefix clamping and `#46689` event-gated host KV restore to prevent silent prefix corruption and scheduler stalls.
  • High-Vocab Logprobs & ADP Connectors: Use TRT-LLM `#20803` Triton raw-logprobs selection to save `74.19 MiB` per 128-request batch and `#19369` for per-owner Attention-DP KV connectors.
  • Multi-Tier CXL DAX & Peer KV Routing: Partition single CXL DAX devices across L1/L2 via LMCache `offset_gb` (`#5878`) and enable Dynamo `shared_cache_multiplier` fetch credits (`#16701`).

⛔ Not For / Explicit Guardrails

  • FULL CUDA Graphs on Sparse Prefill: Never capture DSV4.1 sparse MLA or Compressor prefill inside FULL CUDA Graphs (`vllm #59986`); restrict graph capture to `UNIFORM_BATCH` decode (`max_decode_query_len`).
  • Unstaged XPU USM File DMA without GDS: Linux `io_uring` cannot DMA directly to/from Intel XPU USM device pointers; always stage through page-aligned (`4096 B`) host buffers (`Mooncake #4902`).
  • Deduplicated Module Tables in FSDP: Do not use `IndexedOrder` when a model re-invokes shared/tied modules across a step (`Megatron-LM #8207`); use occurrence-tracked `ExecutionOrder`.
  • Unpinned Triton FP Fusion in CI: Always set `TRITON_DEFAULT_FP_FUSION=1` explicitly when validating B200 golden losses (`torchtitan #5631`).
