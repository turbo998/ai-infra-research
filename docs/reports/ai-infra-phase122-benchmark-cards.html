7 / 7 · MY TAKE · ARCHITECTURAL FIT & READINESS Decision Guide

When to Adopt 32-Row Swap-AB, Symmetric/CE Collectives & Bounded Streams

✅ Good Fit (Adopt Immediately)

  • Blackwell Ultra (`sm_103a`) MoE Decode/Prefill: Use `bs_ue8m0_swap_rp32_s11` when $K \ge 2048$ and $2 \lceil M/32 \rceil (N/256) \le \text{SM\_count}$, and `bs_ue8m0_swap_c1` for 32-row aligned $256 \le K \le 512, N \le 512$.
  • SM120 Workstation / Server Qwen Sampling: Use `(cluster=8, ept=40)` for $131,072 < V \le 163,840$ when `batch < 160`, and switch to the 2-launch chain at `batch ≥ 160`.
  • Multi-Node GB300 NVL64 Collectives: Enable NCCL 2.32.3 Symmetric kernels (`-R2`) for $\le 2\text{ GB}$ AllReduce (`992 GB/s`) and CE (`-R2 -x2`) for `8 GB` AllGather/Alltoall (`751 GB/s` on NVL8).
  • RL / Online Training Refit (`TP=1, PP=1`): Ensure Megatron-LM `#8502` is applied so destination ranks spread reads across all DP replicas.
  • Disaggregated KV Event & Telemetry Streams: Bound `grpc.aio` queues with a semaphore (`LMCache#5858`), configure Mooncake `max_blocks_per_context`, and use NIXL `#2387` collision-only PID labels.

⛔ Not For (Avoid / Guard Against)

  • Multi-Wave Large-M Grouped FP8 GEMMs: Do not force `bs_ue8m0_swap_rp32_s11` when $2 \lceil M/32 \rceil (N/256) > \text{SM\_count}$ (e.g., `M=2432, N=256` on `148` SMs); keep 64/128-row tiles.
  • Symmetric Collectives > 2 GB on NCCL 2.32.3: NCCL 2.32.3 notes a known performance regression for Symmetric kernels above `2 GB`; use Legacy or CE (`NCCL_SYM_CE_THRESHOLD=17179869184`) for `8 GB` payloads.
  • Sub-2 MiB Caching-Allocator Slices on Ascend HCCS: Do not register arbitrary `torch_npu` sub-blocks as remote-accessible `MEM_DEVICE` buffers (`Mooncake#4903` rejects `addr % 2 MiB != 0`).
  • Unpadded 64-Head Sparse-MLA with Tile-Rounded Stores: Never store `seqlen_q_rounded` rows when `use_padded_offsets=False` (`FA4@2865a891`).
