Why MoE Prefill & Decode Need Two Completely Different All-to-All Kernels

Prefill transfers large contiguous token payloads (bandwidth-bound), while Decode transfers tiny scattered payloads across 64 GPUs (`<200us` latency-bound).

  • choice (`DeepEP Normal` for Prefill/Training): Act automatically — Forward tokens hierarchically via intra-node `NVLink` to rail-matched `RDMA` NICs using only `24 SMs` (`46 GB/s/NIC`).
  • choice (`DeepEP Low-Latency IBGDA` for Decode): Act automatically — Issue RDMA writes directly from GPU SMs (`IBGDA`) without CPU proxy threads (`168us` round-trip!).
  • score (Two-Batch Ping-Pong + `EPLB`): Act automatically — Overlap `Batch A` RDMA with `Batch B` compute and replicate top-5% hot experts (`1.09x` skew).
Eliminates both All-to-All latency stalls and hot-expert straggler bottlenecks
