✅ Good fit (Adopt in Production)

  • Small-Batch Blackwell EP8 MoE (T<=32): FlashInfer #7348 Flag-in-Data cuts launch count to 6 and lowers latency to 141.2 us (CUDA Graph).
  • SM120 Workstation Serving (RTX PRO 6000): Cake QSA NVFP4 (#7313, 1.90x) and >=4 MiB/rank PCIe CE Ring Piped (#7301, 1.111x).
  • Ragged / Varlen FA4 Training & Prefill: Always bound K/V TMA descriptors by seqlen_k (#3012/#3013) and use M=64 2-CTA backward at hdim=256 (#2965).
  • Tiered KV Offload & RL Rollouts: Enable vLLM on_store_failed (#61780), EAGLE partial tails (#61019), verl MXFP4 QAT refit (#8306), and Mooncake HCA affinity (#4631/#4854).

⛔ Not for (Architectural Guardrails)

  • Small PCIe Collectives (<4 MiB/rank): Do not force COPY_ENGINE_RING_PIPED on sub-megabyte decode all-reduces; use one-shot/two-shot SM IPC instead.
  • P2P KV Transfer Tiers in vLLM: Do not trip global tier backpressure on 3 failed P2P sends, as single-request peer cancellation is not global disk exhaustion.
  • Partial Sync-Bucket Delta Flush in verl FP4 Refit: Do not run delta_flush before all sync buckets arrive; routed-expert layout rebuild requires complete bucket staging.
  • Megatron EP A2A Full Recompute + Megatron FSDP: Currently guarded by an explicit assertion in combined_1f1b.py.
