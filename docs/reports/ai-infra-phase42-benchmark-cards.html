7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS ARCHITECTURAL GUIDANCE

Deploy Two-Phase DP Role Switching, Collective MNNVL Fallback, and Streaming `_iter_ns` Now; Gate Rubin `sm_107a` on Hardware

✅ Good Fit / 推荐适用场景

  • Rubin (`sm_107a`) Long-Context MTP MLA Decode: `352 B/token` NVFP4 KV cache with `q_len = 2..4` and non-power-of-2 heads (`H=96`, `1.248x` speedup).
  • Single-Node 8xB200 Containerized Comm: Automatic collective fallback from `FABRIC` to `POSIX_FD` when `/dev/nvidia-caps-imex-channels/channel0` is absent.
  • Elastic PD Disaggregation with DP / DP-Attention: Online Prefill ↔ Decode role switching guarded by `prepare_only=True` two-phase preflight.
  • MoE Training & Multi-Tenant KV Store: `LayerShardedMuon` `_iter_ns` (`16x` lower transient memory) + Mooncake `TenantQuotaPolicy` (`Held/Carried`) + ACC.

⛔ Not For / 不适用或禁止场景

  • Hopper (`sm_90a`) or Blackwell (`sm_100a`/`sm_103a`) for `cake_rubin_mla_nvfp4_decode`: Hard-gated to `ARCH_BY_CAPABILITY = {(10, 7): "sm_107a"}` with no family fallback.
  • Multi-Host MNNVL Without IMEX or Matching NVLink Domain: POSIX FDs cannot cross hosts; mismatched NVML `clusterUuid` raises immediately.
  • PD Role Switching with `--ep-size > 1`, `--pp-size > 1`, or `--dcp-size > 1`: Still blocked by `validate_pd_role_switch_config` due to cross-rank KV/expert state coupling.
