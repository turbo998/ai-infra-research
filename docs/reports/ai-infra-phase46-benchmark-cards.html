✅ Good Fit (When to Adopt Immediately)

  • Blackwell `tcgen05` & Spec-Dec Kernels: Mandatory adoption of `(tid & 96) << 16` warpgroup quarter offsets, warp-uniform epilogue `taddr`, `cta_group::2` `producer_tail` stage drain, and `getCustomMaskCapacityUpperBound` (`SwapsMmaAb` 4.0x capacity).
  • Long-Context Multimodal & Large Host KV Tiers: TRT-LLM chunk-scoped multimodal budgeting (`#19884`) + KVCM2 `MAP_NORESERVE` progressive commit (`#19650`, `2048x` smaller startup stall at `64 GiB`).
  • Contiguous RDMA KV Reads & Multi-Node MoE EP: NIXL GPUNetIO `8 MiB` cross-chunk READ coalescing (`2.10x` bandwidth on 2×H20) and 8ch/8thr UCX CPU Proxy EP (`82.97/80.23 GB/s` on 16×GB200).
  • Multi-Process Fork, ROCm HY-V4 & Intel XPU: `omp_pause_resource_all(2)` pre-fork hook, fused `triton_ihc_post_pre_rms_norm`, and XPU bit-63 host-staging KV transfer.

⛔ Not For (Known Boundaries & Non-Targets)

  • Fragmented or Multi-Key GPUNetIO Transfers: `canCoalesceRead` only merges contiguous same-`lkey`/`rkey` READ ranges up to `8 MiB`; fragmented scatter-gather or WRITE descriptors intentionally keep existing boundaries.
  • Oversubscribed Push CTAs on Small Shapes: 16-CTA/peer SM-push (`48` or `112` CTAs) is tuned for `M=512` (TP4) and `M=1024` (TP8) `K=8192`; tiny decode tokens (`M ≤ 64`) should stay on copy-engine or 8-CTA routes.
  • Unauthenticated Public Exposure of `/engine/planner`: Dynamo's runtime reconfiguration endpoint (`DYN_SYSTEM_PORT`) adds no application-level auth and must be restricted to trusted cluster management networks.
  • 8-Byte `int64` Packed MLA Copy on Intel XPU: `copy_mla_rows_into_pack` corrupts non-8B-aligned slices on XPU and must remain disabled in favor of per-token RDMA transfers.
