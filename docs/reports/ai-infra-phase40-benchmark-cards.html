7 / 7 · MY TAKE · GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS ARCHITECT DECISION GUIDE

Adopt Cake MXFP8, EPLB Slot Checks, SGLang Batch Detok & MFSDP v2 Muon Now; Gate Coredump Wait & 1D Params Carefully

✅ Good fit / 适用场景

  • Blackwell Grouped MoE Quantization (`sm_100a`/`sm_103a`): In-tree `CakeProgram` MXFP8 quantization with zero host padding and `[B, pm_tiles, pk_tiles, 32, 4, 4]` scales.
  • DeepSeek-V4 EPLB & Kimi-K3 GB200 Serving: Pre-traffic `check_eplb_replica_slots` validation and 4-tray `TP8 x PP2 + DCP8 (a2a)` 1M-context deployment.
  • Multi-Tokenizer SGLang & Dynamo K8s Pods: Default `SGLANG_BATCH_TOKENIZER_OUTPUTS=True` (`4.0x` fewer ZMQ calls) and `0s` `SIGQUIT` exit when coredumps are off.
  • Megatron MFSDP v2 + Offline KD v2 + NIXL TP: Sharded `FsdpMuon` for 2D weights, 17-bit packed KD monoliths with per-doc CP zigzag, and `prometheus_mp` multi-rank scraping.

⛔ Not for / 不适用场景

  • Pre-Blackwell GPUs or `K % 32 != 0`: `cake_grouped_mxfp8_quantize` targets `sm_100a`/`sm_103a` and enforces `K % 32 == 0`, `M, B <= 65535`.
  • 1D Bias / LayerNorm / Embedding Parameters in `FsdpMuon`: `FsdpMuon` raises `AssertionError` on non-2D tensors unless chained with SGD/Adam for 1D parameters.
  • Vocabulary Sizes > 131,072 (`2^17`) in KD v2: `v2_pack_indices` encodes exactly 16 low bits + 1 packed 17th bit (`<= 131,072` tokens).
  • High-Cardinality (>32 Buckets) NIXL Histograms: `prometheus_mp` shared-memory slots cap histogram buckets at `32` per metric.
