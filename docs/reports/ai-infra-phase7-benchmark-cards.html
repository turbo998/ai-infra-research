7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECTURAL DECISION GUIDE

Production Verdict: When to Deploy Piecewise/Breakable Graphs, 3-Stage EPD & Adaptive EAGLE-3

Actionable architecture boundaries distilled for cloud & AI infrastructure solutions architects.

✅ Good Fit (Deploy Immediately)

  • Piecewise / Breakable CUDA Graphs + Shared Pool: Mandatory for MoE, Hybrid Mamba/Attention, Chunked Prefill, and PD layer-wise KV transfer. Compresses 32-bucket graph memory 73.63x (`1.04 GiB`) and cuts B200 `BS=1` TPOT 1.92x (`1.575 ms`).
  • 3-Stage EPD (`1E/3P/4D`) + `EncoderCacheManager`: Ideal for multi-turn VLM/video assistants (`Qwen2.5-VL-72B`). Cuts P99 TPOT 3.54x (`16.58 ms`), achieves `92.0%` multi-turn cache hit rate, and boosts throughput 2.70x (`14.60 RPS`).
  • Wide Draft Trees (`N_verify=32..64`) at `BS=1..4` + Adaptive Step Shrinkage at `BS ≥ 8`: Delivers 2.605x–2.647x low-BS speedup while automatically shrinking to `chain_s5_k1_v6` at `BS=32` (2.422x) and disabling speculation at `BS ≥ 64` for low-α traffic.

⛔ Not For (Avoid These Traps)

  • Static Wide Speculative Trees (`N_verify ≥ 32`) at High Batch Sizes (`BS ≥ 16`): Causes severe Roofline collapse (`Q_eff = 1,024 = 4.46x` ridge), degrading throughput to 0.889x (code) and 0.660x (chat).
  • Colocating Heavy ViT Encoders on Decode GPUs: Starves LLM decode streams (`58.64 ms` P99 TPOT) and leaves `144 GB` of LLM weights idle for `38.4%` of GPU cycles.
  • Enabling Unsynchronized `AdaptiveSpeculativeParams` Under Attention DP / TBO / PDMux: As enforced in `adaptive_spec_params.py:70-89`, unsynchronized step switches across DP ranks or TBO wrappers cause collective deadlock or wrapper state loss.
