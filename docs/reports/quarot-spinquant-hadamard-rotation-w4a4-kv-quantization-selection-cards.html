Why Post-RoPE R4 Saves 128K KV4

RoPE applies position-dependent 2D rotations after W_k, concentrating huge energy into low-frequency rotary channels (Attention Sinks). Applying register-level FWHT_128 after RoPE equalizes all 128 head channels before INT4 quantization.

Kronecker Block-Hadamard Efficiency

Factoring 28,672 = 256 × 112 replaces an expensive 28672² matrix multiply with 8 warp-shuffle butterfly stages plus a 112×112 shared-memory MMA, trimming overhead to 1.42%.

Robustness Verdict: SpinQuant remains stable even under 120x pathological code/math activation spikes and 128K context windows where unrotated 4-bit quantization fails completely.

MY TAKE 7 / 7

Architect's Verdict: Deploy SpinQuant R1/R2 + Online FWHT for All W4A4 / NVFP4 & INT4 KV Clusters

Clear architectural boundaries separating when orthogonal Hadamard/Cayley rotation is mandatory vs when simpler weight-only quantization suffices.

✅ Good Fit (When to Adopt)

  • Blackwell B200 (NVFP4) & Native INT4 Tensor Core Serving: Mandatory to prevent 78.5x–120x activation channel spikes from ruining 4-bit GEMM accuracy.
  • Single-80GB-GPU 70B + 32K/128K Consolidation: Cuts total memory from 274.2 GB to 68.6 GB (-75%), eliminating multi-GPU TP communication tax.
  • Long-Context 4-Bit KV-Cache Compression: Post-RoPE R4 rotation boosts 128K INT4 KV retrieval from 41.5% to 99.1%.
  • Zero-Shot Export Needs: Fall back to QuaRot (S4) when no calibration dataset is permitted.

⛔ Not For (When to Avoid)

  • Legacy GPUs Without 4-Bit Compute at Batch=1: If hardware lacks native INT4/FP4 Tensor Cores and runs low-batch decode, AWQ W4A16 (S3) avoids the 1.42% online FWHT cost.
  • Unaligned Dynamic Multi-LoRA Serving: Never attach unrotated LoRA adapters onto an R1-rotated base checkpoint without offline R1^T / R1 projection.
  • Sub-1.5B Edge Models on Hard Math: Keep W8A8/FP8 activations on tiny parameter counts (though offline R1/R2 fusion is still free!).
