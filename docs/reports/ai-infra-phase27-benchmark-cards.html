7 / 7 · MY TAKE GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Architect's Verdict: When to Deploy Cake Fused AllReduce, Quantized HF Adapters & UMBP / Straw Recovery

架构师选型建议（中文）：明确界定三套技术在生产环境中的适用边界（Good Fit）与反模式禁区（Not For）。

✅ Good Fit (适用场景)

  • Blackwell Decode-Band Row-Parallel Linears ($M \le 128$): Use cake_gemm_allreduce on `SM100`/`SM103` with `world_size` $\in \{2, 4, 8\}$ and MNNVL workspace when you need 1.1x–1.3x lower latency and bitwise-identical outputs across TP ranks (preventing MoE router divergence).
  • 2-CTA (`cta_group::2`) & Persistent `tcgen05` Kernels: Always enforce `tcgen05.wait::ld.sync.aligned` before `tcgen05.dealloc` and a `ring_depth` `mbarrier_wait` producer tail before load-warp exit.
  • Hybrid KDA/MLA & FP8/MXFP4 HF Checkpoint Exports: Strip unsaved scale keys (`.weight_scale_inv`, `.weight_scale`) from `fqn_to_index_mapping`, redistribute sharded KDA `A_log`/`dt_bias` `DTensor`s to `Replicate()` before padding/flattening, and wrap `SimpleFSDP` placement scans in `disable_active_parametrization()`.
  • Disaggregated KV Pools & Async RL Post-Training: Use vLLM V1 `UMBPStoreConnector` (`DCP` partitioning `TP`, `max_external_token = num_tokens - 1`) and Slime's `committed_{step}.json` joint boundary with 4-tier prompt regeneration.

⛔ Not For (不适用场景 / 禁区)

  • Large-Batch Prefill or Chunked Prefill ($M \ge 256$): Do not route $M \ge 256$ to `cake_gemm_allreduce`; its `token_tile = 128` grid re-streams the weight shard $\lceil M / 128 \rceil$ times, making `mm_bf16 + allreduce_fusion` ~1.3x faster.
  • Non-Blackwell GPUs or Non-MNNVL Topologies: `cake_gemm_allreduce` requires `SM100` / `SM103` (`tcgen05` + TMA + NVLS multicast/unicast pointers) and does not run on Hopper (`SM90`) or PCIe-only rings.
  • Unfiltered `model.safetensors.index.json` Re-Exports: Never pass an unmodified FP8/MXFP4 index map to `HuggingFaceStorageWriter` when saving BF16 weights, or unwritten keys will emit `dtype: ""` headers that break `safetensors`.
  • Pipeline-Parallel vLLM UMBP (`pp_size > 1`) or Tracker-Only RL Resume: UMBP currently raises `NotImplementedError` for `pp_size > 1`; Slime RL recovery must never trust `latest_checkpointed_iteration.txt` without verifying `committed_{step}.json`.
