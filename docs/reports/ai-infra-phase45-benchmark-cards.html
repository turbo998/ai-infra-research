2 / 7 · SCENARIO: GLM-5.3 4-STREAM MHC & MINIMAX-M3 ROUTERDEEP DIVE

19-Step Sinkhorn-Knopp in 1,454.7 us (`15.95x`) & 24-Bit 3-Term BF16 Router (`0.0` Error)

Input Scenario / 场景输入

GLM-5.3-Flash training with `streams=4, hidden=4096` (`u: [65536, 16384]`) on GB300 (`sm_103a`) and MiniMax-M3 FP32 MoE routing under `torch.backends.cuda.matmul.allow_tf32 = True`.

Decision 1 · Cake 4-Stream mHC Checkpoint Mode (`choice`) · Act automatically

Saves `0 B/token` during forward and recomputes the `256 B/token` statistics via `forward_stats` in backward: `2,823.8 us` vs `41,773.9 us` (`14.79x` on GB300) and `1,992.6 us` vs `28,443.5 us` (`14.28x` on R200).

Decision 2 · MiniMax-M3 3-Term BF16-Split Router (`score`) · Act automatically

`split_router_weight_bf16(W, terms=3)` decomposes FP32 weights into 3 residual BF16 tensors `[E, 3H]`, achieving `0.0` (`< 1e-9`) logit drift vs `1.16e-4` TF32 drift in a single BF16 GEMM.

Decision 3 · Quark AITER ASM FP4 `N <= 128` Guard (`noul`) · Confirm

On MI355X (`gfx95`), `N <= 128` with `2 <= M <= 64` hits an untuned AITER heuristic producing wrong rows (`max_diff ~ 93`); automatically fall back to non-ASM GEMM.

Mathematical Invariant: Three residual BF16 terms provide $3 \times 8 = 24$ mantissa bits, matching IEEE-754 FP32 exactly while running on BF16 tensor cores.

7 / 7 · MY TAKEPRODUCTION GUIDANCE & READINESS

When to Adopt Phase 45 Kernel, Router & Distributed KV Optimizations

✅ Good fit (推荐使用场景)

  • GLM-5.3 4-Stream mHC Training: Use `cake_mhc_train(..., checkpoint=True)` on SM100/SM103/SM107 for `14.28x–14.79x` checkpoint speedup with `0 B/token` saved activation stats.
  • FP32 MoE Routers (MiniMax-M3): Use 3-term BF16 residual decomposition (`[E, 3H]`) to retain full 24-bit FP32 accuracy in a single BF16 tensor-core GEMM.
  • Kimi-K3 / DeepSeek-V3 Long-Context MLA CP: All-Gather compressed `(kv_c_normed_TL, k_shared_TR)` before `wkv_b` (`71.11x` smaller All-Gather).
  • 1000+ Slot KV Routing: Pair `AtomicU64` outbound cursors with 16-hex SHA-256 ZMQ topics and `--slots-per-socket=8`.

⛔ Not for (不适用或需门控场景)

  • Un-gated Multi-Wave KV Split: Keep `FLASHINFER_TRTLLM_GEN_MULTI_WAVE_KV_SPLIT=1` opt-in; only beneficial when 1 wave yields `<= 2` splits and `base_ctas <= 2 * SMs`.
  • ROCm AITER ASM FP4 at `N <= 128, 2 <= M <= 64`: Must fall back to non-ASM GEMM on MI355X until AITER heuristic table is patched.
  • Layer-by-Layer MoRIIO Read Waits: Avoid deferring RDMA read completion to `wait_for_layer_load` on ROCm; await at step start (`start_load_kv`).
