Production Adoption Guide: Where Phase 68 Kernel, Serving & Collective Upgrades Deliver Immediate ROI

Actionable architectural boundaries for Solution Architects deploying Blackwell/Rubin, Hopper, Thor, and Ascend AI infrastructure.

✅ Good Fit (Deploy Immediately)

  • SM107a Mega-MHC at Medium/Long Sequences: Enable mhc_infer_tail_program so splits ≤ 22 (T ≥ 385 on 208 SMs, T ≥ 257 on 148 SMs) uses tailpipe fold-overlap while keeping bf16 for short prompts.
  • Hopper/Blackwell PDL Norms: Always pair enable_pdl=True with cudaGridDependencySynchronize() before the first global load and stress-test in-place norms under cross-stream SM contention.
  • Checkpoint-Native Structured Serving: Use SGLang's response_template detector for Gemma-4 and delimited reasoning/tool models, and route CUDA BF16/FP16→FP32 logits copies through Triton cast_to_float32_logits.
  • Blackwell MoE Grouped MXFP8: Use TE's 128×128 wide-tile requantize kernel for 4-byte-aligned fast-math grouped tensors to get 512B coalesced scale writes and live_end early exit.
  • High-Frequency KV / Notification Paths: Use LMCache's transfer_with_address_kernel with pre-pinned host pools and NIXL's exportMoveStr() + InlinedVector UCX handles.

⛔ Not For (Avoid / Requires Guardrails)

  • Forcing MHC Tailpipe at 32/64 Splits: Do not force tailpipe at T ≤ 256 where high split counts make round-1 load overlap slower than the base bf16 tail kernel.
  • Unaligned CUDA MMA K-Tiles or Mixed Ascend FP8×FP4: TileLang CUDA MMA rejects chunk % k_dim != 0 without explicit zero-padded K tiles; Ascend DeepGEMM supports FP8×FP8 and FP4×FP4, not mixed FP8×FP4 dequant yet.
  • Unpinned Shared-Memory KV Buffers on Critical Loops: While LMCache safely stages/pins unpinned chunks and calls synchronize_device(), doing so on the hot path sacrifices async overlap.
  • Annotating Eager Reference Models in Dynamo Tests: Never apply GraphTrainer's annotate_fn({"module_fqn": fqn}) to eager baselines when compiling flex_attention, or FX_ANNOTATION guards will recompile once per layer.
