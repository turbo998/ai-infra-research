4 / 7 · UNDER THE HOOD

Affine maps form an ordered associative monoid,not a commutative sum

earlier:(a,b), later:(c,d)
combined map:x → c*(a*x+b)+d
pair:(c*a,c*b+d)
identity:(1,0)

zero reset before element:(0,b)
prefix(a,b) applied to h0:a*h0+b

↓ Original independent oracle

h[t] = h0*product(a[0:t+1])
 + sum_j b[j]*product(a[j+1:t+1])

Hillis-Steele reads previous round buffer
never mix updated and old prefixes
