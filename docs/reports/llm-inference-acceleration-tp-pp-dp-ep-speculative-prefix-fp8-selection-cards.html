2 / 7 · SCENARIO: 671B MOE VS. 405B DENSE SLICING

Why Dense Models Need `TP × PP` While Sparse MoE Needs `Attention DP × Expert EP`

Sharding `DeepSeek-671B` with `TP=16` forces every GPU to hold slices of all 256 experts and run 122 cross-node `All-Reduces` per token.

  • choice (Attention DP=64 × TP=1): Act automatically — Run independent batches per GPU in Attention with `0` KV duplication & `0` TP AllReduce.
  • score (Expert EP=64 + `DeepEP` + `EPLB`): Act automatically — Place 4 experts/GPU & route tokens via `IBGDA` `Dispatch/Combine` (`128.4K tok/s`).
  • choice (`MTP` / `EAGLE-3` Speculative Decoding): Act automatically — Verify 3–4 draft tokens in one pass (`11.6ms` TPOT).
For 2-node 405B Dense on 80GB H100, use Intra-Node `TP=8` × Inter-Node `PP=2`
