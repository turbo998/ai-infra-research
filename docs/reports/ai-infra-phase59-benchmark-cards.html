7 / 7 · MY TAKEGOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Production Adoption Guide for Phase 59 Kernel, Serving & KV Storage Upgrades

✅ Good Fit

• Blackwell Paged GQA Decode (`hd=128`, `page_size % 16 == 0`): Enable `cake_fmha` aligned `q1` kernels for up to `10.03x` (GB300) and `5.18x` (B200) lower latency.
• Multi-Stream SSM/Conv1d Training: Use two-launch non-cooperative `cake_causal_conv1d_train_backward` so overlapped NCCL/side streams never starve backward (`65x–108x` faster).
• Disaggregated PD + L3 HiCache & AWS EFA: Enforce prefetch-before-prealloc L3 promises in SGLang and enable `2 MiB` THP PTE budgeting in Mooncake (`44 TiB`/NIC).
• Row-Parallel TP Backward: Upgrade to cuBLASMp `>= 0.11.0` so TE `wgrad` reuses `dgrad`'s gathered `grad_output` (`0` extra AllGathers).

⛔ Not For

• Packed NVFP4 Attention Sinks on Hopper FA3 (`sm_90a`): FA3 does not support packed FP4 KV caches (`NotImplementedError`); route NVFP4 attention sinks through the FA2 backend with `maybe_k/v_cache_sf`.
• Unaligned Page Sizes (`page_size % 16 != 0`) or `group_size > 16`: `cake_fmha` aligned `hd128` `q1` kernels require `page_size >= 16` divisible by 16 and `1 <= group_size <= 16`.
• 8B-Aligned Odd Padded Rows in Half-Warp QKNorm: When `(head_dim + head_padding)` is an odd multiple of 8 elements, row 1 is only 8B-aligned; fall back to 1-row-per-warp `fused_qknorm_warp`.
