7 / 7 · MY TAKEGOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Production Adoption Guide for Phase 53 Kernel, Engine & KV-Fabric Upgrades

✅ Good Fit (立即采纳场景)

  • FP16 TP>1 Serving on NVLink/MNNVL: Mandatory upgrade to `flashinfer@1ff146212d` (#6818) to prevent `|x| > 255.94` all-zero token collapse in Qwen3.5-27B and similar models.
  • Blackwell (`sm_100a`/`sm_103a`/`sm_107a`) Comm & BSA: Use per-arch Cake NVFP4 all-reduce (`1.0718×` geomean) and single-launch Sage-FP8 BSA (`1.30–1.40×`).
  • B300 MoE Autotuning & TE GroupedMLP: Pass caller-owned `workspace_buffer=` (`-2,683.1 MiB`) and set `split_alignment=128` (MXFP8/NVFP4) or `256` (BF16).
  • NICs Without GPU-Initiated RDMA: Run `NIXL_EP_DEVICE_MODE=proxy` with `8 channels × 8 NUMA-pinned threads` (`100–109%` of direct bandwidth at `H=7168`).

⛔ Not For (不适用或需规避场景)

  • Unpinned or Oversubscribed NIXL Proxy Threads: Never set `NIXL_EP_PROXY_THREADS=16` on 4-GPU 32-core nodes (`45.5 → 1.2 GB/s` collapse) or omit NUMA CPU affinity (`99% → 45%` drop).
  • Packed `_qp` Quantization on `sm_107a`: Packed two-wide Pass-2 quantization (`_qp`) is faster on `sm_100a`/`sm_103a` but not on `sm_107a` (which uses `_sop` only).
  • Unsharded / Cached ComfyUI ControlNet Tensors: Never cache `comfyui_control` across denoising steps or pickle duplicate block references without `_memo[id(value)]`.
  • ROCm EAGLE3 Without Batch Invariance: Running sync vs async speculative decoding without `VLLM_BATCH_INVARIANT=1` flips near-tie greedy tokens.
