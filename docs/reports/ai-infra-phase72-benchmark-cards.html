choice · split-kv 1. SM100/SM103 tcgen05 Split Count & Grace Co-Resident Dispatch (#7205)
Act automatically

Because tcgen05 TMEM limits residency to 1 CTA/SM (vs 2 on SM80/SM90), B200 selects splits = [33, 33, 18, 9, 4, 2, 1, 1] for rows = 1..128. On Grace (aarch64) eager rows <= 4, decode and merge CTAs co-reside in one kernel launch (1.80x eager, 1.77x graph speedup at rows=1).

choice · stream-fork 2. TRT-LLM Multi-Stream CuteDSL MoE Async Input Quantization (#20808)
Act automatically

_should_quantize_input_async forks fp4_quantize onto the aux stream only when do_multi_stream() or is_current_stream_capturing(), overlapping top-k routing and moe_sort to save 25.0 us/layer (1.525 ms across 61 layers at batch=8 on GB300).

7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECTURAL FIT & READINESS

Where Phase 72 Optimizations & AI Infra Innovator Architectures Fit — and Where They Do Not

✅ Good Fit (Deploy Now)

  • 400B–816B Sparse MoE Serving on Blackwell/Rubin (Fireworks / Together style): Combining NVFP4 CuteDSL MegaMoE (21 GB active weights/step), DSpark/DFlash speculative decoding (AL=4.43), and vLLM batch-sharded adaptive verification maximizes throughput/dollar.
  • Grace-Blackwell Low-Batch Sparse Decode: Enable FlashInfer cake_qsa_nvfp4 on B200/GB300; leverage co-resident fused kernel for eager rows <= 4 on aarch64.
  • Multi-HCA Disaggregated KV Store & Long-Context Offload: Enable Mooncake MC_STORE_HCA_AFFINITY=1 + MC_STORE_HCA_NUMA_SEGMENTS=1 and LMCache packed bytearray token ledgers for 16K–64K+ prompts.
  • Large-Scale MoE RL Post-Training: Adopt TorchTitan's Ulysses CP=4 + EP=16 + FSDP(shard)=8 layout on GB300 to cut expert all-gather domains by 8x.

⛔ Not For (Avoid / Guard Against)

  • Pure On-Chip SRAM Engines (Groq LPU) for 800B+ Total-Parameter MoEs: At 230 MB SRAM/chip, holding 408 GB of NVFP4 weights requires >1,800 LPUs; use Groq LPU for low-jitter <=70B dense models and Cerebras WSE-3 when wafer-resident single-stream speed is paramount.
  • Unconditional Aux-Stream Forking in Eager Mode: Do not fork MoE input quantization onto an aux stream outside CUDA Graph / multi-stream mode (host sync overhead exceeds the 25 us kernel overlap).
  • Rubin sm_107a Builds Below CUDA 13.4: All 41 Rubin RMSNorm entries require min_cuda="13.4".
  • Dynamo --freeze-gc-after-init on Encode / Headless Workers: Only enable GC heap freezing on registering AGGREGATED, DECODE, or PREFILL workers.
