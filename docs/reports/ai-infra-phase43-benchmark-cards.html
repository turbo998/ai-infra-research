7 / 7 · MY TAKE GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & PRODUCTION READINESS

Production Adoption Guide for Phase 43 Kernel, Disaggregation & Multi-Tenant Store Upgrades

✅ Good Fit

  • Nemotron 4 on Blackwell (sm_100a / sm_103a): Single-token decode (splitv8_tile8) and speculative MTP verification up to T=16 (lanerow) with up to 4 Householder updates.
  • NVFP4 Paged KV Cache Serving: Enabling nvfp4_4over6 on outlier-heavy attention layers and deploying PR #6204 immediately to eliminate .cpu() prefill sync bubbles.
  • Prime-Length Speculative Decoding (next_n = 5, 7): DeepSeek-V3.2 / GLM-5 DSA MQA logits on sm_100f at moderate-to-high batch sizes (waves_atom1 ≥ 16).
  • Multi-Tenant Disaggregated KV Pools: Mooncake Store cross-TP/PP KV reuse with replica deduplication and Held/Carried upsert quota isolation.

⛔ Not For

  • Long Varlen GDP Prefills (T > 16) or GQA/GVA Head Broadcast in Decode: cake_gated_delta_product_mtp enforces 1 ≤ T ≤ 16 and H == HV; use chunk_gated_delta_product_varlen for prefill.
  • Single-Request Low-Wave Prime MTP (waves_atom1 < 16): Wide-tile in-kernel splitting adds tile padding overhead below 16 waves; keep the automatic atom=1 fallback.
  • Immediate Buffer Reuse on Logical KV Transfer Failure: Never recycle undelivered destination slots upon Outcome arrival until Attempt.quiesced() is True.
