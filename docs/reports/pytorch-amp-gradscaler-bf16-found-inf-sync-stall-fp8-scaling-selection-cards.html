Why `BF16` Needs Zero `GradScaler` & How `1x128` FP8 JIT Works

// 1. Disable GradScaler whenever dtype is bfloat16 (8-bit exponent!): scaler = torch.amp.GradScaler("cuda", enabled=(amp_dtype == torch.float16)) // When enabled=False, scaler.scale(loss) and scaler.step(opt) are pure no-ops!
↓ Online Blockwise `1x128` FP8 JIT Quantization (<answer>)
// 2. Compute exact local amax per 1x128 activation tile in SRAM: amax_tile = tl.max(tl.abs(x_tile_1x128), axis=1) scale_tile = amax_tile / 448.0 # Exact E4M3 fit, 0 delayed history lag! x_fp8 = (x_tile_1x128 / scale_tile[:, None]).to(tl.float8e4nv)
Zero `amax_history` buffers, zero cross-step lag, and zero `found_inf` skips.

7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Good Fit vs Not For & Production Readiness

✅ Good fit
• All `BF16` training pipelines (`GradScaler(enabled=False)`)
• Hopper / Blackwell `FP8` training with `1x128` / `128x128` blockwise JIT scaling
• MoE models (`DeepSeek-V3`, `Qwen3-MoE`) with high activation outlier kurtosis
⛔ Not for
• Leaving `GradScaler(enabled=True)` when `autocast(dtype=torch.bfloat16)` is used
• Relying on global per-tensor delayed `amax` scaling on outlier-heavy MoE layers
