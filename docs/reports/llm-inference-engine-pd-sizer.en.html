① 📌 Executive Summary: Core Selection Rules

1. Single-Node Colocated Serving (≤8 GPUs)

Choose vLLM v0.19.1 for Day-0 multi-model versatility & multimodal batch encoder CUDA graphs (3.20× encoder speedup, +11.9% long-output throughput with --async-scheduling); SGLang v0.5.10 for multi-turn Agents, shared long system prompts, and JSON schemas (186.4 ms → 54.0 ms TTFT, 3.45× faster at 75% prefix hit rate on 16K context, 2.85× QPS); TensorRT-LLM v1.3.0 for lowest single-request TPOT (8.6 ms, 1.35×) on fixed Hopper/Blackwell backbones.

2. Long-Context & PD Disaggregation (≥32 GPUs + 400G RDMA)

When prompts exceed 8K–16K tokens, colocated batching suffers severe Prefill-Decode interference (P99 TPOT spikes to 68.4 ms). Disaggregating Prefill & Decode via Dynamo + Mooncake (32K context 1.024 GB = 8.192 Gb KV payload transferred over 400G RoCEv2 at 44.91 GB/s = 359.30 Gbps effective goodput in 22.8 ms) cuts Decode P99 TPOT to 18.2 ms (3.76×), lifts SLA goodput to 1,618.4 tok/s/GPU (2.38×), and lowers token cost from $0.76 to $0.50/1M (-34.2% with heterogeneous 4P+4D).

3. Large MoE Quantization & Expert Parallelism (671B/37B · 64 GPUs)

Use W4A16 (AWQ/Marlin) for low-batch dense models; fine-grained block-scaled FP8 (1×128 / 128×128) on Hopper/MI300X; and hardware NVFP4 on Blackwell B200. For multi-node 671B MoE, replace cross-node pure TP=16 with Attention DP + MoE EP + DeepEP + EPLB: 64-GPU FP8+DP8/EP8 achieves 92,200 tok/s (2.65× vs. BF16 TP=16, 15.8 ms P99 TPOT, $0.35/1M, -62.0%), Blackwell NVFP4+DP16/EP16 reaches 151,200 tok/s (4.34×, $0.21/1M, -77.2%), and EPLB cuts Zipf hot-expert P99 TPOT from 25.1 ms to 15.8 ms (-37.1%).
