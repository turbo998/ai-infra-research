// 2. TileLang #3595 (1a52a1cd06): Unpack __nv_bfloat162 -> float2 without prmt.b32
TL_DEVICE float2 __tl_cvt_bf16x2_to_f32x2(__nv_bfloat162 src) {
  const unsigned int bits = reinterpret_cast<const unsigned int &>(src);
  float2 result;
  asm("shl.b32 %0, %2, 16;\n\t"
      "and.b32 %1, %2, 0xffff0000;"
      : "=f"(result.x), "=f"(result.y) : "r"(bits));
  return result;
}

7 / 7 · MY TAKE Good Fit vs Not For & Readiness

Architectural Guidance: Adopt EP16 Alias Fences, Shared Eval Graph Pools & 1 Hz Port Polling

Actionable synthesis for Cloud & AI Infrastructure Solution Architects (面向云与 AI Infra 架构师的落地指南).

✅ Good Fit · 推荐采用场景

  • Multi-Rank NVLink / Peer-Mapped Kernels: Always emit fence.proxy.alias before fence.acq_rel.sys when mixing multicast/peer VA writes with local VA reads.
  • Blackwell Long-Context MLA & Small-M FP4 GEMM: Enable 2-tile QK-ahead for unit_tiles >= 512 and ring-overlay 128x128 tiles when ceil(N/128) > num_sms.
  • Multi-Worker NIXL / LMCache Nodes: Share cuMemObj descriptors via reference-counted clients and scope P2P lookups strictly to (DRAM, LOCAL).
  • Large-Model Train+Eval Loops: Share the training CUDA Graph memory pool with validation after eager warmup to eliminate secondary pool bloat.

⛔ Not For · 不适用或需规避场景

  • Short-Context MLA Decode (unit_tiles < 512): Do not force decode_qk_ahead when prologue cost outweighs 2-tile lookahead overlap.
  • Unaligned 2-CTA Grouped FP8 Routes: Do not dispatch 2-CTA FP32-scale grouped GEMMs when expert row offsets violate 256-row alignment.
  • Blind RDMA Polling on Fatal HCA Errors: Never poll ibv_query_port to recover from IBV_EVENT_DEVICE_FATAL; restrict 1 Hz recovery strictly to IBV_EVENT_PORT_ERR.
  • Per-Chunk Async Task Racing: Avoid wrapping every streaming token chunk in asyncio.wait against a cancellation future.
