7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECT READINESS

Production Fit vs Anti-Patterns for Kernel Engineering, Pipeline Parallelism & Multi-LoRA

✅ Good fit

  • SM90 Ping-Pong + setmaxnreg (24/240): Essential for large-M GEMM and FlashAttention-3 where SFU exp2 or Epilogue quant can overlap with peer WGMMA.
  • SM100 TMEM + 2-CTA UMMA: Default choice on B200/GB200 to free general registers and cut L2 traffic by 50% via TMA multicast.
  • DualPipeV for Cross-Node MoE: Matches DualPipe's <0.6% exposed communication while cutting cluster parameter copies from 2x back to 1x.
  • S-LoRA Unified Paging + Punica SGMV: Ideal for 1,000+ tenant Serverless LoRA when rank r ≤ 64.

⛔ Not for

  • Ping-Pong on Skinny-M (M ≤ 128): Splitting 2 Consumer Warpgroups across separate tiles starves small grids; use Cooperative / Stream-K instead.
  • DualPipe on Memory-Starved Dense Models: DualPipe requires 2x weight copies (unless folded via DualPipeV) and PP+1 activation chunks; use ZB-V for dense training.
  • SGMV for High-Rank Heavy Adapters (r ≥ 96): Above r* = 64, SGMV gather/scatter overhead exceeds +32.6%; route to merged-weight dedicated instances.
