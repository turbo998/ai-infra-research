7 / 7 · MY TAKE ARCHITECTURAL FIT, LIMITATIONS & READINESS

Adopt Single-Entry TVM-FFI, 2-D BLNHC Batch Copy & Multipart S3-RDMA; Guard Thread/ROCm Limits

Actionable guidance for Cloud & AI Infrastructure Solution Architects deploying Blackwell and RDMA clusters.

✅ Good Fit (When to Adopt Immediately)

  • Eager / Partial-Graph Decode MoE on B200/B300: Single TVM-FFI entry (Pipeline.launch) eliminates 75–88% of CPU launch overhead at T=1..4.
  • SM107a Hidden-4096 mHC Pipelines: Use sf_layout="fp8_group128" (UE8M0) or "nvfp4" to eliminate standalone vLLM quantize kernels and save 8 KiB/token HBM reads.
  • Hybrid KDA / Gemma Models with CPU Offload: Generic untyped_storage().data_ptr() scanning safely pins aliased weights without per-layer hacks.
  • TP Vocabulary Training with Accuracy Metrics: parallel_cross_entropy(..., return_argmax=True) saves 125.25 MiB/rank HBM reads per step with exact NaN/tie semantics.
  • vLLM BLNHC KV Offloading & >4 GiB S3-RDMA: LMCache 2-D copy cuts descriptors by nl×; NIXL Multipart RDMA scales cleanly to 64 GiB arenas.

⛔ Not For (Boundaries & Caveats)

  • Whole-Graph CUDA Graph Replay Only: Frost TVM-FFI optimizes host submission; inside a captured CUDA graph replay, device kernel time (`10.33–26.65 µs`) is unchanged.
  • Ulysses Shapes with H * (D / 8) > 1024: ulyssesPackedQkvPostUnscatter asserts threads <= 1024; ultra-wide local head counts require blockIdx.z splitting.
  • Layer-Outermost or ROCm KV Caches: LMCache 2-D batch copy (`cudaMemcpy3DBatchAsync`) is disabled on ROCm (`USE_ROCM`) and returns pitch=0 on layer-outermost layouts.
  • >32 GiB VRAM Registration with Default cuFile: NIXL 64 GiB S3-over-RDMA requires raising max_device_pinned_mem_size_kb in cufile.json above its 32 GiB default.
