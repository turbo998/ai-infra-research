7 / 7 · MY TAKE Good Fit vs Not For & Readiness

Production Guidance for Solutions Architects across GPU Kernels, KV Transports & RL Training

Actionable architectural takeaways from Phase 98 (`priority: 107`).

✅ Good Fit · 推荐适用场景

  • Blackwell MoE & LM-Head: Enable pair+tail grouped FP8 SiLU-quant (`#7454`) with per-call operand rebinding (`#6049`) and single-sync valid-row loss compaction (`#7449`).
  • Disaggregated KV over RDMA/EFA: Sort paired `(local, remote)` block IDs before sub-block expansion (`#63142`), stamp WR chains once per `ibv_post_send` (`#4793`), and use keyed $O(1)$ pending notification checks (`#2436`).
  • Blackwell RL Training: Combine Megatron TE `mxfp8` training with compressed-tensors MXFP8 rollout on vLLM/SGLang (`#8127`).

⛔ Not For · 不适用或需规避场景

  • Shared TMEM Offsets across Warps: Never index per-warp TMEM softmax stats by a shared `head_row_base` when `tcgen05.ld/st` enforces 32-lane warp quarters (`#6895`).
  • Batch-Invariant WNA16 on CUDA Kernels: Keep `VLLM_BATCH_INVARIANT=1` pinned to Triton WNA16 (`#61038`) to prevent reduction-order drift across batch sizes.
  • Per-WR Clock Syscalls under Endpoint Lock: Avoid calling `clock_gettime` inside per-WR loops while holding `RdmaEndPoint::lock_` (`#4793`).
