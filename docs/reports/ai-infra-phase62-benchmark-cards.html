7 / 7 · MY TAKE GOOD FIT VS NOT FOR & READINESS

Production Adoption Guide for Phase 62 Kernel, Serving & Training Upgrades

✅ Good Fit

  • Blackwell TP MoE & Muse Spark Serving: Deploying `cake_tp_moe_finalize_rmsnorm` on B200 (`TP2/TP8`) and GB300 (`TP2/TP4`) (`1.24x–1.33x`) and `cake_muse_spark_norm_backend` (`1.23x–1.82x`).
  • Hybrid Mamba & UVA Offloaded MoE: Applying vLLM `#62841` for single-step shared-prefix Mamba concurrency and `#62054` to force sorted `moe_align_block_size` on UVA weights (`3.2x` less PCIe traffic).
  • FSDP + Muon & GRPO RL Training: Using Megatron mFSDP `grad_comm_dtype`, TorchTitan `DistMuon` BF16 redistribution (`2.25x` on PCIe), and `_DistMoeW13ShardedTensor` (`10x` fewer MXFP8 quantizations).
  • Large-Scale Disaggregated KV Fabrics: Using NIXL Libfabric v2 (`FI_SOURCE` for `>256` peers + 16-bit `xfer_id`) and Dynamo `TenantKey` isolation.

⛔ Not For

  • Unsupported Lamport TP Topologies (`sm_100a TP4`, `sm_103a TP8`): `cake_tp_moe_finalize_rmsnorm` explicitly rejects these combinations and non-`H=4096, topk=8` shapes.
  • Naive Block Assignment on Host-Resident UVA Weights: Skipping `moe_align_block_size` when `_vllm_is_uva_offloaded=True` causes redundant PCIe weight reads per `(token, expert)` pair.
  • Counting Partial Connector Boundary Blocks as Reused: Boundary blocks partially written by the forward pass must remain misses (`[divUp(begin, B), end / B)`).
  • Unscoped Multi-Tenant KV Block Hashes: Indexing `HicacheSharedKvCache` without `tenant_id` lets one tenant's eviction delete another tenant's active routing entry.
Readiness & Provenance: All 22 upstream commits are merged on their respective upstream `main` branches (`apache-2.0` / `bsd-3-clause`). Deterministic models and unit tests (`tests/test_phase62_experiments.py`) are sealed in `turbo998/ai-infra-research` on `main`.
