7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Good Fit vs Not For & Production Readiness

✅ Good fit
• Multi-node Slurm / Kubernetes GPU clusters where `$HOME` is on NFS/Lustre
• Any workload using Triton (`@triton.jit`), `torch.compile`, FlashInfer JIT, or DeepGEMM
• Eliminating `CUDA_ERROR_INVALID_IMAGE` and `EOFError` on job startup
⛔ Not for
• Leaving `TRITON_CACHE_DIR` or `TORCHINDUCTOR_CACHE_DIR` on shared network filesystems
• Allowing all 8 local GPU workers to compile uncached kernels simultaneously without a barrier
CSP self-contained · Zero external assets · Ready for main branch archive.
