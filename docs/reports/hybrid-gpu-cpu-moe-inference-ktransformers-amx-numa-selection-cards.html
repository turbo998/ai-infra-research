Why Hyper-Threading Hurts AMX

Two logical hyper-threads on the same physical core share a single `AMX TMUL` unit and thrash the 48KB L1 data cache. Pinning worker threads strictly to physical cores preserves full `692 GB/s` bandwidth.

Power-Law Hot-Expert Absorption

On coding and math workloads, the top `6.25%` (`16/256`) experts per layer absorb `24.5%` of token routing decisions—allowing leftover GPU VRAM to directly shave a quarter of CPU memory traffic.

Robustness Verdict: All four pillars (NUMA striping, CUDA Graph, AMX tiles, and GPU Hot-Expert Cache) compound multiplicatively to turn a 5.4 tok/s bottleneck into 26.8 tok/s.

Why AMX Scales Better from Batch 1 to 8

When multiple requests in `Batch=8` route to the same cold expert, `AMX TMUL` processes those tokens together inside its 2D `16×64` tile registers without re-reading the expert weights from DDR5!
