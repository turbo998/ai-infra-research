- **FlashInfer 持久化抢块 `cake_rmsnorm_train` 反向（`28b2c6d93f33`, PR `#7131`）与 `sm_107a` 2-Stream mHC（`14df046fc7ac`, PR `#7111`）**：

### 1. Track A: FlashInfer Blackwell (`sm_100a`/`sm_103a`) QSA NVFP4 Paged Decode, Persistent Chunk-Claiming RMSNorm Backward, `sm_107a` 2-Stream mHC, Frost `NVFP4xNVFP4` Low-Latency MoE, Cake DSA Sink Training & TileLang Compiler Guards (`flashinfer-sm100-qsa-nvfp4-rmsnorm-mhc-frost-dsa-tilelang`)
- **FlashInfer Blackwell (`sm_100a`/`sm_103a`) Cake QSA NVFP4 Paged Decode (`379c3f1c7be8`, PR `#7140`)**: Extends `cake_qsa_nvfp4` (`144 B/row` = `3.5556x` compression vs BF16 `512 B`) to B200 (`sm_100a`) and GB300 (`sm_103a`) with single-instruction `prmt.b32` nibble unpacking, `L2::evict_first` streaming loads, non-cooperative single-launch for `rows <= 8`, and `whole_tile` 2D split-K for `rows >= 16`. At `rows=128, tok=2051`, achieves **`1.826x` NHD (`131.69 -> 72.10 us`) / `1.813x` HND on GB300** and **`1.889x` NHD (`146.94 -> 77.79 us`) / `1.878x` HND on B200** (**`1.648x` GB300 / `1.685x` B200 geomean**).
- **Persistent Chunk-Claiming `cake_rmsnorm_train` Backward (`28b2c6d93f33`, PR `#7131`) & `sm_107a` 2-Stream mHC (`14df046fc7ac`, PR `#7111`)**: Replaces cooperative launch with atomic `work_counter` chunk claiming + PDL (`griddepcontrol.wait`) `dweight` reduction (**`1.168x` on GB300 `16.0 -> 13.7 us`, `1.182x` on B200 `18.8 -> 15.9 us`**, bounding side-stream contention to **`<= 1.13x` vs `10x–280x`**). Dedicated `sm_107a` 2-stream ($n=2$) mHC shrinks saved state from `192 B` to **`56 B/token` (`3.4286x`)** and speeds up R200 `T=4096, C=4096` forward by **`2.511x` (`46.2 -> 18.4 us`)** and backward by **`2.712x` (`84.6 -> 31.2 us`)**.
- **Frost `NVFP4xNVFP4` + 10 Activations (`59c07ac25a67`, PR `#7053`), Cake DSA 64-Head Sink Training (`2202dd75c182`, PR `#7059`) & TileLang Guards (`baceed6676df`, `2573d470798a`, `cb26ee0fafe5`)**: Frost delivers **`1.525x` (`15.57 vs 23.75 us`)** on `MXFP4xMXFP8 SiTU` and **`1.234x` (`17.55 vs 21.66 us`)** on `NVFP4xNVFP4 SiTU` at B300 `T=1`; Cake DSA widens 12 `bwd_pre` kernels from `32 -> 128` threads (**`1.0504x` on GB300 `glm53_b`**); TileLang aligns Ascend FP4 MN to `64`, preserves signed `int64` in `cuda::atomic_ref<T1>`, and rejects `step != 1` loop vectorization.

### 2. Track B: TensorRT-LLM DeepSeek-V4 Stack, Rubin `SM107` MXFP8 CuteDSL Grouped GEMM, vLLM MLA Speculative Multi-Token Expansion, SGLang HiCache 3D Zero-Copy & AI Infra Innovator Economics (`trtllm-dsv4-rubin-mxfp8-vllm-mla-spec-sglang-hicache`)
- **TensorRT-LLM DeepSeek-V4 Stack (`0931dfedc9a2`) & Rubin `SM107` MXFP8 CuteDSL Grouped GEMM (`4942e2a02145`)**: Packs `(global_rows << 32) | local_rows` (`61 -> 0` host syncs/step) and pairs with `KVCacheManagerV2` head/tail mixed NVFP4 KV (**`-70.88%` KV memory at `S=32,768`, `3.4343x` compression**). Rubin (`SM107`) CuteDSL MXFP8 Grouped GEMM with 2-CTA multicast TMA and `exp2f_rcp` achieves **`1.281x` speedup (`310.4 -> 242.3 us`)** on R200 (`T=4096, E=32`).
- **vLLM MLA Speculative Multi-Query Expansion (`97131637e9`) & SGLang HiCache 3D Transfer + Rust Abort (`c677171c1d`, `5a86d6ee17`)**: Folding $1+K=4$ speculative draft tokens into a single matrix-absorbed MLA query group cuts KV HBM reads by **`4.0x` (`603.98 -> 150.99 MB/layer` at `B=32, S=8192`)** and restores acceptance to **`81.33%` (`+26.51` pts)**; SGLang `cudaMemcpy3DBatchAsync` shrinks 61-layer descriptors **`1,952 -> 32` (`61.0x`)**, `KVShard` halves DCP=2 L3 traffic (**`-50%`**), and `push_abort` cuts orphan KV retention **`58.57x` (`82.0 -> 1.4 ms`)**.
- **AI Infra Innovators Benchmark (`Fireworks` / `Together` / `Groq` / `Cerebras`)**: Quantifies the Pareto boundary across GB300 NVL72 (`$0.14/1M` tokens, `11.2 ms` TPOT), VR200 NVL144, Fireworks FireAttention NVFP4 (`$0.18/1M`), Together Sequoia EAGLE-3 (`3.18` accepted tokens, `$0.19/1M`), Groq LPU V2 (`80 TB/s` SRAM, `1.15 ms` TPOT, `64`-chip tensor slice for 70B, `$0.58/1M`), and Cerebras WSE-3 (`44 GB` wafer SRAM, `2,150 tok/s/user`, `$0.62/1M`).
