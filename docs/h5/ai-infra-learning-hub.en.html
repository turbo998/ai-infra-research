1. Prefill-Decode (`PD`) Disaggregation Mechanics & Decision Boundary

Prefill is compute-bound while Decode is memory-bandwidth-bound. Keep Colocated + Chunked Prefill for `<=8 GPUs`, `<2K` prompts, or clusters without RDMA; disaggregate into P/D pools over `400G/800G GPUDirect RDMA` when `>=16 GPUs`, prompts `>=4K`, `I:O >=8:1`, and `P99 TPOT <=25ms`.
